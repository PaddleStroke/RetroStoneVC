Box Pusher
# The aim
You are the keeper of a storehouse. Push every crate onto one of the brass rings set in the floor: there are as many rings as crates.
# A move
The keeper walks one cell at a time: up, down, left or right, never through walls or crates. Walking into a crate pushes it one cell further, if the cell beyond is free. Crates are never pulled, and two crates in a row are too heavy to push. A crate pushed into a corner can never come out.
# The end of the game
The level is solved when every crate stands on a ring.
# Levels and stars
Solved at par, the fewest pushes that solve it: 3 stars; with up to half as many pushes again: 2 stars; otherwise 1.
