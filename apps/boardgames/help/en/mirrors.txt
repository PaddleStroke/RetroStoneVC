Mirrors
# The aim
A brass lamp sends a beam of light across the board. Turn and place the mirrors so that the beam lights every gem without touching a bomb.
- A mirror turns the beam by a right angle. A mirror on a walnut pivot stays in its cell: you only turn it.
- The loose mirrors wait in the tray: stand them on empty cells.
- The beam goes straight on through gems and empty cells. A wooden block stops it, and so does the edge of the board.
- A glass plate lets half of the light through and turns the other half.
Every puzzle has exactly one solution, found by reasoning alone.
# A move
Tap a mirror on a pivot to turn it. Tap an empty cell to stand a loose mirror there, again to turn it, a third time to send it back to the tray; or drag it.
# The end of the game
When every gem is lit and no bomb is touched, the puzzle is solved.
# Levels and stars
Each puzzle has a par: the fewest actions that solve it. Solved at par: 3 stars; with up to half as many moves again: 2 stars; otherwise 1. A hint keeps the puzzle to 2 stars, and more than two hints to 1.
