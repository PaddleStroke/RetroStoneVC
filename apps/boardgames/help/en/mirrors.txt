Mirrors
# The aim
A brass lamp sends a beam of light across the board. Turn and place the mirrors so that the beam lights every gem without touching a bomb. Choose the level first (the stones under each picture): easy, medium, hard or expert.
- A mirror turns the beam by a right angle. The mirrors on a walnut pivot stay in their cells: you only turn them one way (/) or the other (\).
- The loose mirrors wait in the tray at the edge of the board: stand them on empty cells.
- The beam goes straight on through gems and empty cells. A wooden block stops it, and so does the edge of the board.
- From the hard level on, a glass plate lets half of the light through and turns the other half.
Every puzzle has exactly one solution, found by following the beam and reasoning.
# A move
Turn a mirror on its pivot, or stand a loose mirror on an empty cell, turn it, move it or take it back.
# The end of the game
When every gem is lit and no bomb is touched, the puzzle is solved: a gold light runs along the beam from the lamp and the gems flash.
# Levels and stars
There are 150 levels in six chapters, from 5 × 5 boards with pivoting mirrors only up to 8 × 8 boards with loose mirrors, bombs and glass, each with one solution; choose one on the level screen. The die tab after the chapters makes a new puzzle at random, with the choice described above. Each puzzle has a par: the fewest actions that solve it (a loose mirror stood the other way takes one more tap). Solved at par: 3 stars; with up to half as many moves again: 2 stars; otherwise 1. A hint keeps the puzzle to 2 stars, and more than two hints to 1. Undo takes a move back, and it no longer counts.
# Playing on the device
- Tap a mirror on a pivot to turn it.
- Tap an empty cell to stand a loose mirror there; tap it again to turn it, a third time to send it back to the tray. You can also drag a mirror from the tray to a cell, from one cell to another, or off the board.
The beam follows every change at once. A lit gem glows; a bomb touched by the beam glows red. The lamp is a hint: it sets one mirror right and lights it in gold. Undo takes back one action at a time, all the way to the start. The bar shows your moves.
