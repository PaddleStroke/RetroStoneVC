Mancala
# The aim
Capture more seeds than the other player. Your store is at the right end of your row. Light plays first.
# Oware
- Pick up all the seeds of one of your pits and sow them one by one into the following pits, counter-clockwise. With twelve seeds or more, the pit you emptied is skipped.
- If your last seed lands in the other player's row and makes two or three seeds there, you capture them, and also the pits just before it in that row, as long as each holds two or three.
- A move that would capture every seed the other player has captures nothing.
- If the other player has no seeds, you must give them some, if you can.
- The game ends when a player has more than 24 seeds, when the player to move cannot play, or when a position comes back. In the last two cases, each player adds the seeds left in their own row. 24 each is a draw.
# Kalah
- Sow counter-clockwise into the following pits and into your own store, but not the other player's store.
- If your last seed lands in your store, you play again.
- If your last seed lands in an empty pit of yours and the pit opposite holds seeds, your seed and those opposite go to your store.
- When one row is empty, the other player puts the seeds left in their row into their store. The most seeds wins; the same number is a draw.
