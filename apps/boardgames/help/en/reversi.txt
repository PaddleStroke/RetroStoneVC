Reversi
# The aim
Have more discs of your colour on the board than the other player when the game ends. Dark moves first.
# A move
- Place a disc of your colour so that it traps one or more lines of the other player's discs between itself and another of your discs: across, up and down, or diagonally.
- Every trapped disc is turned over to your colour.
- A move must turn at least one disc. If you have no such move, you pass.
# The end of the game
The game ends when neither player can move. The player with more discs wins. The same number of discs is a draw.
