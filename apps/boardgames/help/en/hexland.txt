Hex Landscape
# The aim
Build a landscape from a stack of hexagonal tiles. Each edge of a tile shows meadow, forest, field, village, river or rail. Each level sets its objectives at the start.
# Laying a tile
Lay the top tile of the stack next to the landscape, turned as you like.
- Each edge that meets the same terrain scores 10 points.
- A tile whose six edges all meet matching neighbours is perfect: 30 more points and one more tile on the stack.
- Rivers and rails must go on: their edges may only meet a river or a rail of the same kind.
- A tile that fits nowhere is set aside.
# Regions and quests
A region is the tiles joined by edges of one terrain. Exactly asks for a region closed at that size, with no edge of it left open. Flags on tiles are bonus quests: done, they score 50 points and add three tiles.
# The end of the game
A level ends when all its objectives are met, or when the stack is empty. Without the first objective, try again.
# Levels and stars
All objectives: 3 stars; the first and half of them: 2 stars; the first alone: 1. Random play: the score by the flag gives 3 stars.
