Hex Landscape
# The aim
Build a landscape from a stack of hexagonal tiles and score as many points as you can. Each edge of a tile shows meadow, forest, field, village, river or rail.
# Laying a tile
Lay the top tile of the stack next to the landscape, turned as you like.
- Each edge that meets the same terrain scores 10 points.
- A tile whose six edges all meet matching neighbours is perfect: 30 more points and one more tile on the stack.
- Rivers and rails must go on: their edges may only meet a river or a rail of the same kind.
- A tile that fits nowhere is set aside.
# Quests
A flag on a tile asks for a region of its terrain, the tiles joined by it: exactly the number shown, or at least that many when a plus follows it. A quest done scores 50 points and adds three tiles to the stack. An exact region that grows too big, or is closed too small, fails.
# The end of the game
The game ends when the stack is empty.
# Levels and stars
The score by the flag: 3 stars; four fifths of it: 2 stars; otherwise 1.
