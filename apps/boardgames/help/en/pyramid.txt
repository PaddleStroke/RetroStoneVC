Pyramid
# The aim
Clear the pyramid of 28 cards by taking off pairs of cards that add up to 13.
# Moving cards
- A card is free when no card lies over it any more.
- Take off two free cards that add up to 13: an ace counts 1, a jack 11, a queen 12.
- A king counts 13: it goes off alone.
- The turned card and the top card of the waste are free too: pair them with a card of the pyramid, or with each other.
# The stock
- Tap the stock to turn its top card face up; the card turned before goes onto the waste.
- With one pass, the stock is gone through only once. With three passes, tap the empty stock to turn the waste back over.
# The end of the game
You win as soon as the pyramid is cleared, even with cards left in the stock or the waste. The game is lost when no move is left.
# Levels and stars
No hint and no undo: 3 stars.
