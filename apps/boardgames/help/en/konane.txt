Konane
# The aim
Be the last player able to move. Every move is a jump that takes a stone. Dark plays first.
# The start
Dark removes one of their own stones, from the four middle squares or from a corner. Light then removes one of theirs next to that hole.
# Capturing
Jump one of your stones over an enemy stone right next to it (up, down, left or right) onto the empty square just beyond, and take it. The same stone may jump again in the same straight line, but never turn. You may stop after any jump.
# The end of the game
The first player who cannot move on their turn loses. There are no draws.
