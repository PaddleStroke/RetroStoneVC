The Marienbad Game
# The aim
Matches lie in rows on the table. Two players take turns taking matches away; in the classic game, whoever has to take the last match loses.
# The layouts
- Marienbad: four rows of 1, 3, 5 and 7 matches.
- Random: 3 to 5 rows of 1 to 8 matches each, a new layout every game, the shortest row at the top.
# A turn
Take one match or more, as many as you like, but all from the same row. You must take at least one. Light plays first.
# The end of the game
- Misère (the classic rule): the player who takes the last match loses.
- Normal play: the player who takes the last match wins.
The game list offers four choices: Marienbad or a random layout, each in misère or normal play. In their pictures, the last match glows red when taking it loses, and gold when taking it wins; a die marks the random layouts.
# Playing on the device
Tap the matches you want to take: they lift. Tap a lifted match to put it back, or slide your finger along a row to lift several at once. All the lifted matches must be in one row. Then tap the round button with the check mark under the rows: the matches slide off to your side. Each bar shows how many matches that player has taken. Undo takes back the last turn.
