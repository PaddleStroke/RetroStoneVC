# The aim
Get rid of your chips: the French café game with three dice and eleven chips, for two to six players.
# The throws
From the best to the worst, and what each is worth in chips:
- 4-2-1: 10.
- 1-1-1: 7.
- Two 1s and another die (a "fiche"), and three of a kind, worth the face: 1-1-6 and 6-6-6: 6, down to 1-1-2 and 2-2-2: 2. A fiche beats the three of a kind of the same worth.
- The runs 6-5-4 down to 3-2-1: 2.
- Any other throw: 1, the higher dice the better.
- The nénette, 2-2-1: the worst of all.
Between equal throws, the one made in fewer throws wins, then the one made first.
# The charge
The chips start in the pot. Each round, everyone throws once; the worst throw takes from the pot as many chips as the best throw is worth. The loser starts the next round. When the pot is empty, the discharge begins: a player with no chips is out and safe.
# The discharge
Each round, throw up to three times, keeping any dice between throws. The others may throw no more times than the round's first player did. The best throw gives the worst as many of their own chips as it is worth. A player who gives away their last chip is out and safe.
# The end of the game
The game ends when one player holds all the chips: that player has lost, and all the others share the win.
