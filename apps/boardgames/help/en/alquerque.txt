Alquerque
# The aim
Take all the other player's pieces, or leave them with no move. Light plays first.
# Moving
Move one of your pieces along a line to the next point, if it is empty: forwards, straight or slanting, or sideways, never backwards.
# Capturing
- Jump over an enemy piece next to yours along a line onto the empty point just beyond, and take it. Pieces capture in any direction, backwards too.
- After a capture, the same piece goes on jumping while it can take another piece.
- Capturing is compulsory, and a chain of captures must go on to its end. You may choose any capture, not only the longest.
# The end of the game
- A player who cannot move, or has no pieces left, loses.
- The game is a draw when the same position comes back for the third time, or after fifty moves each without a capture.
