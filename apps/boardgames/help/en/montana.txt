Montana
# The aim
Arrange the cards in four rows, each one of a single suit from the 2 to the king, by moving cards into the gaps. The aces are taken out, which leaves four gaps.
# Moving cards
- A gap is filled with the card of the same suit and one rank higher than the card to its left: after the 5 of clubs, the 6 of clubs.
- A gap in the first column takes any 2.
- Nothing can fill a gap after a king.
# The redeals
Three times in a game, tap the hand (the arrow going round): the cards not yet in order are shuffled and dealt again after each row's run, leaving one gap right after it.
# The end of the game
You win when the four rows are in order. The game is over when no gap can be filled and no redeal is left. Not every deal can be won.
