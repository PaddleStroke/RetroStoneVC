Klotski
# The aim
Wooden blocks fill a tray of 4 × 5 cells, all but two. Bring the great red block down to the opening in the middle of the bottom edge, so it can slide out.
# A move
A block slides into empty cells, one cell at a time, up, down, left or right; it is never lifted. One move is one block slid as far as you like, even round a corner, before you touch another block.
# The end of the game
When the great block stands in the middle of the bottom row, it slides out through the opening: solved.
# Levels and stars
Solved at par, the fewest moves that solve it: 3 stars; with up to half as many moves again: 2 stars; otherwise 1.
