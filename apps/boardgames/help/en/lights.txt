Lights
# The aim
Some brass lamps in the board are lit. Turn every lamp off. Every board can be turned off.
# A move
Press a lamp: it turns over (lit to dark, or dark to lit), and so do its neighbours above, below, left and right. The order of your presses does not matter, and pressing a lamp twice is the same as never pressing it.
# The end of the game
The puzzle is solved when every lamp is dark.
# Levels and stars
Solved at par, the fewest presses that solve it: 3 stars; with up to half as many presses again: 2 stars; otherwise 1.
