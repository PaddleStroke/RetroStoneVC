Lights
# The aim
Some brass lamps in the board are lit. Turn every lamp off. Choose the board first: 3 × 3, 5 × 5 or 7 × 7; the larger the board, the more presses it takes.
Every board is made by pressing lamps of a dark board at random, so it can always be turned off.
# A move
Press a lamp: it turns over (lit to dark, or dark to lit), and so do its neighbours above, below, left and right. The order of your presses does not matter, and pressing a lamp twice is the same as never pressing it.
# The end of the game
The puzzle is solved when every lamp is dark: a wave of gold light runs over the board. The fewest presses possible are worked out for every board; match them if you can.
# Levels and stars
There are 150 levels in six chapters, from 3 × 3 lamps up to 7 × 7, many of them laid out in patterns; choose one on the level screen. The die tab after the chapters makes a new puzzle at random, with the choice described above. Each puzzle has a par: the fewest presses that solve it. Solved at par: 3 stars; with up to half as many presses again: 2 stars; otherwise 1. Undo takes a press back, and it no longer counts.
# Playing on the device
Tap a lamp to press it. The lamp beside the board gives a hint: it presses one lamp of the shortest way, in gold light. Undo takes back one press at a time, all the way to the start. The bar shows your presses and, at its right end, the fewest possible from the start; it glows gold when you match it.
