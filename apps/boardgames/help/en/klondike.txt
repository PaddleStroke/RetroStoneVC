Solitaire
# The aim
Move all 52 cards to the four foundations, one for each suit, built up from the ace to the king.
# The layout
- Seven columns of 1 to 7 cards; only the top card of each column is face up.
- The other 24 cards are the stock.
# Moving cards
- On a column, put a card one rank lower and of the other colour: a red 6 on a black 7.
- Face-up cards that follow each other move together.
- Only a king, or a run starting with a king, may fill an empty column.
- A face-down card left on top of a column turns face up.
- A card on a foundation may come back down onto a column.
# The stock
- Draw one: tap the stock to turn one card onto the waste. Draw three: three at a time; only the top one can be played.
- Turn the waste back over as many times as you like.
# The end of the game
You win when all the cards are on the foundations.
# Levels and stars
3 stars: no hint and no undo.
