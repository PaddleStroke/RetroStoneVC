Solitaire
# The aim
Move all 52 cards to the four foundations, one for each suit, built up from the ace to the king. A game for one player, also called Klondike.
# The layout
- Seven columns of 1 to 7 cards; only the top card of each column is face up.
- The other 24 cards are the stock, face down, at the top left; next to it, the waste.
- The four foundations are at the top right, empty at the start.
# Moving cards
- On a column, put a card one rank lower and of the other colour, red on black or black on red: a red 6 on a black 7.
- Face-up cards that follow each other move together.
- Only a king, or a run starting with a king, may fill an empty column.
- When a face-down card is left on top of a column, it turns face up.
- A card on a foundation may come back down onto a column.
# The stock
- Draw one: tap the stock to turn one card onto the waste. Draw three: three cards at a time, and only the top one can be played.
- When the stock is empty, tap its place to turn the waste back over, as many times as you like.
# The end of the game
You win when all the cards are on the foundations. Once the stock and the waste are empty and every card is face up, the cards go up by themselves. If you are stuck, start a new deal.
# Playing on the device
Tap a card to send it to its best place: a foundation if it can go there, otherwise a column. A quick second tap sends a card only to a foundation. You can also drag a card or a run and drop it where you want. The lamp in your bar shows a useful move; the number next to your cards counts your moves. Undo takes back moves, all the way to the deal.
