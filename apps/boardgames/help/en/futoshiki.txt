Futoshiki
# The aim
Fill the square grid so that every row and every column holds each number from 1 to the grid's size exactly once. The signs between neighbouring cells must hold: each sign points at the smaller number (2 < 5, and the same up and down). Some numbers may be given at the start. Every puzzle has exactly one solution, found by reasoning alone.
# A move
Put a number in an empty cell. You can change it or take it out at any time; the given numbers stay.
# The end of the game
The puzzle is solved when the grid is full and right.
# Levels and stars
No hint and no mistake: 3 stars; up to two hints and mistakes together: 2 stars; otherwise 1. A mistake is a wrong number that you take back with undo.
