# The aim
Capture the other player's pieces until they have only two left. Light places and moves first.
# Placing
The players take turns placing one piece in any empty hole, until all the pieces are on the board. You may never place a piece so that three or more of yours stand in a row, across or up and down. A player with no such hole left passes; when neither can place, the pieces still in hand stay out of the game.
# Moving
Then move one of your pieces one hole across or up and down to an empty hole. If the move makes a row of exactly three of your pieces, take any one enemy piece. A row of four does not count, and two rows made at once still take only one piece.
# The end of the game
- A player left with two pieces, or who cannot move on their turn, loses.
- After 100 moves in a row (both players' moves counted) without a capture, the game is a draw.
