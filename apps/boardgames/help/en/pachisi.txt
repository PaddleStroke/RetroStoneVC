Pachisi
# The aim
You command two armies of four pieces, on opposite arms of the cross. Bring all eight pieces round the board and home to the centre, the charkoni, first.
# The board
A piece goes down the middle of its own arm, round the whole board anticlockwise along the outer columns, then back up its arm into the charkoni. The twelve squares marked with a cross are castles.
# Throwing the cowries
Count the mouths turned up: none is 25, one is 10, two to six count as that number. A 6, 10 or 25 is a grace: throw again.
# Moving
- Move one piece of either army by the throw, or decline to move.
- Each army's first piece may leave the charkoni with any throw; every other piece, and one sent back, needs a grace.
- Your pieces may share a square, and nothing blocks the way.
- If no piece can move, the throw is lost.
# Meeting other pieces
- Landing on the other player's pieces sends them all back to the charkoni, and you throw again.
- No one may land on enemy pieces on a castle.
# Going home
A piece enters the charkoni with the exact number only. No throw is a 1, so never stop on the last square before it.
# The end of the game
The first player to bring all eight pieces home wins.
# Four players
Each plays one army, and partners sit opposite. Once your pieces are home, your throws move your partner's. Partners win together.
