Loop
# The aim
Draw one closed loop along the sides of the cells, from dot to dot. The loop never crosses or touches itself and has no loose ends. A number in a cell says how many of the cell's four sides the loop uses; a cell without a number may have any.
Every puzzle has exactly one solution, found by reasoning alone. Tap a side twice to cross it: a note that the loop does not pass there.
# The end of the game
The puzzle is solved when the loop is closed and keeps every rule.
# Levels and stars
No hint and no mistake: 3 stars; up to two hints and mistakes together: 2 stars; otherwise 1. A mistake is a line where the loop does not run, or a cross where it does, which you take back with undo.
