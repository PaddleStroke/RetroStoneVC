Loop
# The aim
Draw one closed loop along the sides of the cells, from dot to dot. The loop never crosses or touches itself and has no loose ends. A number in a cell says how many of the cell's four sides the loop uses; a cell without a number may have any. Every puzzle has exactly one solution, found by reasoning alone. Choose the size and the level first (the stones under each picture):
- Easy: a number's sides, a dot with two lines or none, and never closing a small loop while other lines are left, are always enough.
- Medium: you will also need to look at a number together with the dots at its corners (a 3 in a corner, a 1 against the edge).
- Hard: longer reasoning: try a side and follow it until something breaks.
# Playing on the device
Tap a side to draw a line; tap it again for a cross (a side you are sure stays empty), and once more to clear it. Or drag along the dots to draw several lines at once; a drag that begins along a line takes lines away instead. A number whose lines are all drawn fades (a 0 once it is crossed all round); one with too many turns red. The lamp at the end of the bar gives a hint: it puts a wrong mark right, or else marks a side that reasoning finds, in gold light. Undo takes back one action at a time (a whole drag at once), all the way to the start. The bar shows your moves. When the loop is complete, the wire turns gold.
