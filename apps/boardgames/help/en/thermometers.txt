Thermometers
# The aim
Fill the thermometers so that each row and each column holds as many filled cells as the number beside it.
- A thermometer fills from its bulb up, with no gap: as far as any of its cells, or not at all.
- Each thermometer fills on its own.
Every puzzle has exactly one solution, found by reasoning alone. Tap a cell to fill up to it; hold a cell to cross it out: a note that it stays empty.
# The end of the game
The puzzle is solved when every number is right: a wave of gold light runs over the grid.
# Levels and stars
No hint and no mistake: 3 stars; up to two hints and mistakes together: 2 stars; otherwise 1. A mistake is a wrong cell that you take back with undo.
