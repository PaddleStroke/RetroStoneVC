Tic-Tac-Toe
# Classic
Light plays crosses and goes first. Three of your marks in a row wins: across, up and down, or diagonally. All nine squares filled with no row of three is a draw.
# Ultimate
Nine small boards make up the big board.
- The square your opponent just played, within its small board, sends you to the small board in the same place on the big board. The first move may be anywhere.
- If that board is already won or full, you may play in any open small board.
- Three in a row wins a small board. Three small boards in a row win the game.
- When nobody can win a row of three small boards any more, the game is a draw.
