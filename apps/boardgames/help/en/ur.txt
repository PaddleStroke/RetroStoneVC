Royal Game of Ur
# The aim
Race your seven pieces along your path and off the board before the other player. Ivory throws first.
# The board
Your path runs along your own four squares, then the whole shared middle row, then your own last two squares, and off the board.
# Throwing and moving
- Throw the four dice and count the marked tips pointing up: 0 to 4.
- Move one piece that many squares; a throw of 3 puts a new piece on your third square.
- You cannot land on your own piece.
- A 0, or a throw no piece can use, is lost.
# Rosettes
Landing on a rosette gives another throw. The middle rosette is also safe: nobody may land on a piece there.
# Capturing
Landing on an enemy piece in the shared row sends it back off the board.
# Bearing off
A piece leaves the board with the exact number only: a 1 from your last square, a 2 from the one before, and so on.
# The end of the game
The first player to bear off all seven pieces wins.
