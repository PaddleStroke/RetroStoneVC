Snakes and Ladders
# The aim
Be the first to bring your pawn to square 100. For 2 to 4 players.
# A turn
Throw the die and move your pawn forward by the number. Pawns may share a square.
- Ending at the foot of a ladder, climb to its top.
- Ending on a snake's head, slide down to its tail.
- A 6 gives another throw.
# The end of the game
Square 100 needs the exact number: with more, go up to 100 and back by the extra squares. The first player on square 100 wins.
