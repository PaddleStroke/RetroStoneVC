Mystery Face
# The aim
Both players have the same 24 faces. Each secretly picks one of them. Ask questions that are answered yes or no, and find the other player's face before they find yours. For 2 players.
# The start
Ivory picks first, then Ebony: each in turn takes the device, taps a face, then taps the tick. Your face stays small in the corner of your screen. Then Ivory asks the first question.
# A turn
- Ask one question: tap a picture. A hat, glasses, a beard, a moustache, earrings, bald, long hair, curly hair; a hair colour (black, brown, blond, red, white or grey); an eye colour (brown, blue, green).
- The device answers for the other player: a green tick for yes, a red cross for no.
- Then put down the faces the answer rules out, by tapping them. That is your job: the device only answers. Tap a face again to stand it back up.
- Tap the pass button (a device between two arrows) to end your turn.
# Guessing
Instead of asking a question, you may guess: tap the magnifying glass, then a face still standing. If it is the other player's face, you win; if not, you lose at once.

# Playing on the device
The device passes after each turn: a cover hides the screen until the next player taps start. Only you see your board and your face. Each player's bar shows how many faces still stand on their board. Undo takes back the faces you put down or stood up in this turn; a question asked stays asked.
