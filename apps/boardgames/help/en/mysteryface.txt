Mystery Face
# The aim
Both players have the same 24 faces and each secretly picks one. Ask yes-or-no questions and find the other player's face before they find yours. For 2 players.
# The start
Ivory picks first, then Ebony: tap a face, then the tick. Then Ivory asks the first question.
# A turn
- Ask one question by tapping a picture: a hat, glasses, a beard, earrings, a hair or eye colour...
- The device answers for the other player: a green tick for yes, a red cross for no.
- Put down the faces the answer rules out by tapping them; the device only answers. Then tap the pass button.
# Guessing
Instead of a question, tap the magnifying glass, then a face still standing. If it is the other player's face, you win; if not, you lose at once.
