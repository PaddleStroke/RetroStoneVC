Gomoku
# The aim
Be the first to line up five stones of your colour in an unbroken row: across, up and down, or diagonally. Black plays first. If the board fills up with no winning row, the game is a draw.
# Freestyle and standard
- Freestyle: five or more stones in a row win.
- Standard: exactly five stones in a row win. A row of six or more does not count, but the game goes on.
# Six in a row
Black places one stone to begin; after that, each player places two stones every turn. Six or more in a row win, even when the row is made by the first stone of a turn.
# Renju
- Opening: Black's first stone goes on the centre point, White's first next to it, and Black's second within two points of the centre.
- Black may not make a double three, a double four, or a row of six or more, unless the same stone makes five. These points carry a small red cross.
- Black wins only with exactly five in a row. White wins with five or more.
