Shogi Mate
# The aim
Checkmate the king at the top. You play the pieces pointing up and move first; the pieces in your hand can be dropped, as in shogi.
- Every one of your moves must give check.
- The defender holds every piece that is neither on the board nor in your hand, and always plays the defence that lasts longest.
- The pieces move, promote and drop as in shogi; a pawn dropped to give mate is still not allowed.
# The end of the game
The problem is solved at checkmate; on your last move any mate is right. Before that, a check other than the right one is a mistake: the king's escape is shown, then both moves are taken back.
# Levels and stars
No hint and no mistake: 3 stars; up to two hints and mistakes together: 2 stars; otherwise 1.
