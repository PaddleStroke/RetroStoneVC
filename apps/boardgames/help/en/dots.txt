Dots and Boxes
# The aim
Claim more boxes than the other player. Light plays first.
# A turn
- Draw one line between two dots next to each other, across or up and down.
- If your line closes the fourth side of a box, the box is yours. One line can close two boxes at once.
- Each time you close a box, you must draw another line. Your turn ends with a line that closes no box.
# The end of the game
When every line is drawn, the player with more boxes wins. There is always a winner.
