Train Switches
# The aim
Toy trains wait at their portals. Set the switches, then send the trains: each one must stop at a station of its colour.
# A move
A move flips one switch. A train entering a switch by its single end takes the branch the switch is set to; entering by a branch, it simply runs through.
- The trains leave one at a time, in the order on their cabs, each once the one before has stopped.
- A switch with a brass disc flips by itself after each train that runs over it.
- A crossing lets trains run straight on. A tunnel leads to the other tunnel with the same number.
- When the trains all leave at once, two trains meeting on a square, or head on, crash.
A wrong station, a dead end or a crash ends the run: the trains come back for another try.
# The end of the game
The puzzle is solved when every train has stopped at a station of its colour.
# Levels and stars
3 stars: the fewest flips possible, at the first run, with no hint. 2 stars: up to half as many flips again, with at most two hints and failed runs together. Otherwise 1.
