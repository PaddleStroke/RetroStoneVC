Star Battle
# The aim
Place stars in the grid so that every row, every column and every outlined region holds the same number of stars: one, or two on the 10 × 10 grid. Two stars never touch, not even at a corner. Every puzzle has exactly one solution, found by reasoning alone.
# A move
Tap a cell to put a star in it, tap again for a dot (a note that it stays empty), and once more to empty it.
# The end of the game
The puzzle is solved when all the stars are in place.
# Levels and stars
No hint and no mistake: 3 stars; up to two hints and mistakes together: 2 stars; otherwise 1. A mistake is a wrong star or dot that you take back with undo.
