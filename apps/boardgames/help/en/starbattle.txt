Star Battle
# The aim
Place stars in the grid so that every row, every column and every outlined region holds the same number of stars: one on the 6 × 6 and 8 × 8 grids, two on the 10 × 10 grid. Two stars never touch, not even at a corner. Every puzzle has exactly one solution, found by reasoning alone. Choose the grid and the level (the stones under each picture):
- Easy: the cells around a star are empty; a line or region with all its stars has no more; one with as many cells left as stars missing has a star in each of them.
- Medium: you will also need cells whose star would leave a line or region too little room, and regions squeezed into a single line (the rest of that line is then empty).
- Hard: two or three regions squeezed into as many lines, and following a star through to see where it leads.
The two-star grid is a step harder at each level: its easy puzzles already need the medium ways.
# A move
Put a star in a cell, or a dot in a cell you know is empty. The dots are only notes for you.
# The end of the game
The puzzle is solved when all the stars are in place: a wave of gold light runs over the board.
# Playing on the device
Tap a cell to put a star in it, tap again for a dot, and once more to empty it. Drag along a row or a column from an empty cell to put dots in the empty cells on the way, or from a dot to take the dots away. Stars that touch, or too many stars in a line or region, turn red. The lamp at the right of the bar gives a hint: it takes away a wrong star or dot, or else places the easiest star, in gold light. Undo takes back one action at a time, all the way to the start. The bar shows your moves.
