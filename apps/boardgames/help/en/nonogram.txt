Nonograms
# The aim
A picture is hidden in the grid. The numbers left of each row and above each column are its clues: the lengths of the runs of filled squares in that line, in order, with at least one empty square between two runs (0: the line is empty). Fill the squares to bring out the picture.
Every picture can be found by reasoning one line at a time, without guessing.
# A move
Tap a square to fill it. Hold it to mark it empty with a cross, a note for you. Drag along a line to do the same to a whole run.
# The end of the game
When every row and every column matches its clues, the puzzle is solved and the picture comes out in its colours.
# Levels and stars
No hint and no mistake: 3 stars; up to two hints and mistakes together: 2 stars; otherwise 1. A mistake is a square filled outside the picture, or a cross on it, which you take back with undo.
