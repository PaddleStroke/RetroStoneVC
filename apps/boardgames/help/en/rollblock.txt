Roll the Block
# The aim
A wooden block, one tile wide and two tall, stands on a path of stone tiles. Roll it to the hole and stand it upright on it: it drops in.
# A move
The block rolls over one of its edges, up, down, left or right: standing, it falls flat onto the next two tiles; lying, it rolls along or stands up. It may never hang over the void.
- Cracked tiles bear the block lying, never standing.
- A brass button opens or shuts the bridges of its colour: a round one under any part of the block, a cross only under the block standing.
- On the split tile the block splits into two cubes, sent to the two rings. Move one cube at a time (tap a cube to choose it); side by side, they join again.
# The end of the game
Standing on the hole, the block sinks in: solved.
# Levels and stars
Solved in the fewest moves possible: 3 stars; with up to half as many moves again: 2 stars; otherwise 1.
