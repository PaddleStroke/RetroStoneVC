Breakthrough
# The aim
Be the first to bring one of your pieces to the far side of the board, the other player's home row. Light moves first.
# How the pieces move
- A piece moves one square forward, straight ahead or diagonally, to an empty square.
- It captures by moving one square diagonally forward onto an enemy piece. It never captures straight ahead: an enemy piece right in front of it blocks it.
- Pieces never move sideways or backwards. Capturing is never compulsory.
# The end of the game
- The first player to reach the other player's home row wins.
- A player who loses all their pieces loses.
There are no draws.
