Breakthrough
# The aim
Break through the other army: be the first to bring one of your pieces to the far side of the board, the other player's home row.
# The start
The game is played on a chessboard. Each player has sixteen pieces, filling their own first two rows. Light moves first, then the players take turns, one move each.
# How the pieces move
- A piece moves one square forward, straight ahead or diagonally, to an empty square.
- A piece captures by moving one square diagonally forward onto an enemy piece, which leaves the board.
- A piece never captures straight ahead: an enemy piece right in front of it blocks it.
- Pieces never move sideways or backwards. Capturing is never compulsory.
# The end of the game
- The first player to reach the other player's home row wins.
- A player who loses all their pieces loses.
There are no draws: a player with pieces left can always move.
# Playing on the device
Tap a piece to see where it can go, then tap a square, or drag the piece. Dots show the moves, rings the captures. The winning piece shines gold.
