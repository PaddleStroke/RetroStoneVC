Canfield
# The aim
Move all 52 cards to the four foundations, one for each suit.
# The layout
- The reserve: 13 cards, only the top one face up.
- The card on the first foundation: its rank starts every foundation.
- Four tableau piles; the other 34 cards are the stock.
# Moving cards
- Foundations go up by suit, round the corner: after the king, the ace.
- On the tableau, one rank lower and of the other colour, round the corner too: a black king on a red ace.
- Move the top card of a pile or the whole pile, never part of one.
- The top card of the reserve can go to a foundation or the tableau.
- An empty pile is filled at once from the reserve; once the reserve is empty, by any card.
- A card on a foundation stays there.
# The stock
Tap the stock to turn three cards onto the waste; only the top one can be played. Turn the waste back over as often as you like.
# The end of the game
You win when all the cards are on the foundations.
# Levels and stars
3 stars: no hint and no undo.
