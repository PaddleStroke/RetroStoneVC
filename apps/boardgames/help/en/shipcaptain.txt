Ship, Captain and Crew
# The aim
Win 5 chips by loading the best cargo. You need a ship, a captain and a crew before you can carry anything. For 2 to 6 players.
# A turn
- You have up to three rolls of five dice.
- You need a 6 (the ship), then a 5 (the captain), then a 4 (the crew), in that order; one roll may bring several. They are set aside as they come.
- With all three aboard, the two dice left are your cargo: their sum is your score. With a roll left, stop or roll both cargo dice again: the last roll counts.
- Without ship, captain and crew after three rolls, you have no cargo.
# A round
Each player plays one turn. The highest cargo wins a chip; equal highest each win one.
# The end of the game
The first player with 5 chips at the end of a round wins; players who reach 5 together share the win.
