Valley Harvest
# The aim
Grow your village in a shared valley. After the last round, the most points wins.
# A turn
- Send a worker to a free land in or next to your village and harvest it: one good, plus one for each matching workshop beside it, plus one for a field by the river. Another player's workshop earns its owner a coin.
- Then build and trade as you like.
# Building
Build next to your village.
- Road: your village reaches further; the land stays free to harvest.
- House: a point and one more worker.
- Sawmill, mill, quarry: points, and more goods from their land around them.
- Manor, on one of your houses: more points.
Building beside face-down land turns it up. Ruins pay their finder.
# The market
Prices rise as the stock runs low. Each round, the valley uses one of each good.
# The end of the game
Turns go round until every worker is out; the player with the fewest points starts the next round. At the end, every four coins score a point. A tie goes to the most coins.
