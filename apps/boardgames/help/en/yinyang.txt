Yin-Yang
# The aim
Put a stone, ivory or ebony, in every cell of the grid.
- All the ivory stones are joined in one group, side by side, and so are all the ebony stones.
- No 2 × 2 square holds four stones of one colour.
- The stones shown at the start stay where they are.
Every puzzle has exactly one solution, found by reasoning alone.
# The end of the game
The puzzle is solved when every cell holds its stone: a wave of gold light runs over the grid.
# Levels and stars
No hint and no mistake: 3 stars; up to two hints and mistakes together: 2 stars; otherwise 1. A mistake is a wrong stone that you take back with undo.
