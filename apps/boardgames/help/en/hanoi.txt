Tower of Hanoi
# The aim
Discs of different sizes stand on three pegs, never a disc on a smaller one. Gather them all on the goal peg, the one lit in gold at its foot, as one tower with the largest at the bottom.
# A move
Take the top disc of a peg and put it on another peg, either empty or with a larger disc on top. A disc never goes on a smaller one.
# The end of the game
The puzzle is solved when every disc stands on the goal peg.
# Levels and stars
Solved at par, the fewest moves that solve it: 3 stars; with up to half as many moves again: 2 stars; otherwise 1.
