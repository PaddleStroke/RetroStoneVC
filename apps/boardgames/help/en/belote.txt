Belote
# The aim
Four players in two teams, partners opposite. Be the first team to reach 501 points in Belote, 1000 in Coinche. The pack has 32 cards, from 7 to ace.
# The cards
- Trumps, from high to low: jack 20, 9 14, ace 11, 10 10, king 4, queen 3, 8 0, 7 0.
- Other suits: ace 11, 10 10, king 4, queen 3, jack 2, 9 0, 8 0, 7 0.
- The last trick earns 10 more (dix de der): 162 points in each deal.
- The king and queen of trumps in one hand are belote and rebelote: 20 points.
# Choosing trumps (Belote)
- Each player gets 5 cards, and the next card is turned face up.
- In turn, take it (its suit is trumps) or pass. If all four pass, each in turn names another suit or passes; if they pass again, new cards.
- The taker gets the turned card and 2 more, the others 3.
# The auction (Coinche)
- In turn, bid a contract in a suit, from 80 to 160 in steps of 10, or capot (every trick, 250), higher than the last bid; or pass.
- You may coinche the other team's bid (double); the bidder may surcoinche (double again) or pass. Either way the auction is over.
- Three passes after a bid end the auction; four passes without a bid: new cards.
# The play
- The player after the dealer leads; the winner of a trick leads the next one.
- Follow suit if you can. If not, play a trump, higher than any trump in the trick if you can; with no trump, any card.
- But when your partner is winning the trick, you may play any card.
- When trumps are led, always play a higher trump if you can, even over your partner.
- The highest trump wins the trick, otherwise the highest card of the suit led.
# The score (Belote)
- The takers must make more points than the other team, belote included: then each team scores its points.
- Otherwise they are dedans: the other team scores 162, the takers nothing. Belote always stays with the team that held it.
- A team that takes every trick is capot: 250 instead of 162.
# The score (Coinche)
- The contract is made if the takers make at least their bid (belote included) and more than 81; capot needs every trick.
- Made: the takers score their contract plus their points, the defenders their own points.
- Failed: the defenders score 160 plus the contract.
- Coinched or surcoinched: the winner scores 160 plus the contract, doubled or quadrupled; the other side only its belote.
# The end of the game
The game ends after the deal in which a team reaches the target. More points wins; if the totals are equal, one more deal is played.
