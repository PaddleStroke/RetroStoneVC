Sea Battle
# The aim
Each player hides a fleet on a sea of 10 by 10 squares. Take turns firing at the other player's sea: the first to sink the whole enemy fleet wins. For 2 players. Choose the game first:
- Classic: five ships of 5, 4, 3, 3 and 2 squares (the usual modern fleet), one shot a turn.
- Salvo: the same fleet; each turn you fire as many shots as you have ships still afloat.
- Paper fleet: ten ships, one of 4 squares, two of 3, three of 2 and four of 1, the fleet played on squared paper. The ships may not touch, not even at a corner. One shot a turn.
- Paper fleet with salvos: the ten ships, and a salvo each turn.
# Placing the fleet
Ivory places first, then Ebony, each in secret. A ship lies straight, across or down, inside the sea; two ships never share a square. Your fleet starts in a random place:
- Drag a ship to move it, or tap it and then tap the square for its top or left end.
- Tap the lifted ship again to turn it round the square you tapped.
- The die gives another random fleet. The tick says your fleet is ready, and the device passes.
# A turn
Ivory fires first. Tap a square of the other player's sea to aim at it, then tap the fire button; with a single shot, tapping the aimed square again fires too. A salvo is aimed all at once: the number beside the fire button tells how many shots are left to aim, and tapping an aimed square takes it back. Hit or miss, the turn then ends.
- A white peg: a miss. A red peg: a hit.
- When every square of a ship is hit, it is sunk, and it appears on the sea.
The result stays on the screen for a moment, then the device passes.
# The end of the game
The first player to sink the whole enemy fleet wins.
# Playing on the device
You see the other player's sea big, with your shots on it, and your own sea small beside it, with your fleet and the other player's shots; their last shots are marked. A cover hides the screen whenever the device passes, until the next player taps start. Each player's bar shows how many of their ships are still afloat. Undo takes back a ship moved while placing, or a square aimed at; a shot fired stays.
