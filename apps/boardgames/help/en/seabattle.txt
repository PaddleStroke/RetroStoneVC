Sea Battle
# The aim
Each player hides a fleet on a sea of 10 by 10 squares. Take turns firing at the other player's sea: the first to sink the whole enemy fleet wins. For 2 players.
- Classic and salvo: five ships of 5, 4, 3, 3 and 2 squares.
- Paper fleet: ten ships, one of 4 squares, two of 3, three of 2 and four of 1, which may not touch, not even at a corner.
- With salvos, each turn you fire as many shots as you have ships afloat; otherwise one.
# Placing the fleet
Ivory places first, then Ebony, in secret. Ships lie straight, across or down, and never share a square. Drag a ship to move it; tap it twice to turn it. The tick says your fleet is ready.
# A turn
Tap the squares to aim at, then the fire button. Hit or miss, the turn then ends.
- A white peg: a miss. A red peg: a hit.
- A ship hit on every square is sunk and appears on the sea.
