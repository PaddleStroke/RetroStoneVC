Chinese Checkers
# The aim
Move all ten of your marbles across the star into the point opposite your own. Light plays first, then the others clockwise round the star.
# A move
Move one of your marbles in one of two ways:
- A step: to an empty hole next to it.
- Jumps: over a marble next to it, of either colour, to the empty hole just beyond, in a straight line. The same marble may jump again, as often as you like and in any direction, but never back onto a hole it has been on during this move.
- Nothing is captured. A step and jumps cannot be mixed. Marbles may cross and stop in the empty points, and may leave the point they are filling.
# The end of the game
The first player to fill the opposite point wins: once every hole there is taken and at least one holds your marble, you win, even if others hold an opponent's marbles. With more than two players, the marbles of any player left in your target point count for you, and a player with no move passes. If the player to move has no move (with more players: if nobody can move), the game is a draw.
