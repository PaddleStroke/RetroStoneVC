Pairs
# The aim
Find the pairs of pictures. For 1 to 4 players; alone, find them all in as few moves as you can.
# A turn
- Turn two cards face up.
- A pair: you keep it and play again.
- Not a pair: they turn face down again, and the next player plays.
# The end of the game
When all the pairs are found, the most pairs wins; equal players share the win. Alone, your score is your moves: each two cards turned are one move.
