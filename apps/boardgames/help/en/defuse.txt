Defuse the Bomb
# The aim
Disarm the bomb before its timer reaches zero: light the green DISARM lamp, or put out the amber POWER lamp. If the red DETONATE lamp lights while the bomb has power, even for an instant, it explodes.
# A move
A move flips a switch or cuts a cable. Everything is one circuit fed by the battery: a move often changes all three lamps at once, so the order of your moves matters.
- A cut cable carries 0 beyond the cut, for good.
- NOT reverses its input. AND gives 1 when all its inputs are 1, OR when any is, XOR when exactly one is. NAND and NOR reverse AND and OR.
- A flip-flop turns over each time its input goes from 0 to 1.
- A capacitor charges a step each second while its input is 1; full, it fires a short pulse. Its input at 0 empties it.
- The board timer counts down by itself; at zero it gives 1.
# Levels and stars
3 stars: the fewest moves possible, with no hint. 2 stars: up to half as many moves again, or with a hint. Otherwise 1.
