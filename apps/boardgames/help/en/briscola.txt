Briscola
# The aim
Win the tricks that hold the cards worth the most points: there are 120 points in all, and 61 win. For 2 players, or 4 in two partnerships sitting face to face.
# The cards
The Italian pack of 40 cards. From high to low: ace, 3, re, cavallo, fante, 7, 6, 5, 4, 2. Here the fante is a pawn, the cavallo a knight and the re a king.
# The deal
Each player gets 3 cards. The next card is turned face up: it is the briscola, and its suit is trumps. It is the last card drawn.
# Playing
- Each player in turn plays any card: you never have to follow suit.
- The highest trump wins the trick; with no trump, the highest card of the suit led.
- While the stock lasts, each player then draws a card, the winner first. The winner leads the next trick.
# Scoring
Ace 11 points, three 10, re 4, cavallo 3, fante 2, the other cards nothing.
# The end of the game
A game is one deal. The side with 61 points or more wins; 60 each is a draw.
