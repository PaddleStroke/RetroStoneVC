Bridge
# The aim
Contract bridge, for four players in two partnerships sitting face to face: bid for the right to name trumps, then take the tricks you promised.
# Bidding
The dealer calls first, then each player in turn:
- a bid: 1 to 7 and a suit or no trumps, promising that many tricks over six. It must beat the last bid: a higher number, or the same number in a higher suit (clubs, diamonds, hearts, spades, no trumps).
- pass;
- double the opponents' last bid; redouble a bid of your side that was doubled.
Three passes after a bid end the auction. The last bid is the contract and its suit is trumps. The declarer is the player of that side who first named that suit; their partner is the dummy. Four passes: the cards are dealt again.
# Playing
- The player on the declarer's left leads. Then the dummy's hand is laid face up, and the declarer plays it too.
- Follow the suit led if you can; otherwise play any card. The highest trump wins the trick, or else the highest card of the suit led. The winner leads next.
# Scoring
- Contract made: each trick bid over six scores 20 in clubs or diamonds, 30 in hearts or spades; at no trumps 40 for the first, then 30. Doubled twice that, redoubled four times. 100 or more make a game.
- Each overtrick: its trick value; doubled 100 (vulnerable 200), redoubled twice that. A doubled contract made earns 50 more, a redoubled one 100.
- Slams: 12 tricks bid and made 500 (vulnerable 750), all 13 tricks 1000 (vulnerable 1500).
- Contract down: the defenders score 50 per missing trick (vulnerable 100). Doubled: 100, 200, 200, then 300 each (vulnerable 200, then 300 each); redoubled twice that.
# The end of the game
- Chicago: four deals. Vulnerable: nobody on the first, the dealer's side on the second and third, both sides on the fourth. A game earns 300 more (vulnerable 500), any smaller contract 50.
- Rubber: a game wipes out both sides' part-scores, and a side that has won one is vulnerable. Two games end the rubber: 700 more for two to none, 500 for two to one. Honours, rubber only: the five top trumps in one hand 150, four of them 100, the four aces at no trumps 150.
- The side with the higher total wins.
