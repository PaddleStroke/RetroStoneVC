Go
# The aim
Surround more of the board than the other player. Stones are played on the points and never move. Black plays first.
# Liberties and captures
- The empty points next to a stone, along the lines, are its liberties. Stones of one colour that touch along the lines form a group and share their liberties.
- When you take a group's last liberty, it is captured: its stones leave the board.
- You may not play a stone that would have no liberty, unless it captures and so gains one.
- Ko: no move may bring back a position seen before. When a single stone could be taken straight back, play elsewhere first.
# Passing and counting
Instead of playing, you may pass. When both players pass one after the other, the game stops for counting.
- Tap the groups that could not live: they count as captured. When you agree, tap the pass button.
- Each player scores one point for each of their stones on the board and each empty point they alone surround. White also gets 7.5 points for playing second, so there is never a draw.
# Capture go
A first game for beginners: the first player to capture wins at once. No passing, no counting. A player with nowhere left to play loses.
