Binairo
# The aim
Fill the grid with ivory and ebony discs. Three discs of the same colour may never stand side by side in a row or a column. Every row and every column holds as many ivory discs as ebony ones. No two rows may be the same, nor two columns.
Some discs are given at the start. Every puzzle has exactly one solution, found by reasoning alone.
# A move
Put a disc in an empty cell, change its colour or take it out. The given discs stay.
# The end of the game
The puzzle is solved when the grid is full and keeps every rule.
# Levels and stars
No hint and no mistake: 3 stars; up to two hints and mistakes together: 2 stars; otherwise 1. A mistake is a disc of the wrong colour that you take back with undo.
