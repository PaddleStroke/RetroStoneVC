Binairo
# The aim
Fill the grid with ivory and ebony discs. Three discs of the same colour may never stand side by side in a row or a column. Every row and every column holds as many ivory discs as ebony ones. No two rows may be the same, nor two columns.
Some discs are given at the start, on darker cells. Every puzzle has exactly one solution, found by reasoning alone. Choose the size (from 6 × 6 to 12 × 12) and the level (the stones under each picture):
- Easy: two alike side by side, or two alike with a gap between them, give the colour of the cells beside them; a line with all its discs of one colour takes the other colour everywhere else.
- Medium: you will also need to look at a whole line: all the ways to fill it agree on some cells.
- Hard: longer chains of reasoning, never a guess.
# A move
Put a disc in an empty cell, change its colour or take it out. The given discs stay.
# The end of the game
The puzzle is solved when the grid is full and keeps every rule: a wave of gold light runs over the board.
# Levels and stars
There are 150 levels in six chapters, from 6 × 6 up to 12 × 12, each with one solution; choose one on the level screen. The die tab after the chapters makes a new puzzle at random, with the choice described above. No hint and no mistake: 3 stars; up to two hints and mistakes together: 2 stars; otherwise 1. A mistake is a disc of the wrong colour that you then take back with undo.
# Playing on the device
- Tap an empty cell for an ivory disc, again for an ebony one, once more to empty it.
- Hold a cell a moment to go the other way round: ebony first.
- Drag along a row or a column to do the same to a whole stretch. It only changes the cells that were like the first one.
Three alike in a row glow red; a row or a column with too many discs of one colour, or full and the same as another, turns red. The button at the right end of the bar (in landscape, in the side column) is the light bulb: a hint. It puts a wrong disc right, or else shows the next cell reasoning finds, in gold light. Undo takes back one action at a time, all the way to the start. The bar shows your moves.
