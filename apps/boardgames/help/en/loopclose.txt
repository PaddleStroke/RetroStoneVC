Close the Loops
# The aim
Every square holds a tile with a piece of line: an end, a straight, a corner, a tee or a cross. Turn the tiles until every line meets another one, so the lines close into shapes. No line may stay open or run into the edge of the board.
# A move
Tap a tile to turn it a quarter turn clockwise.
# The end of the game
When every line is closed, the shapes fill with colour. Any way of closing them wins.
# Levels and stars
Each puzzle has a par: the fewest quarter turns that solve it. Solved at par: 3 stars; with up to half as many moves again: 2 stars; otherwise 1. A hint keeps the puzzle to 2 stars, and more than two hints to 1.
