How to play
# Playing together
Two players share one device and take turns. In card games the device goes round the table.
- Secret hands: between two turns the screen goes dark and shows the next player's token. Pass the device; that player taps the round button to see their cards.
- Undo: takes back the last move; tap again to go further back. With secret hands, only the moves of your own turn.
- Face to face: lay the device flat between you. The top player's pieces and bar turn to face them.
# Playing against the device
- Tap a seat to give it to the device. Tap again to make it play better (one, two or three lights: easy, medium, hard), and once more to give the seat back to a person.
