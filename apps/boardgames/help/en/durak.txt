Durak
# The aim
Get rid of all your cards. The last player still holding cards is the durak, the fool, and loses.
# The cards
- From low to high: 6 to 10, jack, queen, king, ace. Six cards each; the card turned up under the stock gives trumps and is drawn last.
- The lowest trump attacks first; the next player defends.
# A bout
- The attacker leads a card, or several of the same rank.
- The defender beats each with a higher card of its suit, or with a trump; a trump only by a higher trump.
- Once all are beaten, the others, the attacker first, may throw in cards of a rank on the table, to be beaten too. At most six attacking cards, never more than the defender held at the start.
- A defender who cannot or will not beat takes: after the last throw-ins, they pick up every card on the table.
# After a bout
- All beaten: the cards are set aside and the defender attacks next. Taken: the player after the defender attacks.
- Everyone draws back up to six, the attacker first, the defender last.
# The end
Once the stock is empty, a player with no cards left is out, safe. If the last players run out together, nobody loses.
# The transfer game
Before beating, the defender may pass the attack on with a card of the same rank, if the next player holds at least as many cards as are then on the table. That player defends against all of them; the one who passed joins the attack.
