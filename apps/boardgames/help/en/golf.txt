Golf
# The aim
Have the lowest score after the last round.
# At the start of a round
- Each player turns two of their cards face up.
# A turn
- Draw from the stock, or take the top card of the discard pile.
- Swap it for one of your cards: your old card goes face up onto the discard pile.
- Or, if you drew it from the stock, discard it and turn one of your face-down cards up.
# The end of a round
As soon as a player has all their cards face up, every other player has one last turn.
# Classic Golf
- Ace 1 point, 2 to 10 their value, jack and queen 10, king 0.
- Two cards of the same rank in one column score 0.
- The game lasts nine rounds.
# Number Golf
- Each card scores its number, from -2 to 12.
- Three face-up cards of the same number in one column are discarded at once and score nothing.
- The player who ended the round scores double if their score is above zero and not strictly the lowest of the round.
- The game ends after the round in which a player reaches 100 points.
# The end of the game
The lowest total wins.
