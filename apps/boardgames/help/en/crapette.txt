Crapette
# The aim
A race patience for two: be the first to empty your crapette, your talon and your waste pile.
# The layout
- The 8 houses and the 8 foundations in the middle are shared. The higher crapette card starts.
# Moving cards
- You may move the top card of your crapette or of a house, and your turned talon card.
- Foundations: ace to king in suit. Houses: down in alternating colours; an empty house takes any card.
- Loading: you may put a card of the same suit, one rank above or below, on your opponent's crapette or waste pile.
# The priorities
- A card that can go to a foundation must go there first, your crapette's top card first of all.
- Next, play your crapette's top card if you can, before you turn your talon.
# The talon
Turn the top card of your talon: play it and turn the next one, or put it on your waste pile, which ends your turn. An empty talon is remade from your waste pile.
# Crapette!
If you break a priority, your opponent may call "Crapette!" with the red cross in their bar: your mistake is undone, the talon card you turned goes onto your waste pile, and your turn ends.
# The end of the game
The first to empty all three piles wins. If nothing can move any more, the player with fewer cards left wins.
