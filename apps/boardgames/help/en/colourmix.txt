Colour Mixer
# The aim
Mix paint in the white pot until it is the colour on the card.
# The paints
Each tap on a tube adds one drop. Paints mix like real paints, not like light: red and yellow make orange, yellow and blue make green, red and blue make violet.
- White makes a colour lighter; black makes it darker, and a little black goes a long way.
- Only how much of each paint there is compared with the others counts: one drop of red and one of yellow give the same orange as two of each.
- Some pots already hold paint, and some tubes hold only a few drops: the badge on the tube shows how many are left.
The meter under the pot fills as the colour gets closer.
# The end of the game
When the pot's colour matches the card, the puzzle is solved.
# Levels and stars
Solved with the fewest drops possible: 3 stars; with up to half as many drops again: 2 stars; otherwise 1.
