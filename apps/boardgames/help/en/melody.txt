Play by Ear
# The aim
A short tune plays on the piano, and each key lights up in gold as its note sounds. Play the notes back in the same order. Only the order of the notes counts, not their rhythm.
# A move
Tap a key. The right key glows in gold and the staff writes its note. A wrong key flashes red: it counts as a mistake, and you go on from the same place.
# The end of the game
When the last note is played, the tune's title shows, with its composer.
# Levels and stars
No mistake, no hint and at most one listen again: 3 stars; up to two of them together: 2 stars; otherwise 1.
