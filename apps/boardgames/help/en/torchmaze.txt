Torch Maze
# The aim
A little mouse is lost in a dark maze with only a torch. Find the stairs out: their golden glow shows through the dark.
# A move
Swipe to walk along a corridor to the next turning; tap beside the mouse to take a single step.
The torch lights only the cells around it. What it has shown stays drawn on your map in chalk.
- A key opens the doors of its colour.
- A pressure plate opens the closed gates and closes the open ones.
- A one-way door lets you through in the arrow's direction only.
- A brazier you step on stays lit and lights its part of the maze.
- In some mazes the torch burns down as you walk; an oil flask fills it again. If it goes out, undo.
- In the last mazes the chalk map fades after a while.
Undo takes back your steps, never your map.
# The end of the game
The puzzle is solved when the mouse reaches the stairs.
# Levels and stars
In no more steps than the flag shows: 3 stars; with up to half as many steps again: 2 stars; otherwise 1. With a hint, 2 stars at most; with three or more, 1.
