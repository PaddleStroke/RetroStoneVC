Torch Maze
# The aim
A little mouse with a torch is lost in a dark maze. Find the stairs out: their golden glow shows through the dark.
# A move
Swipe to walk to the next turning; tap beside the mouse for a single step.
The torch lights only the cells around it. What it shows stays on your map in chalk.
- A key opens the doors of its colour.
- A pressure plate opens the closed gates and closes the open ones.
- A one-way door opens only in the arrow's direction.
- A brazier you step on stays lit.
- In some mazes the torch burns down as you walk; an oil flask refills it. If it goes out, undo.
- In the last mazes the chalk map fades.
Undo takes back steps, never the map.
# The end of the game
The puzzle is solved when the mouse reaches the stairs.
# Levels and stars
In no more steps than the flag shows: 3 stars; up to half as many again: 2 stars; otherwise 1. With a hint, 2 stars at most; with three, 1.
