Marble Push
# The aim
Push six of the other player's marbles off the board. Dark plays first.
# Moving
Move one, two or three of your marbles one hole in any of the six directions. Two or three must be next to each other in a straight row, and move together (tap each of them, or slide across them):
- In line: along their own row. The front marble goes into the hole ahead and the others follow it.
- Sideways: each marble into the hole beside it. All these holes must be empty.
- You may never move your own marble off the board.
# Pushing
When you move a row in line, you may push the other player's marbles straight ahead of it, if yours are more: two marbles push one, three push one or two.
- The hole just beyond the pushed marbles must be empty, or be the edge: then the last pushed marble falls off the board.
- There is no push if one of your own marbles stands among or just beyond them. No marble is ever pushed sideways.
# The end of the game
- The first player to push six marbles off wins.
- The game is a draw when the same position comes back for the third time with the same player to move, or after fifty moves each without a marble pushed off.
