Bridges
# The aim
Islands lie on the board, each with a number. Join them with bridges, straight across or down, between two islands with nothing in between. Two islands may have one bridge or two between them, never more, and no bridge may cross another. Each island must end with as many bridges as its number, and all the islands must be joined into one group. Every puzzle has exactly one solution, found by reasoning alone.
# A move
Drag from an island towards another: a bridge is laid. Do it again for a second bridge, and once more to take both away.
# The end of the game
The puzzle is solved when every island has its bridges and all are joined.
# Levels and stars
No hint and no mistake: 3 stars; up to two hints and mistakes together: 2 stars; otherwise 1. A mistake is a bridge the solution does not have, which you take back with undo.
