Skat
# The aim
Germany's great card game for three, by the rules of the International Skat Order. In each deal the declarer, who wins the bidding, plays alone against the other two.
# The deal
32 cards, 7 to ace. 10 cards each; 2 more lie face down: the skat. After the dealer come forehand, who leads first, middlehand and rearhand (the dealer).
# Bidding
- The bids are game values: 18, 20, 22, 23, 24, 27, 30, 33, 35, 36, 40 and so on.
- Middlehand bids to forehand, who says yes or passes; middlehand goes higher or passes. The one left then hears rearhand in the same way. The last player left is the declarer, at the last bid.
- If nobody bids, forehand may still bid 18; if he passes too, the cards are thrown in (the deal counts in the list, with no score).
# The skat and the game
- The declarer picks up the skat and lays any two cards away, or plays hand, leaving it untouched. Either way the skat counts for him.
- He names the game: a suit (diamonds 9, hearts 10, spades 11, clubs 12), grand (24: only the jacks are trumps) or null.
- Null: take no trick at all. No trumps; every suit ranks A K Q J 10 9 8 7. Worth 23, null hand 35, null ouvert 46, null ouvert hand 59; a null worth less than the bid cannot be chosen.
- In a hand game he may announce schneider, schwarz (with schneider) and ouvert (with schwarz: his cards face up). Null ouvert is open too.
# Trumps and play
- In suit games and grand the four jacks are the highest trumps: clubs, spades, hearts, diamonds. In a suit game the trump suit comes next: A 10 K Q 9 8 7.
- The other suits rank A 10 K Q 9 8 7. A jack always belongs to the trumps.
- Follow suit if you can, otherwise play any card. The highest trump wins, otherwise the highest card of the suit led. The winner leads next.
# Winning
- Card points: ace 11, ten 10, king 4, queen 3, jack 2: 120 in all. With the skat, the declarer needs 61.
- Schneider: a side with 30 points or less. Schwarz: a side without a trick. Announced, schneider needs 90 points and schwarz every trick.
- Null is lost as soon as the declarer takes a trick.
# The game value
- Suit games and grand: the base value (9, 10, 11, 12 or 24) times the levels.
- The matadors: the unbroken run of top trumps, from the jack of clubs down, that the declarer holds ("with 2") or lacks ("without 3"), the skat included.
- The levels: the matadors, plus 1 each for the game, hand, schneider, schneider announced, schwarz, schwarz announced and ouvert.
- Example: clubs with 2, played hand: (2 + 1 + 1) x 12 = 48.
- Overbid: a value below the bid loses, and counts as the lowest multiple of its base value that reaches the bid.
# Scoring
- A game won scores its value; a game lost twice its value, below zero.
- Seeger-Fabian points: 50 for each game won, minus 50 for each game lost, and 40 to each of the two others for every game a declarer loses.
- After the last deal of the list, the highest total wins; equal totals share the win.
