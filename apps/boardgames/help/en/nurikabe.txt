Nurikabe
# The aim
Fill in the sea so that the cells left free form islands.
- Each number lies on an island of exactly that many cells, and each island holds just one number.
- Two islands never touch side by side; they may meet at a corner.
- The sea is all one piece, joined side by side, and it never fills a 2 × 2 square.
Every puzzle has exactly one solution, found by reasoning alone. Hold a cell to put a dot in it: a note for land.
# The end of the game
The puzzle is solved when every rule is kept: a wave of gold light runs over the grid.
# Levels and stars
No hint and no mistake: 3 stars; up to two hints and mistakes together: 2 stars; otherwise 1. A mistake is a wrong cell that you take back with undo.
