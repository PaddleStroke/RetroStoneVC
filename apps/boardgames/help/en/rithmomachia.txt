Rithmomachia
# The aim
Two armies of numbers, the evens (light) and the odds (dark). Win by taking enemy pieces, or by setting three of your pieces in harmony in the enemy's half. The evens move first.
# Moving
A piece moves to an empty square and never jumps over another piece.
- Round: one square diagonally.
- Triangle: exactly two squares along a row or a column.
- Square: exactly three squares along a row or a column.
- Pyramid: like any of the three.
# Captures
After your move, you take every enemy piece caught in one of these four ways:
- Meeting: one of your pieces with the same number could move onto it.
- Assault: a smaller piece of yours on one of the lines it moves along, with only empty squares between them: its number times the number of empty squares equals the enemy's number.
- Ambush: two of your pieces that could both move onto it add up to its number.
- Siege: your pieces stand on all four sides of it, across and up and down; the edge of the board closes a side too.
The pyramid counts as a single piece with its whole number.
# Victories
- Take 8 or Take 12: the first player to take that many enemy pieces wins.
- Proper victory: three of your pieces in the enemy's half, on one row, column or diagonal, equally spaced, whose numbers form a progression in their order along the line: arithmetic (4 6 8), geometric (4 16 64) or harmonic (3 4 6).
# The end of the game
A player who cannot move loses. After 100 moves in a row (both players' moves counted) without a capture, the game is a draw.
