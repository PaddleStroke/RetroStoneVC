Peg Solitaire
# The aim
Marbles stand in the holes of a board. Jump them over each other until only one is left, in the goal hole: the one with a brass ring.
# A move
A marble jumps over a neighbouring marble, in a straight line, into the empty hole just beyond it. The marble jumped over is taken off the board. On the crosses and the diamond the jumps go across and up and down, never diagonally; on the triangles they go along their three lines.
# The end of the game
One marble left: solved. When no jump is left and several marbles remain, they glow red: undo and try another way.
# Levels and stars
One marble in the goal and no hint: 3 stars; a star less for a last marble elsewhere, and a star less for using the hint (always at least 1).
