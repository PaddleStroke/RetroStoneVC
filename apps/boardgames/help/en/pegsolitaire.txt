Peg Solitaire
# The aim
Marbles stand in the holes of a board. Jump them over each other until only one is left, in the goal hole: the one with a brass ring. The boards:
- English: the cross of 33 holes.
- French: 37 holes, the cross and four more.
- Diamond: 41 holes.
- Wiegleb's: the wide cross of 45 holes.
- Triangles: 15 holes, and the big one of 21.
# A move
A marble jumps over a neighbouring marble, in a straight line, into the empty hole just beyond it. The marble jumped over is taken off the board. On the crosses and the diamond the jumps go across and up and down, never diagonally; on the triangles they go along their three lines.
# The end of the game
One marble left: solved, in gold light if it sits in the goal hole. When no jump is left and several marbles remain, they glow red: undo and try another way.
# Levels and stars
There are 150 levels in six chapters, from 3 marbles up to the full classic boards (every hole full but one), which end their chapters; every level can end with one marble in its goal. Choose one on the level screen. The die tab after the chapters lays out a random position on the board you choose, one that can end in its goal. One marble in the goal and no hint: 3 stars; a star less for a last marble elsewhere, and a star less for using the hint (always at least 1). Each jump takes one marble off, so every game to one marble takes the same number of jumps: what counts is where the last one ends.
# Playing on the device
Tap a marble: dots show where it can jump. Tap a dot, or drag the marble there. You can also tap an empty hole that only one marble can reach. The marbles taken off roll into the groove around the board. The lamp at the end of the bar gives a hint: the jump to make lights up, in gold while it leads to one marble in the goal; when no way to one marble is left, the marbles glow red. Undo takes back one jump, all the way to the start. The bar shows the marbles left.
