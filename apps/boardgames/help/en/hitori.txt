Hitori
# The aim
Shade some cells so that no number is left twice in a row or a column.
- Only a number that repeats in its row or its column may be shaded.
- Shaded cells never touch side by side; they may meet at a corner.
- The cells left unshaded are all joined in one piece, side by side.
Every puzzle has exactly one solution, found by reasoning alone. Hold a cell to circle it: a note that it stays.
# The end of the game
The puzzle is solved when every rule is kept: a wave of gold light runs over the grid.
# Levels and stars
No hint and no mistake: 3 stars; up to two hints and mistakes together: 2 stars; otherwise 1. A mistake is a wrong cell that you take back with undo.
