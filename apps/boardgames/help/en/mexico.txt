Mexico
# The aim
Be the last player with lives left. Two dice are thrown under a cup that only the thrower sees, and the value announced may be a bluff. For 2 to 6 players.
# The values
The higher die comes first: a 3 and a 5 make 53. From the lowest:
- 31, 32, 41, 42, 43, 51 ... 65;
- then the doubles, 11 up to 66;
- then 21, Mexico, the highest.
# A round
- The first player throws, looks alone and announces a value: true or a bluff.
- The next player believes it or calls it.
- To believe it, throw again without looking at what was there, then announce a value at least as high. If your throw is lower, you must bluff.
- The cup goes round until someone calls.
# The call
The cup is lifted. If the dice show at least the value announced, the caller loses a life; if not, the announcer does. The loser starts the next round, or the next player if the loser is out.
# The end of the game
A player with no lives left is out. The last player in wins.
