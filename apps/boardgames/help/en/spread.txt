# The aim
Have the most pieces on the board at the end. Light moves first.
# A move
Move one of your pieces to an empty cell, in one of two ways:
- Clone: to a cell next to it, diagonals included. A new piece appears there and the old one stays.
- Jump: to a cell two cells away, in any direction, even over a blocked cell. The piece leaves its cell.
Then every enemy piece next to the cell you reached, diagonals included, becomes one of yours.
# The end of the game
- When the board is full, the player with more pieces wins.
- When the player to move has no move, the game ends and the other player takes all the empty cells.
- After 50 moves in a row that add no piece (only jumps), the pieces are counted as they stand.
The same number of pieces is a draw.
