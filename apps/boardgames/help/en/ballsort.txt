Ball Sort
# The aim
Glass tubes hold coloured balls, all mixed up, with two tubes empty. Move the balls until each colour fills a tube of its own. A tube holds four balls. Every puzzle can be solved.
# A move
Move the top ball of a tube onto a ball of the same colour, or into an empty tube, if the tube has room. One ball moves at a time.
# The extra tube
Once per puzzle you may set an extra empty tube on the shelf: tap the faint tube with a plus at the end of the last shelf.
# The end of the game
The puzzle is solved when every colour fills a tube of its own and the other tubes are empty.
# Levels and stars
Solved at par, the fewest moves that solve it: 3 stars; with up to half as many moves again: 2 stars; otherwise 1. Using the extra tube keeps the puzzle to 2 stars.
