Lines of Action
# The aim
Bring all your pieces together into one group, every piece touching another of yours, side by side or corner to corner. Dark moves first.
# How a piece moves
- A piece moves in a straight line, along its row, its column or a diagonal, exactly as many squares as there are pieces on that whole line: both colours, from edge to edge, the moving piece included.
- It may jump over its own pieces, but never over the other player's.
- It may land on an empty square, or on an enemy piece, which is captured. Never on its own.
# The end of the game
- A player whose pieces all form one group wins. A single piece left counts as a group.
- If a move joins both players' groups at once, the player who made it wins.
- A player with no legal move passes. If neither player can move, or after 1000 moves, the game is a draw.
