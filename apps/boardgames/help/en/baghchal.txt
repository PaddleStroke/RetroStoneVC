Bagh-Chal
# The aim
Four tigers against twenty goats. The tigers try to take five goats; the goats try to hem the tigers in so that none of them can move. The goats play first.
# Moving
- First the goats come in: on each of their turns the goats set one new goat on any empty point, until all twenty are in. Meanwhile the tigers already move. No goat on the board may move before all twenty are in.
- Then the goats move one goat along a line to the next empty point, in any direction.
- A tiger moves along a line to the next empty point, in any direction, or captures.
# Capturing
Only the tigers capture: a tiger jumps along a line over a goat next to it onto the empty point just beyond, and takes it. One goat per turn: a tiger never jumps twice in a row, and never over another tiger. Capturing is not compulsory.
# The end of the game
- The tigers win when they have taken five goats.
- The goats win when no tiger can move or jump.
- A player who cannot move on their turn loses.
- The game is a draw when the same position comes back for the third time with the same player to move, or after fifty moves each without a capture once all the goats are in.
