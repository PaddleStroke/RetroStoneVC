Baker's Dozen
# The aim
Move all 52 cards to the four foundations, one for each suit, built up from the ace to the king. A game for one player.
# The layout
- Thirteen columns of four cards, all face up.
- At the deal, each column's kings go to its bottom.
# Moving cards
- Only the top card of a column moves, one card at a time.
- Put it on a card one rank higher, whatever the suit: a 6 on any 7. Or move it up to its foundation.
- An empty column stays empty.
- A card on a foundation stays there.
# The end of the game
You win when all the cards are on the foundations. Once the cards left can all go up in turn, they go up by themselves.
# Levels and stars
150 levels in six chapters, each a deal that can be won; the die tab deals at random. No hint and no undo: 3 stars; up to two in all: 2 stars; otherwise 1.
