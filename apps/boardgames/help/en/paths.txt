Paths
# The aim
Pairs of dots of one colour lie on the grid. Join the two dots of each pair with a path that goes from square to square: up, down, left or right. Paths may not cross nor share a square, and together they must fill the whole grid. Each pair also has its own mark (a dot, a ring, a square, a star...), so no colour has to be told by eye alone. Choose the size first: 5 × 5, 7 × 7, 9 × 9 or 12 × 12.
Every puzzle has exactly one solution, found by reasoning alone.
# A move
Draw or change one path.
# The end of the game
When every pair is joined and no square is left empty, the puzzle is solved: a gold light runs along every path.
# Levels and stars
There are 150 levels in six chapters, from 5 × 5 up to 12 × 12, each with one solution; choose one on the level screen. The die tab after the chapters makes a new puzzle at random, with the choice described above. No hint and no mistake: 3 stars; up to two hints and mistakes together: 2 stars; otherwise 1. A mistake is a stroke that lays a path on a square that belongs to another colour in the solution; undo does not take it back.
# Playing on the device
- Drag from a dot: its path starts again from it and follows your finger. Drag from a square of a path to go on from there (the rest of that path goes).
- Going back along a path shortens it. Crossing another path cuts it; it comes back if you back off before lifting your finger. A path never passes over a dot of another colour.
- When a path reaches its other dot, the pair is joined and its dots ring.
- With taps alone: tap a dot or a square of a path (its end is marked), then a square in the same row or column: the path runs straight there. Tap the marked end again to let it go.
- The lamp at the right end of the bar gives a hint: it lays one pair's path as in the solution, in gold light.
The squares a path covers are tinted in its colour. Undo takes back one stroke or hint at a time, all the way to the start. The bar shows your moves.
