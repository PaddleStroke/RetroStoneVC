Paths
# The aim
Pairs of dots of one colour lie on the grid. Join the two dots of each pair with a path that goes from square to square: up, down, left or right. Paths may not cross nor share a square, and together they must fill the whole grid.
Every puzzle has exactly one solution, found by reasoning alone.
# A move
Drag from a dot to draw its path, or from a square of a path to go on from there. Crossing another path cuts it.
# The end of the game
When every pair is joined and no square is left empty, the puzzle is solved.
# Levels and stars
No hint and no mistake: 3 stars; up to two hints and mistakes together: 2 stars; otherwise 1. A mistake is a stroke that lays a path on a square that belongs to another colour in the solution; undo does not take it back.
