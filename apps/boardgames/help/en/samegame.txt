Same Game
# The aim
A tray is full of coloured tiles. Take away groups of tiles to score as many points as you can, and clear the whole tray if you can. Choose the size first: 10 × 10 tiles in 3 colours, 15 × 10 in 4, or 20 × 12 in 5.
Every tray can be cleared: each one is built backwards from an empty tray. Finding the way is up to you.
# A move
A group is two or more tiles of the same colour joined side by side (not corner to corner). Take a group away: the tiles above fall into the gaps, and when a column is empty, the columns on its right close up to the left. A single tile can never be taken.
# The score
A group of n tiles scores (n - 2) × (n - 2): 2 tiles score 0, 3 score 1, 4 score 4, 5 score 9, 10 score 64. Big groups pay far more, so let tiles of one colour gather before you take them. Clearing the whole tray earns 1000 points more.
# The end of the game
The game ends when no group is left. A cleared tray lights up in gold. Either way, your final score is shown on a brass plaque.
# Playing on the device
Tap a group: it gets a gold rim, the other tiles dim, and its points show above it. Tap it again to take it away, or tap another group instead. Each colour also carries a small sign. The lamp in the bar gives a hint: it picks a group to take, blinking in gold (while the tray can still be cleared, it shows a way to clear it); tap it to take it. Undo takes back one move at a time, all the way to the start. The bar shows your score.
