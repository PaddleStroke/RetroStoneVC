Same Game
# The aim
A tray is full of coloured tiles. Take away groups of tiles to score as many points as you can, and clear the whole tray if you can. Every tray can be cleared.
# A move
A group is two or more tiles of the same colour joined side by side (not corner to corner). Tap a group, then tap it again to take it away: the tiles above fall into the gaps, and when a column is empty, the columns on its right close up to the left.
The hint follows a way to clear the tray when it knows one from here; otherwise it picks the group that scores most, and the tray may no longer clear.
# The score
A group of n tiles scores (n - 2) × (n - 2): 2 tiles score 0, 3 score 1, 4 score 4, 10 score 64. Clearing the whole tray earns 1000 points more.
# The end of the game
The game ends when no group is left.
# Levels and stars
Each tray has a goal: nine tenths of the best score the computer found for it. Reach the goal: 3 stars; half of it, or a tray nearly cleared (a tenth of the tiles left at most): 2 stars; less: 1 star.
