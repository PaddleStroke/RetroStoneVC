Logic Gates
# The aim
Each wire carries a signal from the switches to the lamps: 0 (dark) or 1 (glowing gold). Set the switches so that every lamp lights up, while any red alarm lamp stays dark.
# The gates
Each gate turns its inputs into one output; its name is engraved on it. A small ring on a gate turns its answer round.
- NOT: the opposite of its input.
- AND: 1 only when all its inputs are 1.
- OR: 1 when any input is 1.
- XOR: 1 when its two inputs differ; with three inputs, when an odd number of them are 1.
- NAND: the opposite of AND.
- NOR: the opposite of OR.
# Route switches
Where the wire forks, the signal goes on along the way the brass track points; the other way carries 0. Tap it to send the signal the other way.
# Cuttable wires
Tap a jumper cable to cut it: beyond the cut the wire carries 0. Only undo or starting the level again mends it. Some levels allow fewer cuts than they have cables.
# Locked switches
A switch with a brass bar screwed across it stays as it is.
# Levels and stars
A move is one flip or one cut. Solved at par, the fewest moves that solve it: 3 stars; up to two moves more: 2 stars; otherwise 1. A hint keeps the level to 2 stars.
