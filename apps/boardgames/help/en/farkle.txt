Farkle
# The aim
Be the first to bank 10,000 points with six dice, pushing your luck. Two to six players, or one alone against the clock.
# A turn
- Throw the six dice. Set aside at least one scoring die, then bank your turn's points or throw the dice left for more.
- Every die set aside must score; combinations count only within one throw.
- A throw with no scoring dice is a farkle: you lose your turn's points.
- Hot dice: when all six have scored, throw all six again and go on.
- Your first bank must be at least 500 points.
# The scoring
- Each 1: 100. Each 5: 50.
- Three of a kind: the face times 100; three 1s: 1,000.
- Four of a kind: 1,000. Five: 2,000. Six: 3,000.
- A straight 1-2-3-4-5-6: 1,500.
- Three pairs: 1,500 (four of a kind and a pair count as three pairs).
- Two triplets: 2,500.
# The end of the game
When a player banks 10,000 or more, every other player has one last turn; then the highest score wins, and equal highest scores share the win. Alone, reach 10,000 within 20 turns.
