Rikiki
# The aim
Say exactly how many tricks you will take, then take exactly that many.
# The cards
52 cards, ace high. The first deal gives 1 card each, the next 2, and so on up to the most (13; 10 with 5 players; 8 with 6), then back down to 1. After each deal the next card is turned face up: its suit is trumps. When no card is left, there are no trumps.
# Bidding
The player after the dealer bids first, the dealer last: from 0 to the number of cards in hand. The dealer may not make the bids add up to the number of cards each holds: somebody must fail.
# Playing
The player after the dealer leads. Follow suit if you can; otherwise play any card (you need not trump). The highest trump wins the trick, or else the highest card of the suit led; its winner leads the next.
# Scoring
- The bid made exactly: 10 points and 1 more for each trick taken.
- Missed: minus 1 for each trick too many or too few.
After the last deal of one card, the highest total wins; tied players share the win.
