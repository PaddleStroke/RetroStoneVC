Mahjong Solitaire
# The aim
The tiles of a Mahjong set lie stacked in a layout. Take them all away, in matching pairs. Choose the layout first:
- Turtle: the classic one, all 144 tiles in five layers. On a phone its tiles are small.
- Pyramid: 80 tiles in three layers, larger tiles, a shorter game.
Every deal can be cleared.
# The tiles
- Three suits, numbered 1 to 9: dots (circles), bamboo (sticks; the 1 is a bird) and characters (a Chinese numeral above 萬, ten thousand).
- The four winds: 東 east, 南 south, 西 west, 北 north.
- The three dragons: 中 red, 發 green, and the white one, a blue frame.
- Four flowers and four seasons, each with its number in a corner.
Each tile of the suits, winds and dragons comes four times; each flower and each season once.
# A move
Take away two matching free tiles. A tile is free when no tile lies on it, even partly, and its left side or its right side is open (no tile right beside it on its layer). Two tiles match when they are the same; any flower matches any flower, and any season any season.
# The end of the game
You win when every tile is gone. If no pair of free tiles is left, you can shuffle: the tiles left are dealt again in the same places, so that they can still all be cleared. If even that cannot help (a tile stuck under another one), the game is over.
# Playing on the device
Tap a free tile, then its match: both lift away. Tap the chosen tile again to let it go. The tiles that are not free are dimmed, and a tap just beside a free tile picks it too. The lamp in the bar shows a pair. When no pair is left, the shuffle button appears in the bar and in the middle of the board. Undo takes back a pair or a shuffle. The bar shows how many tiles are left.
