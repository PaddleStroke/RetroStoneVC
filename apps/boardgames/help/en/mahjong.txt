Mahjong Solitaire
# The aim
Take all the tiles away from the layout, in matching pairs. Every deal can be cleared.
# The tiles
- Three suits, numbered 1 to 9: dots, bamboo (the 1 is a bird) and characters (a Chinese numeral above 萬).
- The four winds 東 east, 南 south, 西 west, 北 north, and the three dragons: 中 red, 發 green, and white, a blue frame.
- Four flowers and four seasons, each with its number in a corner.
# A move
Take away two matching free tiles. A tile is free when no tile lies on it, even partly, and its left or right side is open. Two tiles match when they are the same; any flower matches any flower, and any season any season.
# The end of the game
You win when every tile is gone. If no pair is left, you can shuffle: the tiles left are dealt again in the same places. If even that cannot help, the game is over.
