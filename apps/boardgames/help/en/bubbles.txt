Bubble Pop
# The aim
Marbles hang from the ceiling of a wooden box. Shoot marbles from the launcher to clear the box or, where gems are caught in it, to free every gem, before your shots run out.
# A move
A marble flies straight, bouncing off the side walls, until it touches another marble or the ceiling, and sticks there.
- Three or more of one colour touching pop.
- Whatever no longer hangs from the ceiling falls.
- Tap the next marble to swap it with the one in the launcher.
- Stones and gems never pop: they only fall.
- A caged marble loses its cage when its group pops, and pops the next time.
- A bomb blasts the marbles around it. A rainbow marble matches every colour.
- In some boxes the ceiling comes down a row after every few shots.
# The end of the game
The box cleared, or every gem freed: solved. A marble left past the line, or no shots left: undo, or start again.
# Levels and stars
Solved in the fewest shots possible: 3 stars; with up to half as many shots again: 2 stars; otherwise 1.
