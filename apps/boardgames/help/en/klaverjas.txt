Klaverjas
# The aim
Two teams of two, partners face to face. In each deal one team chooses trumps and must make more than half of all the points. Rotterdam rules (Rotterdams).
# The deal
32 cards, 7 to ace, 8 to each player. The player after the dealer (voorhand) speaks first and leads the first trick.
# Trumps
- From voorhand on, each player names a trump suit or passes. The first to name one plays: his team must make the game.
- If all four pass, voorhand must name a suit (verplicht).
# The cards' order
- Trumps: jack 20, nine 14, ace 11, ten 10, king 4, queen 3, eight, seven.
- The other suits: ace 11, ten 10, king 4, queen 3, jack 2, nine, eight, seven.
- The last trick is worth 10 more: 162 points in all.
# Playing
- Follow suit if you can. If you cannot, you must trump, even when your partner is winning, and go over the highest trump in the trick if you can.
- If you cannot go over it, play another suit; undertrump only when you hold nothing but lower trumps.
- When trumps are led, go over the best trump in the trick if you can.
- The highest trump wins, otherwise the highest card of the suit led. The winner leads next.
# Roem
Bonus points in each trick, for the team that takes it:
- three in sequence in one suit 20, four 50 (the order 7 8 9 10 J Q K A);
- the king and queen of trumps (stuk) 20, also with a sequence;
- four aces, kings, queens or tens 100; four jacks 200;
- pit (all eight tricks): 100 more.
# Scoring
- The playing team must make more than half of all the points, roem included; then each team scores its own.
- If not, it is nat (wet): the other team scores everything, 162 and all the roem.
# The end of the game
After the 16th deal, the higher total wins; equal totals share the win.
