Chess Puzzles
# The aim
Find the winning moves in positions taken from real games. Each puzzle has one line of play that wins: a mate, a piece won, a pawn that promotes. Choose a pack: beginner (rated under 1000), easy (1000 to 1400), medium (1400 to 1800), hard (1800 to 2200), expert (2200 and up), or the mates in one, two or three moves, of every level.
# A move
The pieces move as in chess. Your opponent plays the first move by itself; then it is your turn: find the best move. After each of your right moves the opponent replies by itself, until the line ends. Your pawns always promote to a queen.
# The end of the game
The puzzle is solved when the whole line has been played. A move that gives checkmate is always right, even when it is not the one expected. A wrong move never loses: it turns red, is taken back, and counts as a mistake.
# Playing on the device
The board turns so that your pieces are at the bottom. Tap a piece and then a square, or drag the piece there. The lamp in the bar: a first press lights up the piece to move, a second its square too. Undo takes back your last move and the reply, all the way to the start. The bar shows the puzzle's rating, your streak (puzzles solved in a row with no mistake and no hint) and the puzzles solved this session. Once a puzzle is solved, tap the board for the next one in the pack.
