Chess Puzzles
# The aim
Find the winning moves in positions taken from real games. Each puzzle has one line of play that wins: a mate, a piece won, a pawn that promotes.
# A move
The pieces move as in chess. Your opponent plays the first move by itself; then it is your turn: find the best move. After each of your right moves the opponent replies by itself, until the line ends. Your pawns always promote to a queen.
# The end of the game
The puzzle is solved when the whole line has been played. A move that gives checkmate is always right, even when it is not the one expected. A wrong move never loses: it is taken back and counts as a mistake.
# Levels and stars
No hint and no mistake: 3 stars; one hint or one mistake: 2 stars; otherwise 1.
