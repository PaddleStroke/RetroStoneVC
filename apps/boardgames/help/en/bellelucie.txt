La Belle Lucie
# The aim
Move all 52 cards to the four foundations, one for each suit, built up from the ace to the king. A game for one player, also called the Fan.
# The layout
- The cards are dealt face up in 18 fans of three; the last fan has a single card.
- At the top: the hand (for the redeals), the place of the merci, and the four foundations.
# Moving cards
- Only the top card of a fan can move: onto its foundation, or onto the top card of another fan of the same suit and one rank higher: the 6 of hearts on the 7 of hearts.
- A fan left empty stays empty.
# The redeals
- Twice in a game, tap the hand (the place with the arrow going round, at the top left): the cards still in the fans are gathered, shuffled and dealt again in fans of three. The number in the hand shows the redeals left.
- On the last deal comes the merci, the gift of the classic rules: once, tap a card buried in a fan to draw it out. It waits on its own place until you play it onto a foundation or a fan.
# The end of the game
You win when all the cards are on the foundations; once they can all go up in turn, they go up by themselves. The game is over when no move is left. Not every deal can be won.
# Playing on the device
Tap a top card to send it to its foundation or its fan, or drag it there. A quick second tap sends a card only to a foundation. The lamp in your bar shows a useful move; the number next to your cards counts your moves. Undo takes back moves, all the way to the deal.
