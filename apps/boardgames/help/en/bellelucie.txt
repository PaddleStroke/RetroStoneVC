La Belle Lucie
# The aim
Move all 52 cards to the four foundations, one for each suit, built up from the ace to the king.
# The layout
- The cards are dealt face up in 18 fans of three; the last fan has a single card.
# Moving cards
- Only the top card of a fan can move: onto its foundation, or onto the top card of another fan of the same suit and one rank higher: the 6 of hearts on the 7 of hearts.
- A fan left empty stays empty.
# The redeals
- Twice in a game, tap the hand (top left): the cards still in the fans are gathered, shuffled and dealt again in fans of three.
- On the last deal comes the merci: once, tap a card buried in a fan to draw it out. It waits on its own place until you play it onto a foundation or a fan.
# The end of the game
You win when all the cards are on the foundations. The game is over when no move is left. Not every deal can be won.
