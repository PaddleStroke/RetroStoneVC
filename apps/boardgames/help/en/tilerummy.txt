Tile Rummy
# The aim
Be the first to lay all the tiles of your rack on the table, in runs and groups.
# The tiles
106 tiles: the numbers 1 to 13 in four colours, each twice, and two jokers. 14 tiles each; the others stay face down in the pool.
# Sets
- A run: three or more numbers in a row, all of one colour. 1 does not follow 13.
- A group: three or four tiles of the same number, all of different colours.
- A joker stands for any tile.
# A turn
- Lay tiles from your rack: new sets, or tiles added to the sets on the table.
- Rearrange the table as you like (two quick taps on a tile pick up its whole set). When your turn ends, every set must be valid, and no table tile may go back to a rack.
- Win a joker back by putting the tile it stands for in its place, then use the joker in another set in the same turn.
- If you cannot or will not lay anything, draw a tile from the pool; once it is empty, pass.
# The first meld
Your first meld must be worth 30 points or more, from your own tiles alone in one turn; a joker is worth the tile it stands for. Until then, you may not touch the sets on the table.
# The end of a round
The first player to empty their rack scores the tiles left in the other racks: each number its value, a joker 30. The others lose what is left in their own racks. If the pool is empty and everyone passes in turn, the lowest rack wins: each other player loses the difference, and the winner gains it.
# The end of the game
One round: its winner wins. A match: as many rounds as players, each starting one round; the highest total wins.
