Hex Stacks
# The aim
Put stacks of coloured hex tiles on the honeycomb and gather the colours. Reach the level's goal: clear a number of tiles, or a number of tiles of one colour.
# The rules
- Drag a stack from the tray onto an empty cell. When the tray is empty, three more stacks come.
- Neighbouring stacks with the same colour on top come together: their top tiles move onto the purest of them, the one with the fewest other tiles underneath. What they uncover may match in turn.
- Ten or more tiles of one colour on top of a stack clear.
- Stones stay empty. A locked cell opens once as many more tiles as its number have been cleared.
# The end of the game
The level is solved when its goal is reached, and lost if the board fills up first. Endless play goes on until the board is full: each tile cleared scores a point.
# Levels and stars
The goal reached with no more stacks than the computer needed: 3 stars; with up to half as many again: 2 stars; otherwise 1.
