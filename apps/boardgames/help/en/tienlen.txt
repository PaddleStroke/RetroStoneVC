# The aim
Be the first to play all your cards; the others play on for the places. 13 cards each; with fewer than four players, the rest stay out of play.
# The cards
- From low to high: 3 to 10, jack, queen, king, ace, and the 2, the highest of all.
- Equal ranks go by suit, low to high: spades, clubs, diamonds, hearts. The 2 of hearts is the highest card.
# The plays
- A single card, a pair, three or four of a kind.
- A sequence: three or more ranks in a row, of any suits, never with a 2.
- A double sequence: three or more pairs in a row, such as 3-3-4-4-5-5, without 2s.
- A play is beaten by one of the same kind and size with a higher highest card.
# A trick
- The holder of the lowest card dealt (the 3 of spades with four players) leads the first trick with a play that includes it.
- In turn, beat the last play or pass. A player who passes is out of the trick until a new one starts.
- When all the others have passed, the last player who played starts a new trick with any play.
# Bombs
Some plays beat 2s:
- three pairs in a row beat a single 2;
- four of a kind beats a single 2, or three pairs in a row;
- four pairs in a row beat a single 2, a pair of 2s, four of a kind, or three pairs in a row;
- five pairs in a row also beat three 2s.
A bomb also beats a lower one of its own kind.
