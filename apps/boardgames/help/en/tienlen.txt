# The aim
Be the first to play all your cards; the others play on for the places. A Vietnamese game (its name means "go forward") for 2 to 4 players, with a pack of 52 cards: 13 each (with fewer than four players, the rest stay out of play).
# The cards
- From low to high: 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, jack, queen, king, ace, and the 2, the highest of all.
- The suits, from low to high: spades, clubs, diamonds, hearts. Two cards of the same rank go by their suit: the 2 of hearts is the highest card.
# The plays
- A single card, a pair, three or four of a kind.
- A sequence: three or more cards of ranks in a row, of any suits. The 2 is never part of one.
- A double sequence: three or more pairs of ranks in a row, such as 3-3-4-4-5-5, without 2s.
- A play is beaten by a play of the same kind with as many cards and a higher highest card.
# A trick
- The player with the lowest card dealt (the 3 of spades with four players) leads the first trick, with a play that includes it.
- In turn, each player beats the last play or passes. A player who passes stays out of the trick until a new one starts.
- When all the others have passed, the last player who played starts a new trick with any play.
# Bombs
Some plays beat 2s, played in turn like any other:
- three pairs in a row beat a single 2;
- four of a kind beats a single 2, or three pairs in a row;
- four pairs in a row beat a single 2, a pair of 2s, four of a kind, or three pairs in a row;
- five pairs in a row also beat three 2s.
A bomb also beats a lower one of its own kind.
# The end
The first player out of cards wins. The others play on: the order in which they go out gives their places, and the last one still holding cards comes last.
# Playing on the device
Pass the device round: each player sees only their own cards, at the bottom, from low to high. Along the top sit the others: their cards face down, how many, and a pass sign while they are out of the trick; once out of cards, their place. The last play lies big in the middle with its player's chip, the plays before it smaller behind. Tap cards to choose them, then tap the middle or drag them there: the middle lights up when your choice can be played. The lowest card glows for the player who must lead it. The pass button is in your bar. At the end, a table shows the places.
