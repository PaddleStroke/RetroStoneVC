Heyawake
# The aim
Shade some cells of the grid, which is divided into rooms.
- A number says how many shaded cells its room has. A room without a number may have any.
- Shaded cells never touch side by side.
- The unshaded cells are all joined in one piece, side by side.
- A straight line of unshaded cells never crosses more than one room wall.
Every puzzle has exactly one solution, found by reasoning alone. Hold a cell to put a dot in it: a note that it stays unshaded.
# The end of the game
The puzzle is solved when every rule is kept: a wave of gold light runs over the grid.
# Levels and stars
No hint and no mistake: 3 stars; up to two hints and mistakes together: 2 stars; otherwise 1. A mistake is a wrong cell that you take back with undo.
