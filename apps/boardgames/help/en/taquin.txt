15 Puzzle
# The aim
Numbered tiles lie mixed up in a tray with one place empty. Slide them back into order: 1, 2, 3... row by row from the top left, the empty place last, at the bottom right. On a picture, each tile's number is small in its corner.
Every shuffle can be solved.
# A move
Tap a tile in the same row or column as the empty place: it slides, and the tiles between it and the empty place move with it.
# The end of the game
When every tile is in its place, the puzzle is solved.
# Levels and stars
Each puzzle has a par: the fewest tiles moved, found by the computer (on a large random tray, the best way it finds in a moment). A slide of three tiles counts three. Solved at par: 3 stars; with up to half as many tiles moved again: 2 stars; otherwise 1.
