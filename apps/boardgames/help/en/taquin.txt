15 Puzzle
# The aim
Numbered tiles lie mixed up in a tray with one place empty. Slide them back into order: 1, 2, 3... row by row from the top left, the empty place last, at the bottom right. Choose the tray first: 3 × 3 (8 tiles), 4 × 4 (the classic 15 tiles) or 5 × 5 (24 tiles), with numbers or cut from a picture (a chess game in progress, each tile's number small in its corner).
Every shuffle can be solved. Half of all the ways to mix the tiles could never be slid back into order, and those are never dealt.
# A move
A tile next to the empty place slides into it. Tiles only slide; they are never lifted out.
# The end of the game
When every tile is in its place, the puzzle is solved: a gold light runs over the tiles in their order, and a picture gets its missing piece.
# Playing on the device
Tap a tile in the same row or column as the empty place: it slides, and the tiles between it and the empty place move with it. You can also push a tile towards the empty place with your finger: past halfway it goes on, otherwise it falls back. Undo takes back one slide at a time, all the way to the start. The bar counts the tiles moved: a slide of three tiles counts three.
