Pig
# The aim
Be the first to reach 100 points. Roll as long as you dare, but a 1 wipes out your turn. For 2 to 6 players.
# A turn
- Roll as often as you like; each roll adds to your turn's total.
- A 1 loses the turn's total and ends your turn.
- Or hold: the turn's total goes to your score. Roll at least once first.
# Two-dice pig
The same with two dice, adding their sum.
- A single 1 loses the turn's total and ends the turn.
- Two 1s also lose your whole score: back to 0.
# The end of the game
The first player to hold with 100 points or more wins at once.
