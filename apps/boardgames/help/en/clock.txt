Clock
# The aim
Turn every card face up before the fourth king comes out. A little game of pure chance for one player: the cards play themselves.
# The layout
- The cards are dealt face down in 13 piles of four: twelve around a clock face, one for each hour, and the kings' pile in the middle.
- The ace belongs to one o'clock, the 2 to two o'clock, and so on: the jack to eleven, the queen to twelve.
# Moving cards
- Turn the top card of the middle pile and put it face up under the pile of its hour; a king goes under the middle pile.
- Then turn the top card of the pile you have just put a card under, and go on.
# The end of the game
You win if all the cards are face up when the fourth king turns up. If the fourth king comes while cards are still face down, the game is lost.
