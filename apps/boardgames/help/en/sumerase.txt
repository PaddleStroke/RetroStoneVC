Sum Erase
# The aim
Each row has a target at its end, and each column one under it. Erase digits so that the digits left in every row and every column add up to its target. Every puzzle has exactly one solution, found by reasoning alone. Hold a digit to ring it: a note that it stays.
# The end of the game
The puzzle is solved when every row and every column makes its target.
# Levels and stars
No hint and no mistake: 3 stars; up to two hints and mistakes together: 2 stars; otherwise 1. A mistake is a wrong cell that you take back with undo.
