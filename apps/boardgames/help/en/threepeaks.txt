Three Peaks
# The aim
Clear the three peaks by playing their cards onto the waste, one rank up or down. A game for one player.
# The layout
- 28 cards in three peaks side by side, in rows of 3, 6, 9 and 10 cards, each row lying over half of the row above. Only the bottom row is face up.
- One card face up on the waste; the other 23 cards are the stock, face down, next to it.
# Moving cards
- A card is free when no card lies over it any more. A free card goes onto the waste if it is one rank higher or lower than the top card of the waste, whatever its suit: a 7 or a 9 on an 8.
- Round the corner (the classic rule): an ace and a king follow each other too. The other choice: they don't.
- A face-down card turns face up as soon as it is free.
- The streak: the number next to the waste counts the cards you have played from the peaks in a row. It starts again when you turn a card from the stock.
# The stock
When no card can go, tap the stock to turn its top card onto the waste. The stock is gone through only once.
# The end of the game
You win when the three peaks are cleared. The game is lost when the stock is empty and no card can go. The deals are shuffled at random, so not every deal can be won.
# Levels and stars
There are 150 levels in six chapters, from the easiest deal to the hardest, each one a deal that can be won; choose one on the level screen. In chapters 1 to 4 a king and an ace follow each other, in chapters 5 and 6 they do not. The die tab after the chapters deals a new game at random, with the choice described above. No hint and no undo: 3 stars; up to two hints and undos together: 2 stars; otherwise 1.
# Playing on the device
Tap a free card to play it onto the waste, or drag it there. The lamp shows a good move: the card that starts the longest run it can see. The number next to your cards counts your moves. Undo takes back moves, all the way to the deal.
