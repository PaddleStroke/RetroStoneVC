Draw and Guess
# The aim
Draw so that the others guess your word, and guess theirs. For 2 to 8 players.
# A turn
- The drawer looks at a word alone, then draws it for 60 seconds while the others call out guesses.
- Only the drawing: no letters, no numbers, no words, no gestures.
- When someone says the word, the drawer taps that player.
# Points
- The first right guess: 2 points for the guesser, 1 for the drawer.
- Nobody before the time is up: no points.
# The end of the game
Each player draws 3 times (twice from 4 players). The most points win.
