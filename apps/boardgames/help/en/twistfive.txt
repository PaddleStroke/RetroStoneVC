Twist Five
# The aim
Be the first to line up five marbles of your colour: across, up and down, or diagonally. Light plays first.
# A turn
- Place one marble in any empty hole.
- Then turn one of the four quarters of the board a quarter turn, either way. Any quarter may be turned, even an empty one, and its marbles turn with it.
# The end of the game
- Five in a row wins, made by the marble or by the turn. With five after placing the marble, you win at once, without turning.
- If a turn makes five for both players at once, the game is a draw.
- If the board is full and nobody has five, the game is a draw.
