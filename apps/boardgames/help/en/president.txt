President
# The aim
Get rid of your cards before the others. The first one out becomes the Président, the last one the Trou, and the ranks change from deal to deal. After five deals, the highest total wins.
# The cards
From low to high: 3 to 10, jack, queen, king, ace, and the 2, the highest of all. Suits do not matter.
# A trick
- The leader plays one card, or two, three or four cards of the same rank.
- In turn, the others play as many cards of a higher rank, or pass; a player who passed may play again when their turn comes back.
- When all the others have passed in a row, the last player who played leads the next trick (if they are out, the next player who still has cards).
- A 2 ends the trick at once: its player leads again.
# Going out
The order in which the players go out gives the ranks for the next deal: Président, Vice-président (four players or more), Neutre in the middle, Vice-trou (four players or more), Trou.
# The exchange
From the second deal on, the Trou leads. The Trou gives their two best cards to the Président, and the Vice-trou their best card to the Vice-président. Then the Président gives any two cards back, and the Vice-président any one.
# The score
In each deal the first player out scores one point less than the number of players, the next one less again, and so on down to 0 for the last. Tied players share the win.
