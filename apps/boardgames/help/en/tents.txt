Tents
# The aim
Pitch a tent next to every tree: just above, below, left or right of it. Each tree has its own tent and each tent its own tree. Tents never touch one another, not even at a corner. The numbers left of the rows and above the columns tell how many tents they hold.
Every field has exactly one solution, found by reasoning alone.
# A move
Tap a cell to pitch a tent, again to take it down. Hold a cell to put grass in it: a note that no tent goes there.
# The end of the game
The field is solved when every tree has its tent, no tents touch and every number is right.
# Levels and stars
No hint and no mistake: 3 stars; up to two hints and mistakes together: 2 stars; otherwise 1. A mistake is a wrong tent or grass that you take back with undo.
