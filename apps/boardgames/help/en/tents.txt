Tents
# The aim
Pitch a tent next to every tree: just above, below, left or right of it. Each tree has its own tent and each tent its own tree. Tents never touch one another, not even at a corner. The numbers left of the rows and above the columns tell how many tents they hold.
Every field has exactly one solution, found by reasoning alone. Choose the size (from 6 × 6 to 12 × 12) and the level (the stones under each picture):
- Easy: one look is always enough: no tent away from the trees or round a tent; a line with all its tents, or with just enough room for them; a tree with one place left.
- Medium: you will also need the stretches of free cells that just fit a line's tents, and to look one tent ahead: a tent here would leave a tree or a line without room.
- Hard: longer chains of reasoning, never a guess.
# A move
Pitch a tent in a cell, or take it down. You can also put grass in a cell to remember that no tent goes there; the grass is only a note for you.
# The end of the game
The field is solved when every tree has its tent, no tents touch and every number is right: a wave of gold light runs over the field.
# Levels and stars
There are 150 levels in six chapters, from 5 × 5 up to 12 × 12, each with one solution; choose one on the level screen. The die tab after the chapters makes a new puzzle at random, with the choice described above. No hint and no mistake: 3 stars; up to two hints and mistakes together: 2 stars; otherwise 1. A mistake is a wrong tent or grass that you take back with undo.
# Playing on the device
- Tap a cell to pitch a tent; tap it again to take it down.
- Hold a cell a moment to put grass in it, or take the grass out.
- Drag along a row or a column to put grass in a whole stretch (or take it out of a stretch of grass).
Tents that touch glow red, and so does a tree with no room left for its tent. A number turns red when its line has too many tents, and fades once it has all of them. The buttons beside the board:
- Tent or grass: what a tap puts. Tap it to swap tap and hold.
- Light bulb: a hint. It puts a wrong cell right, or else shows the next cell reasoning finds, in gold light.
Undo takes back one action at a time, all the way to the start. The bar shows your moves.
