Forty Thieves
# The aim
Move all 104 cards of two packs to the eight foundations, two for each suit, built up from the ace to the king.
# The layout
- Ten columns of four cards, all face up.
- The other 64 cards are the stock.
# Moving cards
- On a column, put a card one rank lower of the same suit: the 6 of clubs on the 7 of clubs.
- Only one card moves at a time, never a run.
- Any card may fill an empty column.
- A card on a foundation stays there.
# The stock
Tap the stock to turn one card onto the waste; the top card of the waste can be played. The stock is gone through only once.
# The end of the game
You win when all the cards are on the foundations. Few deals can be won.
