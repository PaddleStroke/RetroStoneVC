Big Two
# The aim
Be the first to play all your cards. With 3 players, the last card goes to the player holding the 3 of diamonds.
# The cards
- Low to high: 3 to 10, jack, queen, king, ace, 2.
- Equal ranks go by suit, low to high: diamonds, clubs, hearts, spades.
# The plays
- A single card, a pair, three of a kind.
- Five cards, low to high: a straight (3-4-5-6-7 up to 10-J-Q-K-A, never with a 2), a flush, a full house, four of a kind plus any card, a straight flush.
- The same kind goes by its highest card (flushes: then the next ones); a full house or four of a kind by its three or four cards.
# A trick
- The holder of the 3 of diamonds leads the first trick with a play that includes it.
- In turn, beat the last play with one of the same kind and size, or pass; you may still play later.
- When all the others pass in a row, the last player who played leads any play.
# The score
The first out of cards wins. The others score a penalty point per card left, doubled with ten or more.
