Fillomino
# The aim
Write a number from 1 to 9 in every cell.
- Cells with the same number that touch side by side form a region, and each region has exactly as many cells as its number.
- So two regions of the same size never touch side by side.
Some numbers are given. Every puzzle has exactly one solution, found by reasoning alone.
# The end of the game
The puzzle is solved when every region has the right size: a wave of gold light runs over the grid.
# Levels and stars
No hint and no mistake: 3 stars; up to two hints and mistakes together: 2 stars; otherwise 1. A mistake is a wrong number that you take back with undo.
