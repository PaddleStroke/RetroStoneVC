Clear the Arrows
# The aim
Clear the board: every tile must leave it.
# A move
Tap a tile: it slides off the board the way its arrow points, if nothing stands between its head and the edge. A long tile moves whole, its arrow at its head.
If another tile is in the way, the tapped tile bumps into it and stays where it was: that is a mistake.
A tile on a brass disc that cannot go turns a quarter turn clockwise instead, with no mistake.
# The end of the game
When the last tile has left, the board is clear.
# Levels and stars
No hint and no mistake: 3 stars; up to two hints and mistakes together: 2 stars; otherwise 1.
