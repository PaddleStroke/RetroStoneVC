Clear the Arrows
# The aim
Clear the board: every arrow must leave it.
# A move
Tap an arrow: if every square from its head straight to the edge is free, it slides out the way its head points, its body following the head's path like a snake.
If another arrow is in the way, the tapped arrow bumps into it and stays where it was: that is a mistake.
# The end of the game
When the last arrow has left, the board is clear.
# Levels and stars
No hint and no mistake: 3 stars; up to two hints and mistakes together: 2 stars; otherwise 1.
