Mines
# The aim
Mines are hidden under some of the wooden tiles of the field. Lift every tile that has no mine under it.
The levels and the fields without a die can be cleared by reasoning alone. A field with a die is drawn by pure chance: now and then only a guess will do.
# A move
Lift a tile. Under it you find a mine, or a number: how many of its eight neighbours hide a mine. A tile with no mine around it lifts its neighbours by itself. Your first tile is never a mine.
Hold a tile to plant a flag on it, a note for you that also keeps it from being lifted. Tap a number with all its flags around it to lift its other neighbours at once.
# The end of the game
The field is cleared when every tile without a mine is lifted. A mine lifted glows red: undo takes it back, and you go on.
# Levels and stars
No hint and no mistake: 3 stars; up to two hints and mistakes together: 2 stars; otherwise 1. A mistake is a mine lifted (even taken back) or a wrong flag the lamp has to pull up.
