Shut the Box
# The aim
Shut as many of your numbered tiles as you can: your score is the sum of the tiles left standing, and lower is better. Shut them all and you win at once. For 1 to 4 players.
# A turn
- Throw the two dice, then shut any standing tiles that add up to the throw: one tile or several.
- Throw again and shut again, as long as you can.
- Once all your tiles from 7 up are shut, you may throw just one die.
- When no tiles make the throw, your turn is over.
# The end of the game
Each player plays one full turn; then the lowest score wins. Equal lowest scores are a draw. Alone, try to shut the box.
