Tracks
# The aim
Lay one railway line across the grid, from the entry on the edge of the board to the exit on another edge.
- The line goes from cell to cell through their sides: each cell it uses holds a straight piece or a curve. It never branches and never crosses itself.
- The number above each column and left of each row says how many of its cells the line uses.
- A few pieces are given, on darker cells.
Every puzzle has exactly one solution, found by reasoning alone.
# A move
Drag from cell to cell to lay the track along your finger. Hold a cell to cross it: a note that the line does not use it.
# The end of the game
When the line is complete and every count is right, the puzzle is solved: a little train runs from the entry to the exit.
# Levels and stars
No hint and no mistake: 3 stars; up to two hints and mistakes together: 2 stars; otherwise 1. A mistake is a piece laid in a cell the line does not use, or a cross on a cell it does use; undo does not take it back.
