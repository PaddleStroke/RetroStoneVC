Truco
# The aim
Argentine Truco, for 2 players, or 4 in two partnerships sitting face to face. Win hands of three cards and bet on them: the truco on the cards, the envido on two cards of a suit. The first side to reach 15 or 30 points, as chosen, wins at once, even in the middle of a hand.
# The cards
The Spanish pack of 40, without 8s and 9s: oros (coins), copas (cups), espadas (swords), bastos (clubs); the sota (10), caballo (11) and rey (12) are a pawn, a knight and a king. From high to low: the 1 of espadas, the 1 of bastos, the 7 of espadas, the 7 of oros, the 3s, the 2s, the 1s of copas and oros, the 12s, 11s and 10s, the 7s of copas and bastos, the 6s, 5s and 4s. Cards of the same strength tie.
# Playing a hand
- 3 cards each. The mano, the player after the dealer, leads the first round.
- Each player plays one card a round; the highest wins the round and its player leads the next.
- A tie between the sides is a parda, and the same player leads again; partners who tie win the round together.
- Two rounds take the hand. After a first-round parda, the next round decides; a parda after a won round goes to the first round's winner; three pardas: the mano's side.
- A hand is worth 1 point.
# Truco
- On your turn, before you play, you may call truco: the hand is worth 2. The other side answers quiero (accept), no quiero (refuse: you take the hand at its worth before the call), or raises: retruco (3), then vale cuatro (4).
- Only the side that accepted the last call may raise it, later, on one of its turns.
- Me voy al mazo: instead of playing, a player gives up the hand; the opponents take it at its current worth.
# Envido
- Only in the first round, before your first card, you may call envido (2), real envido (3) or falta envido. Answering a truco in the first round, you may call envido first (el envido está primero). Once a truco is accepted, no envido can be called.
- The answer: quiero, no quiero, or a higher call: envido once more, then real envido, then falta envido. Accepted, the best envido scores the sum of the calls; refused, the callers score what was accepted before the last call, or 1.
- Falta envido is worth what the leading side lacks to win; in a game to 30 while both sides are under 15 (las malas), the whole game.
- A player's envido: two cards of one suit count 20 plus their numbers (figures 0); without them, the highest number. A tie goes to the player nearer the mano.
# Flor
A variant chosen before the game. Three cards of one suit are a flor: shown at the deal, worth 3 points to its side, and that hand has no envido. If both sides have one, the best (20 plus the three numbers) takes 3 points for each flor. No contraflor.
