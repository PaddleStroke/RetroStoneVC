The Game of the Goose
# The aim
Be the first to bring your pawn to square 63, the goose's garden in the middle of the spiral. For 2 to 6 players.
# A turn
Throw the two dice and move your pawn forward by their sum.
- A first throw of 9 (and again after death): 6 and 3 go straight to 26, 5 and 4 to 53.
- Square 63 needs the exact number. With more, go to 63 and back by the extra points.
# The squares
- A goose: move again by the same throw, the same way.
- The bridge (6): go on to 12.
- The inn (19): miss two turns.
- The well (31) and the prison (52): wait until another pawn comes in and frees you.
- The maze (42): back to 30.
- Death (58): back to the start.
# Meeting another pawn
If you end on another pawn's square, it goes to the square you came from, freed from the inn, well or prison, and you take its place. With two players, if one pawn is in the well and the other in the prison, the next player to throw is freed.
# The end of the game
The first player on square 63 wins.
