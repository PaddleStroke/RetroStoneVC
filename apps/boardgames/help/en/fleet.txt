Hidden Fleet
# The aim
Find the fleet hidden in the sea: straight ships, one to four cells long.
- The number beside a row or above a column tells how many of its cells hold a ship.
- Ships never touch each other, not even at a corner.
- Some cells are shown at the start: water, or a part of a ship (a one-cell ship, an end, a middle).
Every puzzle has exactly one solution, found by reasoning alone. Hold a cell to mark it as water.
# The end of the game
The puzzle is solved when every ship is in place: a wave of gold light runs over the sea.
# Levels and stars
No hint and no mistake: 3 stars; up to two hints and mistakes together: 2 stars; otherwise 1. A mistake is a wrong cell that you take back with undo.
