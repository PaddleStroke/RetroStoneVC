Dominoes
# The aim
Be the first to lay all your bones, matching them to the open ends of the line.
# The deal
7 bones each with two players, 5 with three or four. The rest form the boneyard.
# A turn
- First round: the highest double starts (no double: the heaviest bone). Next rounds: the last winner starts with any bone.
- Lay one bone matching an open end.
- Block game: if you cannot play, pass.
- Draw game and All Fives: if you cannot play, draw until you can; once the boneyard is empty, pass.
# All Fives
- The first double is the spinner: once both its long sides are played on, its short sides open too.
- If the open ends add up to a multiple of 5 after your bone, you score that total. A double at an end counts both halves.
# The end of a round
The round ends when a player goes out or nobody can play. The player who went out, or who holds the fewest pips, scores the pips left in the other hands (All Fives: rounded to the nearest 5); a tie for the fewest scores nothing.
# The end of the game
After the round in which a player reaches 100 points (All Fives: at once), the highest total wins.
