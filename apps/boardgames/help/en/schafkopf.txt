Schafkopf
# The aim
Bavaria's great card game for four, by the Bavarian rules. In each deal the winner of the bidding plays a game, with a partner he calls or alone, against the others. After 12 deals the highest total wins; equal totals share the win.
# The cards
32 cards, 7 to ace, with French suits for the German ones: clubs for acorns (Eichel), spades for leaves (Gras), hearts (Herz), diamonds for bells (Schellen). The queens are the Obers, the jacks the Unters, the aces the Sau. 8 cards each; the player after the dealer speaks first and leads.
# Bidding
- In turn, each player passes (weiter) or offers to play: a Rufspiel, a Wenz or a Solo.
- Rufspiel: he calls the ace of a suit (clubs, spades or diamonds, never hearts) of which he holds a card, but not the ace. Its holder is his partner, unknown until the ace is played.
- Wenz and Solo: he plays alone against the three others.
- Solo beats Wenz, which beats Rufspiel; between equal games, the first to speak. If everyone passes, the cards are thrown in, or a Ramsch is played if chosen.
# Trumps
- Rufspiel: the four Obers (clubs, spades, hearts, diamonds), then the four Unters in the same order, then the hearts: ace, 10, king, 9, 8, 7.
- Solo: the same, with the chosen suit in place of hearts. Wenz: only the four Unters.
- The other suits: ace, 10, king, (Ober), (Unter), 9, 8, 7.
# Playing
- Follow suit if you can (all trumps count as one suit); otherwise play any card. The highest trump wins, otherwise the highest card of the suit led. The winner leads next.
- The called ace must be played the first time its suit is led. Until then its holder may not throw it away, and may lead that suit only with the ace, unless he holds four or more cards of it: then he may run away (davonlaufen) with another one. In the last trick anything goes.
# Winning
- Card points: ace 11, 10 10, king 4, Ober 3, Unter 2: 120 in all. The playing side wins with 61 or more.
- Schneider: the playing side wins with 91 or more, or loses with 30 or fewer. Schwarz: the losing side took no trick.
# The tariff
- Rufspiel 10 points; Solo and Wenz 50; schneider 10 more, schwarz 10 more.
- Laufende: the unbroken run of top trumps from the club Ober down (in the Wenz from the club Unter) held by one side: 10 each, from 3 on (in the Wenz from 2 on).
- Rufspiel: each winner gains the value, each loser loses it. Solo and Wenz: the soloist wins or loses three times the value, each other player once.
- No Kontra, Re, Tout or Sie.
# Ramsch
Each plays for himself, with the Rufspiel's trumps. The player with the most card points pays 10 to each of the others; at equal points, the one with more tricks; if still equal, each of them. Taking every trick (Durchmarsch) wins 50 from each.
