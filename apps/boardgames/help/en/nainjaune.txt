Yellow Dwarf
# The aim
Play your cards in sequences, collect the chips of the board's five compartments, and be the first to empty your hand. The most chips at the end wins.
# The board
Five compartments: the ten of diamonds, the jack of clubs, the queen of spades, the king of hearts, and the seven of diamonds, the yellow dwarf. Everyone starts with 100 chips. Before each deal, each player stakes 1 chip on the ten, 2 on the jack, 3 on the queen, 4 on the king and 5 on the yellow dwarf.
# Playing
The cards rank from the ace (1) up to the king; suits do not count. The cards left over at the deal are not played.
- The player on the dealer's left plays any card, then the next card up, of any suit, for as long as they can.
- Then the next player round the table who has the next card goes on. If you have it, you must play it.
- A king ends the sequence and its player starts a new one. If nobody has the next card, the last player who played starts a new one.
- Whoever plays one of the board's cards takes all the chips in its compartment.
# The end of a deal
The first player out of cards wins the deal: each other player pays them 1 chip per card left in hand. A player still holding a board card also pays its stake again. Chips nobody took stay for the next deal. A player who cannot pay pays what they have.
# Grand opera
In the classic game, a player who goes out before anyone else has played a card also takes the chips of all five compartments.
# The end of the game
The game ends when everyone has dealt twice, or as soon as a player cannot pay the 15 chips of the stakes. Equal players share the win.
