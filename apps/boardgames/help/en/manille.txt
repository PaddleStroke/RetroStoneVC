Manille
# The aim
Two teams of partners sitting face to face. Win the tricks that hold the tens and the aces; the first team to reach 34 points wins.
# The cards
- 32 cards. In each suit the ten, the manille, is highest, then the ace, the manillon, then king, queen, jack, 9, 8, 7.
- Ten 5 points, ace 4, king 3, queen 2, jack 1, and each trick 1 more: 68 in a deal.
- The dealer's last card is turned face up: its suit is trumps.
# Doubling
Before the first card, each player in turn may double: the deal counts twice. The other team may then redouble: four times.
# Playing
- Follow suit if you can. If an opponent holds the trick with that suit, beat it if you can.
- If you cannot follow and an opponent holds the trick, trump, over-trumping if you can; otherwise play any card.
- If your partner holds the trick and you cannot follow, play any card.
- The highest trump wins, or else the highest card of the suit led.
# Scoring
The team with more than 34 in the deal scores the difference, doubled or quadrupled if the deal was. At 34 each, nobody scores.
