Merge
# The aim
Numbered tiles lie on a square grid. Push them all one way: equal tiles that meet merge into one tile of twice the number. Make the target tile, the one two 1024 tiles merge into (two 128 tiles on the small grid), then go on for a higher score if you like.
# A move
Swipe up, down, left or right: every tile slides as far as it can that way. Two tiles of the same number that meet become one, and its number is added to your score. A tile merges only once in a push. After every push, a new tile, a 2 or now and then a 4, appears on an empty square. A push that would move nothing is not allowed.
# The end of the game
The game is over when no push can move anything. Stars: 3 for the target tile or beyond, 2 for a tile half the target, 1 otherwise.
