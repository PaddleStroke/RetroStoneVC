Écarté
# The aim
Be the first to reach 5 points by winning most of the five tricks of each deal.
# The cards
32 cards, ranked king, queen, jack, ace, 10, 9, 8, 7. Each player gets 5 cards and the next card is turned up: its suit is trumps. The player who does not deal is the elder hand.
# The king
- A king turned up scores 1 for the dealer.
- Holding the king of trumps scores 1 before the play.
# The exchange
The elder may propose to change cards, or play at once. If the dealer accepts, the elder changes 1 to 5 cards, then the dealer 0 to 5, and the elder may propose again. If the dealer refuses, the play begins.
# Playing
Follow suit and beat the card led if you can; otherwise trump if you can. The higher card of the suit led wins, or the trump.
# Scoring
- 3 or 4 tricks: 1 point. All 5 tricks (the vole): 2 points.
- Playing without proposing, or refusing the first proposal, then taking fewer than 3 tricks gives the opponent 2.
# The end of the game
The first to reach 5 points wins, even by a king's point.
