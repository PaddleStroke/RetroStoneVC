Fox and Geese
# The aim
Thirteen geese against a single fox. The geese win by hemming the fox in so that it cannot move. The fox wins by taking geese until fewer than nine are left. The geese move first.
# The geese
A goose moves along a line to the next point, if it is empty: forwards, diagonally forwards or sideways, never backwards. Geese never jump and never take the fox.
# The fox
- The fox moves along a line to the next point, if it is empty, in any direction.
- Or it jumps over a goose next to it, along a line, to the empty point just beyond, and takes that goose.
- Jumping is not compulsory. But once the fox has jumped, it must go on jumping as long as it can.
# The end of the game
- The geese win when the fox cannot move or jump.
- The fox wins when fewer than nine geese are left, or when the geese cannot move.
- The game is a draw when the same position comes back for the third time, or after fifty moves each without a capture and without a goose moving forwards.
