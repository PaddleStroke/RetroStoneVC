Falling Dominoes
# The aim
Dominoes stand on the wooden squares of a board. Move some of them, then push one: every domino must fall, and the red trigger domino must fall last.
# A move
Pick up any piece but the trigger and put it on a free wooden square, or turn it where it stands: each is one move. Then push a domino along its length.
- A domino falls only when it is hit along its length, and knocks over the next piece in its way.
- A turn sends the chain round a corner.
- A splitter sends the chain both ways, to the left and to the right.
- A delay domino, marked with an hourglass, falls a moment after it is hit.
- A long domino reaches two squares on, over a gap.
- A stopper never falls and stops the chain.
# The end of the game
If something is left standing, or the trigger falls too soon, everything stands up again: move and push again.
# Levels and stars
Solved in the fewest moves possible: 3 stars; with up to half as many moves again: 2 stars; otherwise 1.
