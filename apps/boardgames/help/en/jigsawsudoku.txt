Jigsaw Sudoku
# The aim
Fill the grid of 9 × 9 cells so that every row, every column and every region holds each digit from 1 to 9 once. The regions are not boxes of 3 × 3: thick lines outline nine shapes of nine cells, each in its own wood. Some digits are given at the start. Every puzzle has exactly one solution, found by reasoning alone.
# The end of the game
The puzzle is solved when the grid is full and right.
# Levels and stars
No hint and no mistake: 3 stars; up to two hints and mistakes together: 2 stars; otherwise 1. A mistake is a wrong digit that you take back with undo.
