Barbu
# The aim
Score the most points over seven contracts, chosen by each player in turn.
# The cards
All 52 cards, 13 each. The ace is high, then king, queen, jack, 10 down to 2.
# The contracts
The declarer chooses one contract per deal, each of the seven once:
- No tricks: minus 2 per trick.
- No hearts: minus 2 per heart, minus 6 for the ace. No heart lead while holding another suit. Ends once all hearts are taken.
- No queens: minus 6 per queen. Ends once the four are taken.
- No king of hearts (the barbu): minus 20. No heart lead while holding another suit; his holder must throw him as soon as they cannot follow suit. Ends when he is taken.
- No last two tricks: minus 10 for the next-to-last, minus 20 for the last.
- Trumps: the declarer names trumps; plus 5 per trick. On a trump lead, beat the best trump if you can; if you cannot follow suit, trump, over the best trump if you can.
- Dominoes: the declarer names a starting rank. In turn lay one card: that rank starts its suit's row; one rank above or below a row's end lengthens it (ace high, no wrap round). Lay if you can, otherwise pass. First out 45, second 20, third 5, last minus 5.
# Playing
The declarer leads first. Follow suit if you can, otherwise play any card (unless the contract says otherwise). The highest card of the suit led, or the highest trump, wins; the winner leads next.
# Doubling
With doubling, once the contract is named each other player in turn may double any of the others (in trumps and dominoes only the declarer). Then each doubled player, the declarer last, may redouble. After the deal each doubled pair compares scores: the difference goes from the lower to the higher, twice if redoubled.
# The end of the game
Full game: 28 deals, each player declaring seven in a row. Short game: 7 deals, the declarer changes every deal and each contract is played once (the first three players choose two, the fourth one). Scores add up to zero. The highest total wins; ties share the win.
