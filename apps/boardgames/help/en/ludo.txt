Ludo
# The aim
Bring your four tokens all the way round the board and into the centre before the others. For 2 to 4 players; Petits chevaux, with horses, follows the Ludo rules below.
# Throwing and moving
- Throw the die and move one token clockwise by the number.
- Only a 6 brings a token out of its yard onto its start square. With a 6 you may also move a token already out.
- A 6 gives another throw. A third 6 in a row is lost and ends your turn.
- If no token can move, the throw is lost.
# Meeting other tokens
- Landing exactly on another player's token sends it back to its yard (both, if two stand there).
- The start squares and the star squares are safe: nobody is sent back there.
- Your own tokens may share a square; they never block.
# Going home
After a full lap your token turns into its home column, where it is safe, and must reach the centre with the exact number.
# Petits chevaux (Little Horses)
- Only a 6 brings a horse out. Every 6 gives another throw.
- A horse never passes another horse, and two never share a square. Stopping on another player's horse sends it back, even on a start square.
- A horse must stop exactly at the foot of its staircase, then climb one step at a time with the step's number, 1 to 6; a 6 again takes it into the centre.
# The end of the game
The first player to bring all four tokens into the centre wins.
