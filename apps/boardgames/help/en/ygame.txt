# The aim
Join all three sides of the triangle with one group of your stones. A corner cell belongs to both sides that meet there. White plays first.
# Playing
- In turn, each player places one stone on any empty cell. Stones never move and are never captured.
- Two of your stones are joined when their cells touch along an edge.
- There is never a draw.
# The swap
On the second move only, Black may take White's first stone instead of placing one: the stone turns black where it stands. Then White plays again.
