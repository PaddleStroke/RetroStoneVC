# The aim
Join all three sides of the triangle with one group of your stones. A corner cell belongs to both sides that meet there. White (light) plays first.
# Playing
- In turn, each player places one stone on any empty cell. Stones never move and are never captured.
- Two of your stones are joined when their cells touch along an edge: each cell has six neighbours.
- There is never a draw: once the board is full, one of the players has always joined the three sides.
# The swap
Playing first is a large advantage. On the second move only, Black may take White's first stone instead of placing one: the stone turns black where it stands. Then White plays again.
# The end of the game
The first player to join the three sides wins.
