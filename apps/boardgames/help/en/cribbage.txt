Cribbage
# The aim
Be the first to score 121 points.
# The deal
- Each player gets 6 cards and lays 2 away (3 players: 5 and 1, plus one card from the pack): the crib, an extra hand for the dealer.
- The top card of the cut pack is the starter. A jack gives the dealer 2 points (his heels).
# The play
- In turn, lay a card and add it to the count (ace 1, pictures 10), never past 31; a player who cannot lay says "go".
- Exactly 15 or 31: 2. A pair with the card before: 2, three of a kind 6, four 12. A run in the last 3 or more cards, in any order: 1 a card.
- When nobody can lay, the last player who did scores 1 (nothing more on 31) and the count starts again from 0. The last card of the play scores 1 too.
# The show
Each hand counts with the starter: the dealer's left first, the dealer last, then the crib.
- 15s: 2 each. Pairs: 2 each. Runs: 1 a card (a double run counts twice).
- Four cards of one suit in the hand: 4, or 5 with the starter. The crib only counts a flush of five.
- The jack of the starter's suit in the hand: 1 (his nobs).
# The end of the game
The first to reach 121 wins at once, even in the middle of the play or of the show.
