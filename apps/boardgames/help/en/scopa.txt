Scopa
# The aim
Capture cards by matching their value, and sweep the table clean: each sweep is a scopa, worth a point. For 2 players, or 4 in two partnerships sitting face to face. The first side to reach 11 points wins.
# The cards
The Italian pack of 40: coins (denari), cups (coppe), swords (spade) and clubs (bastoni), from 1 to 7, then the fante (8, a pawn), the cavallo (9, a knight) and the re (10, a king).
# Playing
3 cards each and 4 face up on the table (dealt again if three or four re show); 3 more each whenever every hand is empty, until the pack runs out.
- Play a card. It captures a table card of the same value (if several, you choose one); otherwise table cards that add up to its value (if several sets, you choose one).
- A single card comes before a sum: with a 7 on the table, a 7 cannot take a 3 and a 4.
- A card that can capture must capture; one that cannot stays on the table.
- Clearing the table is a scopa, worth 1 point, except with the very last card of the round.
- At the end of the round, the cards left go to whoever captured last.
# Scoring
After each round, each side scores 1 point for:
- the most cards;
- the most coins;
- the settebello, the 7 of coins;
- the primiera: each side's best card in each suit, the 7 counting 21, the 6 18, the ace 16, the 5 15, the 4 14, the 3 13, the 2 12, the courts 10; the higher total wins, and a side missing a suit has none;
- each scopa.
On a tie, nobody scores that point.
# The end of the game
After the round in which a side reaches 11 points, the side with more points wins; if both have the same score, another round is played.
