Happy Families
# The aim
Collect whole families: 7 families of 6 members (grandfather, grandmother, father, mother, son and daughter).
# The cards
- The colour and the animal tell the family; the figure in the corner tells the member.
# A turn
- 7 cards each with 2 or 3 players, 6 with 4, 5 with 5 or 6. The rest is the stock.
- Ask another player for a member of a family you already hold a card of, but not a card you hold.
- If they have it, they give it to you and you ask again.
- If not, "pioche!": draw from the stock. If it is the card you asked for, ask again; if not, the next player plays.
- A complete family is laid down at once.
- With no cards on your turn, draw one. If nobody else has cards, ask anyone: the stock answers.
# The end of the game
When all 7 families are down, the most families wins; equal players share the win.
