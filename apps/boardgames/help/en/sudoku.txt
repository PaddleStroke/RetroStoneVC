Sudoku
# The aim
Fill the grid of 9 × 9 cells so that every row, every column and every box of 3 × 3 holds each digit from 1 to 9 once. Some digits are given at the start. Every puzzle has exactly one solution, found by reasoning alone. Choose the level first (the stones under each picture):
- Easy: a cell with only one digit left, or a digit with only one place left in its row, column or box, is always enough.
- Medium: you will also need pairs, and digits whose places in a box all lie in one row or column.
- Hard: triples, and patterns across several rows and columns.
- Expert: longer chains of reasoning.
# A move
Put a digit in an empty cell. You can change it or take it out at any time; the given digits stay. Small notes in a cell help you remember the digits it could still hold.
# The end of the game
The puzzle is solved when the grid is full and right: a wave of gold light runs over the board.
# Levels and stars
There are 150 levels in six chapters, each with one solution: the first ones need only singles and give many digits, the last ones ask you to try a digit and follow it until something breaks. Choose one on the level screen. The die tab after the chapters makes a new puzzle at random, at the level you choose. No hint and no mistake: 3 stars; up to two hints and mistakes together: 2 stars; otherwise 1. A mistake is a wrong digit taken back with undo.
# Playing on the device
Tap a cell, then a key under the board: its digit goes in, in blue ink (the given digits are dark). Tap the same key again to take it out, or drag a key onto a cell. The cell's row, column and box are lit faintly, and every cell holding its digit more warmly. With no empty cell picked, a key lights the cells of its digit. The buttons beside the board:
- Pencil: notes. The keys then put small digits in the cell, or take them out. A digit you put in clears that note from its row, column and box.
- Eraser: takes out the cell's digit, or its notes.
- Lamp: a hint. It puts a wrong digit right, or else fills the easiest cell, in gold light.
- Eye: shows clashes (a digit already in its row, column or box turns red). It is on at first; tap it to hide them.
A key fades once all nine of its digits are on the board. Undo takes back one action at a time, all the way to the start. The bar shows your moves.
