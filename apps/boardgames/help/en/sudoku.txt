Sudoku
# The aim
Fill the grid of 9 × 9 cells so that every row, every column and every box of 3 × 3 holds each digit from 1 to 9 once. Some digits are given at the start. Every puzzle has exactly one solution, found by reasoning alone.
# A move
Tap a cell, then a key beside the board: its digit goes in. You can change it or take it out at any time; the given digits stay. With the pencil, the keys put small notes instead: the digits the cell could still hold.
# The end of the game
The puzzle is solved when the grid is full and right.
# Levels and stars
No hint and no mistake: 3 stars; up to two hints and mistakes together: 2 stars; otherwise 1. A mistake is a wrong digit that you take back with undo.
