Scorpion
# The aim
Build the four suits in the columns, each one in order from the king down to the ace.
# The layout
- Seven columns of seven cards. In the first four, the three bottom cards are face down.
- The last three cards wait at the top left.
# Moving cards
- Any face-up card can move, with all the cards on it, onto the top card of a column that is one rank higher and of the same suit: the 6 of hearts on the 7 of hearts.
- Only a king, with what lies on it, may fill an empty column.
- A face-down card left on top of a column turns face up.
- Once, at any time, tap the three waiting cards to deal them onto the first three columns.
# The end of the game
A whole suit in order from king to ace on top of a column goes off by itself. You win when all four suits have gone.
# Levels and stars
No hint and no undo: 3 stars.
