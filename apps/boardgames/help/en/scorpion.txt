Scorpion
# The aim
Build the four suits in the columns, each one in order from the king down to the ace. A game for one player.
# The layout
- Seven columns of seven cards. In the first four, the three bottom cards are face down; all the others are face up.
- The last three cards wait at the top left.
# Moving cards
- Any face-up card can move, with all the cards lying on it, onto the top card of a column that is one rank higher and of the same suit: the 6 of hearts on the 7 of hearts.
- Only a king, with what lies on it, may fill an empty column.
- When a face-down card is left on top of a column, it turns face up.
- Tap the three waiting cards to deal them, one on each of the first three columns: at any time, but only once.
# The end of the game
A whole suit lying in order from the king to the ace on top of a column goes off by itself. You win when all four suits have gone off.
# Levels and stars
150 levels in six chapters, each a deal that can be won; the die tab deals at random. No hint and no undo: 3 stars; up to two in all: 2 stars; otherwise 1.
