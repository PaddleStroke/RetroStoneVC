Eight Queens
# The aim
Place queens on a board so that no queen attacks another: never two on the same row, column or diagonal. Every row gets one queen. The black queens are already placed and cannot be moved. Some boards have holes: squares cut out where no queen may stand; the queens' lines run on across them.
Each level has one solution, found by reasoning alone. The random boards have many (92 on 8 × 8).
# A move
Tap a square to put a queen on it, tap it again to take the queen off.
# The end of the game
The puzzle is solved when every row has its queen and none attacks another.
# Levels and stars
No hint and no mistake: 3 stars; up to two hints and mistakes together: 2 stars; otherwise 1. A mistake is a queen put on a square another queen attacks; undo does not take it back.
