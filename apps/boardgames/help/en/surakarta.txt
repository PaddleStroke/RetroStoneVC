Surakarta
# The aim
Take all the other player's pieces. Light plays first.
# Moving
Move one of your pieces one step to any of the eight neighbouring points, if it is empty. The loops are not used for these steps.
# Capturing
A piece captures by travelling along a circuit (the red or the green lines), over empty points and round at least one loop, until it meets a piece. If that piece is an enemy one, it is taken and the capturing piece takes its place.
- The way must be clear. The point the piece started from counts as empty if its way crosses it again.
- It goes straight on where lines cross and turns only by the loops, round as many as it likes.
- Capturing is never compulsory.
# The end of the game
- A player who has no pieces left, or cannot move, loses.
- When the same position comes back for the third time, or after forty moves each without a capture, the player with more pieces wins; with as many pieces, it is a draw.
