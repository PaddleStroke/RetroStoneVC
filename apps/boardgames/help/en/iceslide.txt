Ice Slide
# The aim
A red puck lies on a sheet of ice, among rocks. Somewhere on the ice is a hole. Push the puck into the hole in as few pushes as possible. Every puzzle can be solved.
# A move
Push the puck up, down, left or right. On ice it cannot stop by itself: it slides until a rock or the edge of the ice stops it. If it passes over the hole on the way, it drops in.
# The end of the game
The puzzle is solved when the puck drops into the hole.
# Levels and stars
Solved at par, the fewest pushes that solve it: 3 stars; with up to half as many pushes again: 2 stars; otherwise 1.
