Ice Slide
# The aim
A red puck lies on a sheet of ice, among rocks. Somewhere on the ice is a hole. Push the puck into the hole in as few pushes as possible. Choose the puzzle first: easy, medium or hard (a larger sheet, more pushes needed).
Every puzzle is made fresh and can always be solved. Its par, the fewest pushes possible, is found by trying every way.
# A move
Push the puck up, down, left or right. On ice it cannot stop by itself: it slides until a rock or the edge of the ice stops it. If it passes over the hole on the way, it drops in.
# The end of the game
The puzzle is solved when the puck drops into the hole. Solved in the par, a gold light runs out over the ice.
# Levels and stars
There are 150 levels in six chapters, from 5 × 5 squares of ice up to 10 × 9; choose one on the level screen. The die tab after the chapters makes a new puzzle at random, with the choice described above. The par is the fewest pushes. Solved at par: 3 stars; with up to half as many pushes again: 2 stars; otherwise 1. Undo takes a push back, and it no longer counts.
# Playing on the device
Swipe on the ice the way you want the puck to go, or tap a square in the puck's row or column, or tap one of the four arrow buttons under the board (on a computer, the arrow keys work too). An arrow is dimmed when the puck cannot move that way.
The lamp shows a hint: the next push of a shortest way, its arrow lit and its path dotted in gold. If the hole can no longer be reached from where the puck is, the hint first takes back the pushes that led there. Undo takes back one push at a time, all the way to the start. The bar shows your pushes and, at its right end, the par.
