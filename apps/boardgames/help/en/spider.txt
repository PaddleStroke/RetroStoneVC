Spider
# The aim
Build eight runs from king down to ace, each in one suit, and clear them all from the table. Two packs: 104 cards.
# Moving cards
- On a column, put a card one rank lower, of any suit: a 6 on any 7.
- Several cards move together only if they follow each other in one suit.
- Any card or run may go to an empty column.
- When a face-down card is left on top of a column, it turns face up.
- As soon as a column ends with a whole run of one suit, from king to ace, the run leaves the table by itself.
# The stock
Tap the stock to deal one card face up onto every column. You can only deal when no column is empty.
# The end of the game
You win when all eight runs are complete.
# Levels and stars
No hint and no undo: 3 stars.
