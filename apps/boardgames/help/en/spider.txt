Spider
# The aim
Build eight runs from king down to ace, each in one suit, and clear them all from the table. A game for one player with two packs: 104 cards.
# The variants
- One suit: every card is a spade. The easiest.
- Two suits: spades and hearts.
- Four suits: two whole packs. The hardest.
# The layout
- Ten columns: 6 cards in each of the first four, 5 in the others; only the top card of each column is face up.
- The other 50 cards are the stock, at the top right: one back for each deal of ten cards still to come. At the top left, eight places for the finished runs.
# Moving cards
- On a column, put a card one rank lower, of any suit: a 6 on any 7.
- Several cards move together only if they follow each other in one suit.
- Any card or run may go to an empty column.
- When a face-down card is left on top of a column, it turns face up.
- As soon as a column ends with a whole run of one suit, from king to ace, the run leaves the table by itself.
# The stock
Tap the stock to deal one card face up onto every column. As in the classic rules, you can only deal when no column is empty.
# The end of the game
You win when all eight runs are complete. If you are stuck, undo or start a new deal.
# Playing on the device
Tap a card to move it, with the cards on top of it, to its best place: onto a card of its own suit if it can, otherwise onto any card one rank higher, otherwise to an empty column. You can also drag a run and drop it where you want. The lamp in your bar shows a useful move; the number in your bar counts your moves. Undo takes back moves, all the way to the deal.
