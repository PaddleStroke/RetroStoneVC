Light Up
# The aim
Put lamps in the white cells so that every white cell is lit. A lamp lights its own cell and its whole row and column, both ways, up to a dark wall or the edge. No lamp may light another lamp. A wall with a number has exactly that many lamps next to it (above, below, left and right); a wall without a number may have any.
Every puzzle has exactly one solution, found by reasoning alone. Hold a cell to put a dot in it: a note that no lamp goes there.
# The end of the game
The puzzle is solved when every white cell is lit, no lamp lights another and every number has its lamps.
# Levels and stars
No hint and no mistake: 3 stars; up to two hints and mistakes together: 2 stars; otherwise 1. A mistake is a wrong lamp or dot that you take back with undo.
