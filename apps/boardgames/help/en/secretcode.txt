Secret Code
# The aim
The device hides a code of coloured pegs under the lid at the end of the board. Find it on your own before the rows run out. Choose the game first:
- Gentle: 4 pegs, 6 colours, 10 rows. No colour appears twice in the code.
- Classic: 4 pegs, 6 colours, 10 rows. Colours may repeat.
- Harder: 5 pegs, 8 colours, 12 rows. Colours may repeat.
# A move
Fill the next empty row with a peg in each hole, then check it. A guess may use a colour more than once. Small pegs then answer beside the row:
- A dark peg for each peg of the right colour in the right hole.
- A light peg for each other peg whose colour is in the code, but in another hole. Each peg of the code answers only once.
The dark pegs come first, then the light ones, so they never tell which of your pegs they mean.
# The end of the game
You win as soon as a row matches the code: all its answer pegs are dark. If the last row is checked and the code is still hidden, you lose. Either way, the lid slides away and shows the code.
# Playing on the device
Tap a colour in the tray: it goes into the marked hole, and the mark moves on to the next empty one. Tap a hole to mark it, tap it again to change its colour, or drag a peg from the tray to a hole. When the row is full, tap the tick to check it. Undo takes back the last peg placed; a checked row stays. Each colour also has its own sign on top, to tell the pegs apart without the colours. The bar shows how many rows are left.
