Old Maid
# The aim
Do not be the one left with the jack of spades. The jack of clubs is taken out of the pack, so the jack of spades has no partner.
# Pairs
- A pair is two cards of the same rank and the same colour: the two red sevens, the two black kings.
- All 51 cards are dealt. Every pair goes down at once, at the deal and during the game.
# A turn
- Draw one card, face down, from the hand of the player before you.
- If it makes a pair with one of your cards, the pair goes down.
- A player with no cards left is out: they have won.
# The end of the game
The last player holding a card, the jack of spades, loses. Everyone else wins.
