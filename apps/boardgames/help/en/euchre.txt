Euchre
# The aim
Two sides of two partners. The side that makes trumps must take at least three of the five tricks.
# The deal
24 cards, 9 to ace. Each player gets 5; of the four left over, the top one is turned up.
# Making trumps
- Round one, from the dealer's left: each player may order up the turned card, making its suit trumps; the dealer takes it and lays one card away. Or pass.
- Round two: each player may name another suit, or pass. The dealer may not pass.
- The maker may go alone: his partner sits out.
# Trumps
The jack of trumps (the right bower) is highest, then the other jack of the same colour (the left bower, a trump), then A K Q 10 9. The other suits rank A K Q J 10 9.
# Playing
Follow suit if you can. The highest trump wins the trick, otherwise the highest card of the suit led.
# Scoring
- The makers take 3 or 4 tricks: 1 point.
- All 5 tricks, a march: 2 points; alone: 4.
- Fewer than 3: the makers are euchred, the other side scores 2.
# The end of the game
The first side to reach 10 points wins.
