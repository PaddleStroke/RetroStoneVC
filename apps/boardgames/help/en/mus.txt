Mus
# The aim
The Basque game for two sides of two partners sitting face to face. Four lances are played each hand: in each the best cards win, unless a bet scares the other side away. The first side to reach 40 points (tantos) wins, at once, even in the middle of the count.
# The cards
The Spanish pack of 40: 1 to 7, then the sota (10), the caballo (11) and the rey (12). With eight kings (ocho reyes): every 3 counts as a rey and every 2 as an as (an ace).
# Mus
4 cards each. The mano, the player after the dealer, speaks first and wins every tie. In turn, each says mus (to change cards) or cuts. If all four say mus, each lays away 1 to 4 cards and draws as many, and mus is asked again. As soon as one player cuts, the lances begin.
# The four lances
- Grande: the highest cards win, compared one by one: rey, caballo, sota, 7, 6, 5, 4, as.
- Chica: the lowest cards win.
- Pares: a pair (par) is worth 1, three alike (medias) 2, two pairs or four alike (duples) 3. Duples beat medias, medias beat a pair; between equals the higher cards win.
- Juego: figures count 10, the others their number. 31 or more is juego: 31 is best, then 32, 40, 37, 36, 35, 34, 33. If nobody has juego, the lance is Punto: the highest count wins.
Only the players with pares or juego take part in those lances; if only one side has them, there is no betting.
# Betting
- In each lance the players speak in turn from the mano: pass (paso) or bet (envido: 2 points or more).
- Facing a bet, the opponents accept (quiero), raise, bet the whole game (órdago) or refuse (no quiero). It is refused only when both refuse.
- Refused: the betting side scores 1 point, or the bet before the last raise, and wins the lance.
- An accepted órdago: the hands are shown, and the winner of that lance wins the game.
# The count
At the end of the hand the lances count in order:
- Grande and Chica: the accepted bet, or 1 if nobody bet.
- Pares: the accepted bet, plus the pares of both winning partners.
- Juego: the accepted bet, plus 3 for each 31 and 2 for any other juego on the winning side. Punto: the accepted bet plus 1, or 1.
- The winners of a refused lance, or of one only their side had, still count their pares or juego.
