Doppelkopf
# The aim
A card game for four with a double pack, by the rules of the German Doppelkopf League (DDV), without compulsory solos. The two players holding the queens of clubs (Re, the Alten) play against the other two (Kontra); at first nobody knows who is with whom.
# The deal
48 cards, 9, 10, jack, queen, king and ace of each suit, each card twice; 12 cards each.
# Reservations
- Before the first card, each player says healthy (gesund) or that he has a reservation (Vorbehalt): a solo or a marriage. The first solo in turn is played; otherwise the marriage; otherwise the normal game.
- With both queens of clubs you may announce a marriage (Hochzeit), or keep quiet and play a silent solo.
# Trumps
- Normal game, from the highest: the 10 of hearts (the Dulle), the queens, the jacks (each in the order clubs, spades, hearts, diamonds), then the ace, 10, king and 9 of diamonds.
- The other suits rank ace, 10, king, 9 (hearts has no 10). Of two equal cards, the first played wins.
# The parties
- Marriage: the first player other than the bride to win one of the first three tricks becomes her partner; if the bride wins all three, she plays a solo.
# Solos
One player, the soloist (Re), plays against the three others (Kontra).
- Queens' or jacks' solo: only the queens, or only the jacks, are trumps.
- Suit solo: the trumps of the normal game, with the chosen suit in place of diamonds.
- Fleshless (Fleischlos): no trumps; every suit ranks ace, 10, king, queen, jack, 9.
# Playing
Follow suit if you can (all trumps count as one suit), otherwise play any card. The highest trump wins, otherwise the highest card of the suit led; the winner leads next.
# Announcements
- Before playing a card you may announce for your party: Re or Kontra while holding at least 11 cards, no 90 at least 10, no 60 at least 9, no 30 at least 8, schwarz at least 7. Each includes the ones below it.
- In reply to the other party's announcement, Re or Kontra may come one card later than its limit.
- In a marriage nothing may be announced before the partner is found; the limits then move on by the tricks it took.
# Winning
- Card points: ace 11, 10 10, king 4, queen 3, jack 2 (240 in all). Re needs 121, Kontra 120; if only Kontra announced, Re needs 120 and Kontra 121.
- A party that announced no 90 (60, 30, schwarz) loses if the other party reaches 90 (60, 30, a trick). If both parties fail such announcements, nobody wins.
# The game value
- Won: 1. The losers under 90, under 60, under 30, without a trick: 1 each.
- Re or Kontra announced: 2 each; each no 90, 60, 30 and schwarz announced: 1.
- 120 against no 90, 90 against no 60, 60 against no 30, 30 against schwarz: 1 each.
- Extra points, normal game and marriage only, 1 each: a fox (an opponent's ace of diamonds in your trick), a Doppelkopf (a trick of 40 or more), Karlchen (the last trick won with the jack of clubs), against the Alten (Kontra wins). The two parties' extra points cancel out.
# Scoring
- Each Re player scores the game value if Re won (below zero if Kontra won), plus Re's extra points, minus Kontra's; each Kontra player the opposite. A soloist scores three times as much.
# The end of the game
After the last deal the highest total wins; equal totals share the win.
