Jungle
# The aim
Move one of your animals into the other player's den. You also win by taking all their animals, or when they cannot move. Light moves first.
# The animals
The number on each animal is its strength, from the elephant (8) down to the rat (1).
# Moving
- An animal moves one square forwards, backwards or sideways. No animal may enter its own den.
- Only the rat may enter the water.
- The lion and the tiger jump across a river in a straight line, unless a rat is in the water on the way.
# Capturing
- An animal captures an enemy animal of the same or a lower strength. The rat captures the elephant, but the elephant cannot capture the rat.
- An animal in one of the other player's traps (the carved squares round the den) has no strength left: any enemy can capture it.
- No capture between the water and the land: a rat in the water can only capture the other rat in the water.
# The end of the game
The game is a draw when the same position comes back for the third time, or after fifty moves each without a capture.
