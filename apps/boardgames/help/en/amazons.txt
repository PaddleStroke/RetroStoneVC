Amazons
# The aim
Be the last player able to move. Light moves first.
# A turn
Every turn has two parts, both made by the same amazon:
- Move: one of your amazons moves like a chess queen, any number of empty squares along a row, a column or a diagonal. Amazons never jump and never capture.
- Shoot: from where it landed, the amazon shoots an arrow, also like a queen, onto an empty square. That square is burnt for the rest of the game: no amazon may stand on it or cross it, and no arrow may fly over it.
- The arrow may fly back to the square the amazon has just left, or across it.
# The end of the game
A player who cannot move any amazon at the start of their turn loses. There are no draws.
