Rectangles
# The aim
Divide the grid into rectangles (squares count too) so that every cell belongs to one rectangle and each rectangle holds exactly one number: the number of cells it covers. Every puzzle has exactly one solution, found by reasoning alone.
# A move
Drag from one corner to the other to lay a rectangle, or tap its two corners. A new rectangle replaces the ones it covers. Tap a rectangle to take it away.
# The end of the game
The puzzle is solved when the rectangles cover the grid, each holding one number equal to its area.
# Levels and stars
No hint and no mistake: 3 stars; up to two hints and mistakes together: 2 stars; otherwise 1. A mistake is a wrong tile that you take back with undo.
