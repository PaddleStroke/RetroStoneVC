Canasta
# The aim
Be the first side to reach 5000 points: 4 players in two partnerships, or 2 players.
# The cards
- Two packs and four jokers. Jokers and 2s are wild.
- Values: joker 50, ace and 2 20, king to 8 10, 7 to 4 5, black 3 5.
- A red 3 is laid down at once and replaced: 100 each, all four 800, counted against a side that has melded nothing.
# The deal
- 11 cards each (2 players: 15). If the card that starts the discard pile is wild or a red 3, it is covered and the pile is frozen.
# A turn
- Draw from the stock (2 players: two cards) or take the whole discard pile, meld if you like, then discard one card.
# Melds and canastas
- A meld: three or more cards of the same rank, at least two natural and at most three wild. Your side has one meld of each rank.
- A canasta: seven cards or more. Natural 500, mixed 300.
- Your side's first melds, laid in one turn, must be worth at least 50 (90 from 1500 points, 120 from 3000, 15 below zero). You may lay them before drawing, to take the pile.
# The discard pile
- To take it, meld its top card at once; the rest goes into your hand.
- It is frozen while a wild card is in it, and for a side that has not melded: then only a natural pair of the top card's rank from your hand takes it.
- Never when a black 3 or a wild card is on top.
# Going out
- Only once your side has a canasta (2 players: two). Until then you may not meld your hand down to fewer than two cards.
- Black 3s may be melded (three or four, no wild card) only when going out.
- Going out scores 100; concealed, 200: the whole hand melded in one turn after drawing from the stock, with nothing melded before, and no initial count needed.
- When the stock is empty, you must take the pile if its top card goes on a meld of your side; otherwise the hand ends.
# The points
Each side scores its melded cards, canastas, red 3s and going out, minus the cards left in its hands.
# The end of the game
The game ends after the hand in which a side reaches 5000 points. The highest total wins; a tie is shared.
