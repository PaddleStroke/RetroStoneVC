Knight's Tour
# The aim
Move a knight so that it visits every square of the board exactly once. Some boards have holes: squares cut out, which the knight jumps over but never lands on. The knight starts on the square given, always one from which a whole tour exists.
# A move
The knight jumps as in chess: two squares in one direction, then one to the side. It may never land on a square it has already visited.
# The end of the game
The tour is complete when every square has been visited. When the last square is a knight's jump away from the first, the tour is closed. When the knight has no move left before the end, it is a dead end: undo and try another way.
# Levels and stars
No hint and no dead end: 3 stars; up to two hints and dead ends together: 2 stars; otherwise 1. Undo does not take a dead end back.
