Ring and Fork
# The aim
Be the first to make one of three shapes with a chain of your stones. Stones are chained when their cells touch along an edge.
- Ring: a loop around at least one cell. The cells inside may be empty or hold stones of either colour.
- Bridge: a chain joining two of the six corner cells.
- Fork: a chain joining three of the six sides. A corner cell belongs to no side.
# A turn
Place one stone on any empty cell. Stones never move and are never captured. Light plays first.
# The swap
With the swap, Dark's first move may instead take Light's first stone: it turns dark where it stands. Then Light plays again.
# The end of the game
The first shape wins. If the board fills up without one, the game is a draw.
