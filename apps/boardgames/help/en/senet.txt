Senet
# The aim
Be the first to take all your pieces off the board. The path snakes through the thirty squares: along the top row, back along the middle one, out along the bottom one. The pieces start in turn on the first squares; light throws first.
# Throwing and moving
- Throw the four sticks: count the pale sides up, 1 to 4; none up counts 5. A 1, 4 or 5 gives another throw.
- Move one piece forwards by the throw, never onto your own piece.
- Landing on a lone piece of the other player swaps the two. Two pieces side by side are safe; three in a row cannot be passed.
- If no piece can go forwards, one must go back. If none can move, the throw is lost.
# The houses
- Square 26: every piece must stop on it exactly.
- Square 27, the water: a piece landing there goes back to square 15, or the first free square before it.
- Squares 26 to 30 leave the board with the exact throw: 5 from 26, 3 from 28, 2 from 29, 1 from 30.
# The end of the game
The first to bear off all their pieces wins.
