Calcudoku
# The aim
Fill the grid so that every row and every column holds each digit from 1 to the grid's size once. Thick lines cut the grid into cages; each cage shows a target and a sign. Its digits added (+) or multiplied (×) make the target; in a cage of two cells, the larger less the smaller (−) or the larger divided by the smaller (÷) makes it. A cage of one cell holds its target. A digit may appear twice in a cage, but never twice in a row or column. Every puzzle has exactly one solution, found by reasoning alone.
# The end of the game
The puzzle is solved when the grid is full and right.
# Levels and stars
No hint and no mistake: 3 stars; up to two hints and mistakes together: 2 stars; otherwise 1. A mistake is a wrong digit that you take back with undo.
