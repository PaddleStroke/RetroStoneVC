Lasca
# The aim
Leave the other player without a move. Lasca is played like draughts, but the pieces taken stay on the board, as prisoners in columns. Light plays first.
# Moving
- A column belongs to the player whose piece is on top, and moves as that piece does, carrying the whole column.
- A soldier moves one square diagonally forward. An officer (a piece with a crown) moves one square diagonally, forwards or backwards.
- A soldier on top of its column that reaches the far row becomes an officer.
# Capturing
Jump over an enemy column diagonally next to yours onto the empty square just beyond. Only its top piece is taken, and goes under your column as a prisoner. The column left behind belongs to whoever owns its new top piece.
- Soldiers capture forwards only, officers forwards and backwards.
- Capturing is compulsory, but you may choose any capture. The same column goes on jumping while it can, but never over the same square twice.
- A soldier that reaches the far row during a capture becomes an officer, and its turn ends there.
# The end of the game
- A player who cannot move loses.
- The game is a draw when the same position comes back for the third time, or after forty moves each without a capture or a soldier moving.
