Hearts
# The aim
Take as few points as you can.
# The cards
All 52 cards are dealt. With 3 players the 2 of diamonds is taken out; with 5, the 2 of diamonds and the 2 of spades; with 6, the 2s and 3s of diamonds and spades. The ace is the highest card.
# Passing
Before the play each player passes three cards: to the left, then to the right, then across, then none, and so on. With 3 or 5 players: left, right, none.
# Playing
- The player holding the 2 of clubs leads it to the first trick.
- Follow suit if you can. The highest card of the suit led wins the trick; there are no trumps.
- On the first trick nobody may play a heart or the queen of spades, unless they hold nothing else.
- Hearts may not be led until a heart has been played, unless the leader holds only hearts.
# Scoring
- Each heart taken: 1 point. The queen of spades: 13 points.
- Shooting the moon: a player who takes all 13 hearts and the queen of spades scores nothing, and every other player scores 26.
# The end of the game
The game ends after the round in which a player reaches 100 points. The lowest total wins; players tied for it share the win.
