Word Tiles
# The aim
Lay letter tiles on the board to make words that cross like a crossword, and score the most points. The game never checks the words, only where the tiles go: the players keep each other honest with the challenge.
# A turn
Each player has 7 tiles. On your turn, do one of these:
- Play: lay one or more tiles in a single row or column, with no gap (tiles already on the board may fill the gaps), touching the tiles already played. The first play covers the centre star and uses at least two tiles. Then draw back up to 7.
- Exchange: put some tiles back in the bag and draw as many, while the bag holds at least 7.
- Pass.
# The score
Every new word a play makes, along its line and across it, scores the values of its letters. The coloured squares count only for the tiles just laid on them:
- Light green: double letter. Dark green: triple letter.
- Amber: double word. Red: triple word. The centre star is a double word.
All seven tiles in one play: 50 more points.
# The challenge
Before doing anything else, the next player may challenge the last play. A challenge always succeeds: the play is taken back, its tiles go back to their owner's rack, and that player has lost their turn. Play fair: challenge only a word that does not exist.
# The tiles
100 letters and 2 blanks, a set for each language: the commoner a letter, the more tiles and the fewer points. A blank stands for any letter and scores nothing. The English set, by points:
- 1: A×8, E×11, H×6, I×7, N×6, O×7, R×6, S×6, T×8
- 2: C×3, D×4, L×4, U×3
- 3: B×2, F×2, G×2, M×3, P×2, W×2, Y×2
- 4: V×1
- 5: K×1
- 8: J×1, Q×1, X×1
- 9: Z×1
# The end of the game
The game ends when a player lays their last tile while the bag is empty (the next player may still challenge it), or after six scoreless turns in a row. Each player then loses the points left on their rack; the player who went out gains them all.
