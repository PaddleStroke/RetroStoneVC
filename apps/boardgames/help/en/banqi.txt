Banqi
# The aim
Capture all of the other player's pieces, or leave them without a move. The 32 pieces of xiangqi start face down and shuffled, one on every square.
# Colours
The first player turns any piece over: its colour, red or black, becomes theirs, and the other player takes the other colour.
# A turn
Do one of these:
- Turn any face-down piece over, of either colour.
- Move one of your face-up pieces one square along a row or a column, to an empty square.
- Capture: move one of your face-up pieces one square onto a face-up enemy piece that it may capture. Face-down pieces can never be captured.
# Ranks
From the highest: general, advisor, elephant, chariot, horse, cannon, soldier. A piece captures pieces of its own rank or lower, with two exceptions:
- The soldier captures the general. The general cannot capture a soldier.
- The cannon captures by jumping along a row or a column over exactly one piece (face up or down, of either colour), any distance, onto the enemy piece just beyond. It can take any face-up enemy piece that way, but never the piece next to it.
# The end of the game
- A player who has lost all sixteen pieces, or cannot move on their turn, loses.
- After 50 turns in a row (both players' turns counted) without a piece turned over and without a capture, the game is a draw.
