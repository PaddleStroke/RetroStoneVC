Untangle
# The aim
Brass pins stand on the board, joined by cords, and the cords cross each other. Move the pins until no two cords cross.
Every puzzle can be untangled.
# A move
Drag one pin anywhere on the board; its cords follow it.
# The end of the game
When no cord crosses another, nor touches a pin it does not belong to, the puzzle is solved.
# Levels and stars
Each puzzle has a par: in a level, the number of pins that were moved; in a random puzzle, the fewest pins found that must move. Solved at par: 3 stars; with up to half as many moves again: 2 stars; otherwise 1. A hint keeps the puzzle to 2 stars, and more than two hints to 1.
