Kakuro
# The aim
Fill every white square with a digit from 1 to 9. A run is a line of white squares, across or down, between dark ones; the dark square before it holds its clue. The digits of a run add up to its clue, and no digit appears twice in a run. In a dark square, the clue above the diagonal is for the run to its right, the one below it for the run underneath. Every puzzle has exactly one solution, found by reasoning alone.
# The end of the game
The puzzle is solved when every square is right.
# Levels and stars
No hint and no mistake: 3 stars; up to two hints and mistakes together: 2 stars; otherwise 1. A mistake is a wrong digit that you take back with undo.
