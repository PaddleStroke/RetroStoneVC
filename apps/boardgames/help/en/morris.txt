Nine Men's Morris
# The aim
Leave the other player with only two pieces, or with no move. Light plays first.
# Placing
At first, the players take turns placing one piece on any empty point, until all their pieces are on the board.
# Moving
Then move one of your pieces along a line to the next point, if it is empty. A player with only three pieces left may fly: move a piece to any empty point.
# Mills
Three of your pieces in a row along one line make a mill. Each time you make one, while placing or moving, remove one of the other player's pieces. You may not take a piece in a mill, unless all their pieces are in mills. Opening a mill and closing it again makes a new mill.
# Twelve men's morris
Twelve pieces each. Pieces also move along the corner diagonals, and three along a diagonal make a mill. If placing fills all 24 points, the game is a draw.
# Six men's morris
Six pieces each. The three points along each side of a square make a mill; the short lines between the squares are only for moving. There is no flying.
# The end of the game
- A player left with two pieces, or who cannot move, loses.
- The game is a draw when the same position comes back for the third time, or after fifty moves each without a mill.
