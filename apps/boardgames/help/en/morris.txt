Nine Men's Morris
# The aim
Leave the other player with only two pieces, or with no move. Each player has nine pieces. Light plays first.
# Placing
At first, the players take turns placing one piece on any empty point, until all eighteen pieces are on the board.
# Moving
Then, on your turn, move one of your pieces along a line to the next point, if it is empty. A player with only three pieces left may fly: move a piece to any empty point.
# Mills
Three of your pieces in a row along one line make a mill. Each time you make a mill, while placing or while moving, remove one of the other player's pieces from the board. You may not take a piece that is in a mill, unless all their pieces are in mills. Opening a mill and closing it again makes a new mill.
# The end of the game
- A player left with two pieces loses.
- A player who cannot move any piece loses.
- The game is a draw when the same position comes back for the third time, or after fifty moves each without a mill.
# Playing on the device
Tap an empty point to place a piece. To move, tap your piece and then its new point, or drag it. After a mill, the pieces you may take glow: tap one.
