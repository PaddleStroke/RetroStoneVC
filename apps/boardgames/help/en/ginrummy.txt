Gin Rummy
# The aim
Be the first to reach 100 points: gather your cards into melds and knock.
# The cards
- A meld: three or four cards of a rank, or three or more cards of one suit in sequence. The ace is low: Q K A is not a run.
- Deadwood: the cards in no meld, at their value; jack, queen and king 10.
# The deal
- 10 cards each. The non-dealer, then the dealer, may take the first card turned up.
# A turn
- Draw from the stock or the discard pile, then discard, but not the card just taken from the pile.
# Knocking
- With 10 or less deadwood after discarding, you may knock and end the hand. Gin: no deadwood at all.
- The defender may lay off deadwood on the knocker's melds, except against gin.
# The points
- The knocker scores the difference between the deadwoods; gin scores 25 plus the defender's deadwood.
- Undercut: a defender with as little deadwood or less scores the difference plus 25.
- Two cards left in the stock and no knock: the hand is dead, and the same dealer deals again.
# The end of the game
At 100 points the winner adds 100, and each player 25 per hand won. If the loser won no hand, the winner's total is doubled.
