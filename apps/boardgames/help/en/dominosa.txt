Dominosa
# The aim
A full set of dominoes was laid on the grid, then the lines between them were wiped away: only the numbers are left. Find where each domino lies. Every domino of the set, from 0-0 to the highest double, is in the grid exactly once. Every puzzle has exactly one solution, found by reasoning alone.
# A move
Join two neighbouring cells into a domino, or take a domino away. A new domino replaces the ones on its cells.
# The end of the game
The puzzle is solved when the dominoes cover the grid, each domino of the set once.
# Levels and stars
No hint and no mistake: 3 stars; up to two hints and mistakes together: 2 stars; otherwise 1. A mistake is a wrong domino that you take back with undo.
