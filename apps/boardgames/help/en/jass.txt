Jass
# The aim
The Schieber: two teams of two partners sitting face to face. The first team to reach 1000 points (2500 in the long game) wins.
# The cards
36 cards, 6 to ace.
# The deal
Each player gets 9 cards; the deal moves on one seat each time. The player after the dealer chooses the game and leads the first trick.
# Choosing the game
- Name a trump suit, or Obenabe (no trumps, the ace is high) or Undeufe (no trumps, the six is high).
- Or push (schieben): your partner must then choose, but you still lead the first trick.
# Trumps
- Trumps: jack, the Puur (20 points), then 9, the Nell (14), ace 11, king 4, queen 3, 10 10, 8, 7, 6.
- Other suits: ace 11, king 4, queen 3, jack 2, 10 10, 9, 8, 7, 6.
- Obenabe: ace to six, the 8s worth 8. Undeufe: six to ace, the 6s worth 11, the 8s 8, the aces nothing.
- The last trick is worth 5 more. A match (all nine tricks) is worth 257.
# Playing
- Follow suit or play a trump. If you cannot follow, play any card.
- When another suit was led, no trump lower than one already in the trick, unless you hold only trumps.
- When trumps are led, follow with a trump; you never have to play the Puur.
- In Obenabe and Undeufe, just follow suit if you can.
# Weis and Stöck
- Declare weis with your first card. A sequence in one suit (6 to ace): three cards 20, four 50, five 100, six 150, seven 200, eight 250, nine 300. Four jacks 200, four nines 150, four aces, kings, queens or tens 100.
- Only the team with the best single weis scores, all its weis.
- Stöck: king and queen of trumps in one hand, 20, counted when the second is played.
# Scoring
- Each team scores its card points, weis and Stöck.
- The long game multiplies: diamonds and hearts once, spades and clubs twice, Obenabe and Undeufe three times.
# The end of the game
Stöck counts when played, weis after the first trick, tricks at the end of the deal, which is always played out. If both teams pass the mark in the same deal's tricks, the higher total wins.
