Water Sort
# The aim
Glass tubes hold layers of coloured liquid, all mixed up, with two tubes empty. Pour them until each colour fills a tube of its own. A tube holds four layers.
Every puzzle can be solved.
# A move
Tap a tube, then another to pour into it. The top layer goes, and every layer of the same colour just under it, as far as the other tube has room. You may only pour onto the same colour, or into an empty tube.
# The extra tube
Once per puzzle you may add an extra empty tube: tap the faint tube with a plus at the end of the last shelf.
# The end of the game
The puzzle is solved when every colour fills a tube of its own and the other tubes are empty.
# Levels and stars
Each puzzle has a par: the fewest pours that solve it. Solved at par: 3 stars; with up to half as many pours again: 2 stars; otherwise 1. Using the extra tube keeps the puzzle to 2 stars.
