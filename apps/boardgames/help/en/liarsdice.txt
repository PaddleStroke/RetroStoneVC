Liar's Dice
# The aim
Be the last player with dice. Everyone has five dice under a cup and sees only their own. For 2 to 6 players.
# A round
- Everyone throws under their cup.
- The first player bids: at least so many dice on the whole table show a face.
- Each player in turn bids higher or calls the last bid a lie.
- Higher means more dice (any face), or as many dice with a higher face.
- Ones are wild and count as the bid's face, so bids are on faces 2 to 6.
# The call
Every cup is lifted. If the dice showing the face, plus the ones, are at least the bid, the caller loses a die; if not, the bidder does. The loser starts the next round, or the next player if the loser is out.
# The end of the game
A player with no dice left is out. The last player with dice wins.
