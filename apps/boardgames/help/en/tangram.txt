Tangram
# The aim
The tangram is an old Chinese puzzle: a square cut into seven pieces, the tans. Two large triangles, a medium one, two small ones, a square and a parallelogram. Use all seven to fill the dark silhouette, without any overlap. There are 98 figures in six chapters: shapes, people, animals, boats and houses, things, letters and numbers. Each one was made for this game from the seven tans, so every figure can be solved.
# A move
Move a tan, turn it an eighth of a turn, or turn the parallelogram over (the others look the same either way). Tans lie flat and never overlap.
# The end of the game
The figure is solved when the seven tans cover the silhouette exactly: gold light runs over them. Any way that fills the silhouette counts, not only the one it was made with. Three stars without a hint, two with one or two hints, one with more.
# Playing on the device
Choose a figure on the level screen; the solved ones show their stars.
- Drag a tan. When you let go, it settles into a fitting place: its corners meet the corners of the silhouette or of another tan.
- Tap a tan: it turns an eighth of a turn.
- Tap the parallelogram twice quickly: it turns over.
The tan you touched last lies on top. The lamp gives a hint: it slides one tan into its place, in gold light. Undo takes back one action at a time, all the way to the start. The bar shows your actions.
