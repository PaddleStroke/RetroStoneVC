Tangram
# The aim
A square is cut into seven pieces, the tans: two large triangles, a medium one, two small ones, a square and a parallelogram. Use all seven to fill the dark silhouette, without any overlap.
# A move
Drag a tan: when you let go, it settles into a fitting place. Tap a tan to turn it an eighth of a turn. Tap the parallelogram twice quickly to turn it over.
# The end of the game
The figure is solved when the seven tans cover the silhouette exactly. Any way that fills the silhouette counts, not only the one it was made with.
# Levels and stars
No hint: 3 stars; up to two hints: 2 stars; otherwise 1.
