Four in a Row
# The aim
Be the first to line up four discs of your colour: across, up and down, or diagonally.
# A move
The board stands upright with seven columns. On your turn, drop one disc into a column: it falls to the lowest empty hole. A full column cannot take another disc. Light plays first.
# The end of the game
The first player with four discs in a row wins. If the board fills up with no row of four, the game is a draw.
# Playing on the device
Tap a column, or slide your disc along the top and let go. Each bar shows how many discs that player has left.
