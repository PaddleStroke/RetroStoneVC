Pipes
# The aim
Every tile holds a piece of brass pipe: an end, a straight, a bend or a tee. Turn the tiles so that all the pipes join into one network fed by the tank in the middle, with no pipe left open: no pipe facing a tile that doesn't meet it, none running into the edge of the board.
On a wrapped board (brass portholes round the frame), a pipe leaving one edge comes back in at the opposite edge.
Every puzzle can be solved by reasoning alone and has only one solution.
# A move
Tap a tile to turn it a quarter turn clockwise.
# The end of the game
When every tile is joined and no pipe is open, the water reaches every tile.
# Levels and stars
Each puzzle has a par: the fewest quarter turns that solve it. Solved at par: 3 stars; with up to half as many moves again: 2 stars; otherwise 1. A hint keeps the puzzle to 2 stars, and more than two hints to 1.
