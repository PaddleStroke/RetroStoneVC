Pipes
# The aim
Every tile holds a piece of brass pipe: an end, a straight, a bend or a tee. Turn the tiles so that all the pipes join into one network fed by the tank in the middle, with no pipe left open: no pipe facing a tile that doesn't meet it, none running into the edge of the board. The water then fills every tile. Choose the size first: 5 × 5, 7 × 7, 9 × 9, 11 × 11, or 9 × 9 wrapped (brass portholes round the frame): there a pipe leaving one edge comes back in at the opposite edge.
Every puzzle can be solved by reasoning alone and has only one solution.
# A move
Turn one tile a quarter turn clockwise.
# The end of the game
When every tile is joined and no pipe is open, the water reaches every tile and a gold light runs out from the tank.
# Levels and stars
There are 150 levels in six chapters, from 4 × 4 up to 11 × 11 and wrapped boards, each with one solution; choose one on the level screen. The die tab after the chapters makes a new puzzle at random, with the choice described above. Each puzzle has a par: the fewest quarter turns that solve it. Solved at par: 3 stars; with up to half as many moves again: 2 stars; otherwise 1. A hint keeps the puzzle to 2 stars, and more than two hints to 1. Undo takes a move back, and it no longer counts.
# Playing on the device
- Tap a tile to turn it a quarter turn clockwise.
- The water runs from the tank along every pipe joined to it. Where it reaches an open pipe, it spills: look for the drops.
- The lamp gives a hint: it turns one wrong tile the right way (next to the water when it can), in gold light.
Undo takes back one turn or hint at a time, all the way to the start. The bar shows your moves.
