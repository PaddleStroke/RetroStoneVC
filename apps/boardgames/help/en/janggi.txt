Janggi
# The aim
Checkmate the other general. Cho (blue-green) moves first, then Han (red).
# The board
The pieces stand on the points, not in the squares. There is no river. The diagonals of the palaces are lines too.
# How the pieces move
- General and guard: one point along a line of the palace, the diagonals included, without leaving it.
- Horse: one point straight, then one point diagonally outward. It is blocked if the first point is taken.
- Elephant: one point straight, then two points diagonally outward. It is blocked if either point it passes is taken.
- Chariot: any number of points along a line, and along the diagonals of a palace.
- Cannon: moves and captures only by jumping over exactly one piece on its line, the diagonals of a palace included. It may not jump over or capture a cannon.
- Soldier: one point forward or sideways. In the other side's palace it may also move forward along a diagonal.
# Passing
Instead of moving, you may pass, except when in check. A player who has no legal move but is not in check must pass.
# Check and the end of the game
You may never leave your own general in check, nor leave the two generals face to face on a file with nothing between them.
- Checkmate: the general is in check and no move can save it. That player loses.
- Draw: the same position for the third time with the same player to move (four passes in a row do it too), or a thousand moves each. There is no count of points.
