Egyptian Ratscrew
# The aim
Win all the cards. A fast game of nerves, called Bataille corse in France: everyone slaps the pile in real time.
# Turning cards
- All 52 cards are dealt face down, one pile for each player. Nobody looks at their cards.
- In turn, each player turns the top card of their pile onto the pile in the middle.
- An ace, king, queen or jack demands tribute: the next player turns cards until they turn one of those too, with 4 tries for an ace, 3 for a king, 2 for a queen, 1 for a jack. If they do, the next player owes tribute in turn; if not, the face card's player wins the whole pile and plays next.
# Slapping
- Anyone may slap the pile at any moment: tap your own zone, away from your pile. A double, two cards of the same rank one on the other, wins the pile for the first to slap, who plays next.
- In the sandwich game, a sandwich counts too: two cards of the same rank with one card between them.
- A wrong slap costs a card: your top card goes face down under the middle pile.
- A player with no cards left may still slap to come back. A wrong slap with no card to give puts them out.
