Yacht
# The aim
Score the most points by filling every row of your column with throws of five dice. For 1 to 6 players; alone, aim for 200 points (Generala: 150).
# A turn
- Throw the five dice up to three times, keeping any dice between throws.
- Then write them in one empty row. If they fit no row, you must still choose one: it scores 0.
# The score sheet
Each row is a picture of dice.
- One die (ones to sixes): the sum of that face.
- The star: a bonus for 63 or more in ones to sixes: 35 (Classic), 50 (Nordic).
- Two dice overlapping (Nordic): a pair, its sum. Two pairs (Nordic): their sum.
- Three dice overlapping: three of a kind. Classic: all five dice. Nordic: the three.
- Four dice overlapping: four of a kind. Classic: all five. Nordic: the four. Generala (poker): 40.
- Three dice and two dice: a full house. Classic: 25. Nordic: the dice. Generala: 30.
- Four small dice in a row (Classic): four in a row, 30.
- Five small dice in a row: a straight. Classic: 40. Nordic: 1-2-3-4-5, 15; 2-3-4-5-6, 20. Generala: 1-2-3-4-5, 2-3-4-5-6 or 3-4-5-6-1: 20.
- Dice thrown every way: chance, the sum.
- Five dice alike: five of a kind, 50. With a star (Classic): the extra fives of a kind, 100 each.
- Two rows of five alike (Generala): the double generala, 100.
# Classic: more fives of a kind
Once the five-of-a-kind row holds 50, each further one scores 100 more. Once that row is filled, a five of a kind is a joker: it goes in its face's row if empty; otherwise any empty row below the star, where the full house and straights score in full; otherwise a row among ones to sixes, for 0.
# Generala
A straight, full house or poker on the first throw ("served") scores 5 more. A served generala wins at once. The double generala scores 100 only once the generala row holds 50; otherwise it scores 0. No bonus for ones to sixes.
# The end of the game
When every row is filled, the highest total wins; equal totals share the win.
