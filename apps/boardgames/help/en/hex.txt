Hex
# The aim
Join your two sides of the board, painted in your colour, with an unbroken chain of your stones. White plays first.
# Playing
- In turn, each player places one stone on any empty cell. Stones never move and are never captured.
- Two of your stones are joined when their cells touch along an edge. The corner cells belong to both sides that meet there.
- There is never a draw.
# The swap
On the second move only, Black may take White's first stone instead of placing one: it becomes black and moves to its mirror cell across the long diagonal. Then White plays again. To swap, Black taps White's first stone.
