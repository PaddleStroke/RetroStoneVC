Free Cells
# The aim
Move all 52 cards to the four foundations, one for each suit, built up from the ace to the king.
# The layout
- The 52 cards lie face up in eight columns, below four free cells and the four foundations.
# Moving cards
- Move the top card of a column: onto a card one rank higher and of the other colour, into an empty free cell, or onto its foundation.
- Each free cell holds one card. Any card may go to an empty column.
- A run of cards in order moves together if there is room to move it one card at a time: as many cards as the empty free cells plus one, doubled for each empty column (not counting the column it goes to).
- A card on a foundation stays there.
# The deals
Deals are numbered 1 to 32000, as in other versions of the game; all can be won except deal 11982.
# The end of the game
You win when all the cards are on the foundations.
# Levels and stars
3 stars: no hint and no undo.
