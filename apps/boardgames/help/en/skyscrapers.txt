Skyscrapers
# The aim
The grid is a town seen from above. Put a building in every cell, its height from 1 to the size of the grid, each height once in every row and every column. A number on the edge tells how many buildings you see from there, looking along that row or column: a taller building hides every lower one behind it.
Some heights may be given at the start, on darker cells. Every puzzle has exactly one solution, found by reasoning alone.
# A move
Tap a cell, then a key beside the board: its height goes in. With the pencil, the keys put small notes instead: the heights the cell could still hold.
# The end of the game
The puzzle is solved when every cell has its building and the town keeps every rule.
# Levels and stars
No hint and no mistake: 3 stars; up to two hints and mistakes together: 2 stars; otherwise 1. A mistake is a wrong height that you take back with undo.
