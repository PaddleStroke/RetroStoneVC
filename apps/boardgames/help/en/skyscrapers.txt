Skyscrapers
# The aim
The grid is a town seen from above. Put a building in every cell, its height from 1 to the size of the grid, each height once in every row and every column. A number on the edge tells how many buildings you see from there, looking along that row or column: a taller building hides every lower one behind it.
Some heights may be given at the start, on darker cells. Every puzzle has exactly one solution, found by reasoning alone. Choose the size (from 4 × 4 to 7 × 7) and the level (the stones under each picture):
- Easy: a height with one place left in its row or column, a cell with one height left, and what an edge number allows near it (1: the tallest stands first; the full count: all in order, lowest first).
- Medium: you will also need to look at a whole line: every order of its heights that fits its numbers agrees on some cells.
- Hard: longer chains of reasoning, never a guess.
# A move
Put a height in a cell, change it or take it out. Small notes in a cell help you remember the heights it could still hold.
# The end of the game
The puzzle is solved when every cell has its building and the town keeps every rule: a wave of gold light runs over the board.
# Playing on the device
Tap a cell, then a key under the board: its height goes in (the same key again takes it out). You can also drag a key onto a cell. Tap the chosen cell again to raise its building by one; hold a cell a moment to clear it. The buildings are little wooden towers: the taller, the bigger. The buttons at the right end of the bar:
- Pencil: notes. The keys then put small heights in the cell, or take them out. A height you put in clears that note from its row and column.
- Light bulb: a hint. It puts a wrong height right, or else fills the next cell reasoning finds, in gold light.
A height twice in a row or a column turns red. An edge number fades when its full line shows the right count, and turns red when it shows another. A key fades once its height is in every row. Undo takes back one action at a time, all the way to the start. The bar shows your moves.
