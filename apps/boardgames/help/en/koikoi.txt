Koi-Koi
# The aim
Capture cards by matching their months and form yaku, the scoring combinations. Then stop and score, or call koi-koi and play on for more. The most points after 12 rounds (6 in the short game) wins.
# The cards
48 cards: 12 months of 4.
- Brights (5): the crane, the curtain, the moon, the rain man and the phoenix.
- Animals (9), ribbons (10: three red poetry ribbons with writing, three blue, four plain red) and plains (24).
# A turn
8 cards each and 8 face up on the field, dealt again if the field holds a whole month. The dealer starts: ivory, then the winner of the last round.
- Play a card from your hand: it captures a field card of its month (with two, you choose; with three, you take all four). Otherwise it stays on the field.
- Then turn over the top card of the stock and play it the same way.
# The yaku
- Gokō: the 5 brights, 10. Shikō: 4 brights without the rain man, 8. Ame-Shikō: 4 with him, 7. Sankō: 3 without him, 5.
- Inoshikachō: the boar, the deer and the butterflies, 5.
- Akatan: the 3 poetry ribbons, 5. Aotan: the 3 blue ribbons, 5.
- Hanami-zake: the curtain and the sake cup, 5. Tsukimi-zake: the moon and the sake cup, 5.
- Tane (5 animals), Tan (5 ribbons), Kasu (10 plains): 1, plus 1 for each further card.
Only the best bright yaku counts. The sake cup is both an animal and a plain.
# Stop or koi-koi
When your turn ends with a new yaku or more points, stop and score, or call koi-koi and play on. If your opponent then stops first, you score nothing and their points are doubled. With no card left in your hand, you stop.
# The points
- Your yaku added up, doubled from 7 points, and doubled again if your opponent had called koi-koi.
- If both hands are played out and nobody stopped, nobody scores.
- Four cards of one month (Teshi) or four pairs of months (Kuttsuki) in the hand dealt win the round at once, for 6 points.
