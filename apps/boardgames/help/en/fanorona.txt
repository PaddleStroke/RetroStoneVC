Fanorona
# The aim
Take all the other player's pieces. Light plays first.
# Moving
Move one of your pieces along a line to the next point, if it is empty. A move that takes nothing is allowed only when no capture is possible anywhere on the board.
# Capturing
Pieces capture without jumping, in two ways:
- Approach: step towards an enemy piece, so that you end right next to it on the same line. It is taken, and so is every enemy piece directly behind it on that line, up to the first gap or one of your own pieces.
- Withdrawal: step straight away from an enemy piece that was right next to you. It is taken, with the unbroken line of enemy pieces behind it.
- When one step could do both, you choose. Capturing is compulsory, but you may choose any capture, not only the biggest.
- After a capture, the same piece may go on capturing, one step at a time, but never back to a point it has stood on this turn, and never twice in a row in the same direction. You may stop whenever you like: tap the piece.
# The end of the game
- A player who loses all their pieces, or cannot move, loses.
- The game is a draw when the same position comes back for the third time, or after fifty moves each without a capture.
