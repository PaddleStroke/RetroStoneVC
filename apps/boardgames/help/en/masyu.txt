Masyu
# The aim
Draw one closed loop through the middles of the cells, from cell to cell side by side. It never crosses or touches itself, and it passes through every pearl.
- On a white pearl the loop goes straight, and it turns in at least one of the two cells just before and after the pearl.
- On a black pearl the loop turns, and on both sides it goes straight on through the next cell.
Every puzzle has exactly one solution, found by reasoning alone. Hold the gap between two cells to cross it: a note that the loop does not pass there.
# The end of the game
The puzzle is solved when the loop is closed and keeps every rule: the wire turns gold.
# Levels and stars
No hint and no mistake: 3 stars; up to two hints and mistakes together: 2 stars; otherwise 1. A mistake is a wrong line or cross that you take back with undo.
