Crazy Eights
# The aim
Be the first to play all your cards.
# A turn
- Play a card of the same suit (or colour), number or symbol as the top card of the discard pile. If you cannot, draw.
# Crazy Eights
- An eight can be played at any time: name the suit to follow.
- Draw until you can play; with nothing left to draw, pass.
- Points: eight 50, king, queen and jack 10, ace 1, the others their number. The game goes to 100.
# Last Card
- A wild card can be played at any time: name the colour to follow.
- Skip: the next player misses a turn. Reverse: play changes direction (with two players, a skip). Draw two: the next player draws 2 and misses a turn.
- Wild draw four: name the colour; the next player draws 4 and misses a turn. Only allowed with no card of the colour to follow.
- Draws cannot be stacked. Instead of playing, you may draw one card: play it if it fits, or pass.
- Before you play your second last card, tap the last card button. If you forget, the next player may catch you first by tapping your hand: you draw 2.
- Points: number cards their value, skip, reverse and draw two 20, wild cards 50. The game goes to 500.
# The end of a round
The first player out scores the points left in the other hands. If nobody can play or draw, the lowest hand wins and scores the difference with each other hand.
# The end of the game
The first player to reach the target score wins.
