Xiangqi
# The aim
Checkmate the other general. Red moves first.
# How the pieces move
The pieces stand on the points, not in the squares.
- General: one point along a line, inside its palace.
- Advisor: one point diagonally, inside its palace.
- Elephant: exactly two points diagonally. It cannot cross the river, and it is blocked if the point it passes over is taken.
- Horse: one point along a line, then one point diagonally outward. It is blocked if the first point is taken.
- Chariot: any number of points along a line.
- Cannon: moves like the chariot, but captures only by jumping over exactly one piece on the way.
- Soldier: one point forward. Once across the river it may also move sideways. It never moves back.
# Check and the end of the game
You may never leave your own general in check, or facing the other general on a file with nothing between them.
- Checkmate: the general is in check and no move can save it. That player loses.
- A player who has no legal move also loses, even when not in check.
- Draw: the same position for the third time with the same player to move, or sixty moves each without a capture.
