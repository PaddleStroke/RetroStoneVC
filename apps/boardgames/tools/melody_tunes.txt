# Play by Ear's tunes (games/melody*): every one in the public domain, written down here from the tunes themselves

# died more than 70 years ago. tools/melody_gen.py reads this file and writes src/games/melody_levels.h.

# A tune is a block:
#   tune   its id (the order here is the order of help/<lang>/melody_titles.txt)
#   title  its English title (the titles shown are in help/<lang>/melody_titles.txt)
#   by     the composer, or "traditional"; the year the composer died ("-" for a traditional tune); the source
#   bpm    quarter notes a minute (the tune is played with its rhythm; only the order of the pitches is asked)
#   notes  the melody: a pitch (C D E F G A B, # or b, an octave 3..6 that stays until another is given), then
#          ",d" its length in sixteenths (4 by default: a quarter note; 2 an eighth, 8 a half note...); "r,d" a rest;
#          "|" a place where a level may end (its first bar, a longer phrase, the whole phrase).
# The keyboard shown runs from C4 (middle C) up to C5, G5 or C6: every tune is written within C4..C6.
