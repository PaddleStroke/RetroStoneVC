# Privacy policy: RetroStone Arcade and RetroStone Arcade Free

*Effective date: 5 October 2026*

This policy covers the apps **RetroStone Arcade** and **RetroStone Arcade Free** (Android, and their Windows
versions), made by 8BCraft (Pierre-Louis Boyer). Contact: [CONTACT E-MAIL].

## In short

The apps collect **no personal data at all**. They have **no ads**, **no analytics**, **no tracking**, **no account**
and **no in-app purchases**. They do not even ask for permission to use the internet: nothing you do in them can leave
your device through the apps.

## What stays on your device

To let you pick up a game where you left it, the apps keep, in their private storage on your device:

- each game's **best scores** and medals;
- a **saved state** of each game you left (where you were in it).

Nobody else can read these files, and we never receive them. They are deleted when you uninstall the app, or when you
clear its data (Android: Settings > Apps > the app > Storage > Clear data).

If you have turned on Android's own backup, Android may copy the app's files (these saves) to your Google account, as
it does for most apps, so they can be restored on a new phone. That copy is handled by Google under its own privacy
policy; we have no access to it.

## Permissions

- **Vibration**: only to make a connected gamepad rumble.
- Touch screen, Bluetooth and USB gamepads are read on the device to play; nothing is recorded.

## Links to Google Play

RetroStone Arcade Free shows the other games of the full version in its menu. Its "Get it on Google Play" button
opens the Google Play page of RetroStone Arcade: what happens in Google Play is covered by Google's privacy policy.
The apps contain no other links, no ads and no third-party code that collects data.

## Children

The games are made for all ages. As the apps collect no data from anyone, they collect none from children either.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at this same address, with its date. An app that
starts collecting data would say so here first, and in its Google Play listing.

## Third-party components

The apps are built with open-source components (SDL2, libxmp-lite, GNU Unifont, stb_image_write), all running on
your device only; none of them sends data. Their licences are in the apps, under "Credits & licences".
