RetroStone gathers 253 games in one free app: 216 board, card and puzzle games around a green table (chess, go, backgammon, mahjong, solitaire, belote, bridge, sudoku, nonograms...) and 37 pixel-art arcade games made for the touch screen. No ads, nothing locked: every game and every level is free.

THE ARCADE
• 37 pixel-art arcade games made for the touch screen, from one-finger games to platformers
• Your best score is kept, and every game waits where you left it
• Gamepads work too: up to 4 players in some games

PLAY TOGETHER, OR AGAINST THE DEVICE
• Friends on one device, passed from hand to hand: 2 to 6 players, depending on the game
• Or let the device take seats, at three levels (easy, medium, hard): in 103 of the 110 games for two or more
• Face to face: lay the phone or tablet flat between you, and the top player's pieces turn to face them
• Card games keep every hand secret: a cover hides the cards while the device goes to the next player
• Undo, turn the board round, portrait or landscape, and each game's rules a tap away

PUZZLES AND LEVELS
• 101 puzzles and solitaire games with hand-made levels, 150 for most of them: over 14,000 levels
• Up to 3 stars per level, and a hint when you are stuck
• Then random puzzles without end

EVERY DAY
• A daily streak, experience, levels and achievements, and your progress in every tab
• Light or dark theme, and a search to find any game by its name

FREE, WITH NO ADS
• Every game and every level, free: no ads, nothing locked, no account
• Open source: the whole code is on GitHub, free to read
• No data collected; it plays offline
• 22 languages
• Like it? An optional tip in the settings helps make new games
