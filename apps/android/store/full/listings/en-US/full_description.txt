RetroStone Arcade gathers 20 original arcade games made in the spirit of the 16-bit consoles: crisp pixel art, catchy music and quick rounds you can pick up in a second.

THE GAMES
• Beaver Rush: gnaw a giant tree log by log, from the left or the right
• Biscuit Bandit: catch the cookies falling from the bakery belts, but never the cups
• Blueberry Tumble: roll down the mountain and jump on the beat of the music
• Crab Coconut: bounce a coconut off your claws and break the falling shells
• Duck Parade: hop across roads and rivers and lead the lost ducklings home
• Frog Reach: stretch the frog's tongue just far enough to cross the swamp
• Hedgehog Hills: curl up downhill, let go uphill and fly over the woodland hills
• Leady Squid: tap to keep a heavy little squid afloat and swim through the gaps
• Mole Marbles: split the bouncing gemstones with your harpoon
• Moth Around: circle the lantern and slip through the closing rings
• Mouse Espresso: fly a mouse wearing an espresso machine through the cafe
• Nutty Branch: run along an endless branch without sliding off its sides
• Otter Rapids: paddle down the river and catch every golden fish
• Pancake Tower: drop the pancakes and stack the tallest tower
• Penguin Slalom: ski between the flags down an endless snowy slope
• Pogo Mamie: chase a runaway cat across the rooftops of Paris on a pogo stick
• Raccoon Recycler: sort the toys by kind or by colour while the rules keep changing
• Radish Rascals: shoo the rabbits from your vegetable beds, spare the hedgehogs
• Snail Switch: flip above or below the vine to dodge thorns and caterpillars
• Spider Swing: swing from flower to flower and climb ever higher

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Simple touch controls, made for each game
• Bluetooth and USB gamepads work too, with up to 4 players in many games
• Leave a game at any moment: it waits for you right where you were
• Your best scores stay on your phone

NO ADS, NO TRACKING
• One purchase for every game: no ads, no in-app purchases, no account
• No data collected; it plays offline
• 22 languages
