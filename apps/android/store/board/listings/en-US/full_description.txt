PLAY TOGETHER, OR AGAINST THE DEVICE
• Friends on one device, passed from hand to hand: 2 to 6 players, depending on the game
• Or let the device take seats, at three levels (easy, medium, hard): in 90 of the 91 games for two or more
• Face to face: lay the phone or tablet flat between you, and the top player's pieces turn to face them
• Card games keep every hand secret: a cover hides the cards while the device goes to the next player
• Undo, turn the board round, portrait or landscape, and each game's rules a tap away
