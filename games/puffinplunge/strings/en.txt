# jellyfish, beetles, moths...): bubbles pop them, a stomp on a soft one bounces the puffin. Gems buy upgrades in the
# shops. Four zones of three levels, the Grumpy Walrus at the bottom. Everything else comes from the house kit
# (games/common/strings/en.txt). The game's name is never translated. Keep it gentle and family-friendly: critters are
# "popped", never killed.
