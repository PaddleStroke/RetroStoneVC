# Hamster Havoc (games/hamsterhavoc): its strings, the English source (docs/i18n.md).
# Everything else comes from the house kit (games/common/strings/en.txt; the title says PRESS A TO PLAY). The game's
# name is never translated.

#. In play, as each wave flies in: 2x letters (2 columns each) centred on row 12, for 1.5 s; %d is the wave.
#. max 18
WAVE_N = WAVE %d

#. In play, under WAVE_N on every 4th wave (a big mouse mothership comes): 2x letters centred on row 15.
#. max 18
BOSS = MOTHERSHIP!

#. In play, when the mice reach the moon: 2x letters centred on row 13 (small letters if it is longer).
#. max 18
LANDED = THEY LANDED!

#. In play, small letters centred on row 18 for 2 s: a player earned an extra ship. With 2-4 players the player's
#. tag is put before it (P2 EXTRA SHIP!).
#. max 30
EXTRA_LIFE = EXTRA SHIP!

#. The same, when a hamster catches a power-up from the bonus saucer: two seeds at once for 10 s ...
#. max 30
POWER_DOUBLE = DOUBLE SEEDS!

#. ... seeds three at a time, faster, for 10 s ...
#. max 30
POWER_RAPID = RAPID SEEDS!

#. ... a bubble around the ship that takes one hit.
#. max 30
POWER_SHIELD = BUBBLE SHIELD!
