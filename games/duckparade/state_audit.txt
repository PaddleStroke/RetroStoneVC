# Save states (tools/state_audit.py): the mutable statics that are NOT saved, and why.
# Everything else is registered in the game's state functions (main.c game_state(), draw.c draw_state(),
# sfx.c sfx_state(), bot.c bot_state(), the house kit's hu_state() and ha_state()).
sfx.snd_buf         sfx_init(): the synthesis buffer (the samples are made at start-up, not saved)
main.SV             the battery save's copy (best scores, medals): stays in the .srm, a state never takes it back
bot.cls             the bot's tile class table: a cache, made again the same when needed
bot.cls_ready       ... its flag (a fresh process builds it again)
lanes.land_delay    set and reset within one bot decision: always 0 between two frames
sfx.half_buf        sfx_init(): the 16-kHz scratch of the synthesis (start-up only)
lanes.S             the judge's scratch (judge_run): cleared at the start of every search
lanes.W             ... the positions carried to the next frame
lanes.F             ... the positions that have just landed
lanes.arr           ... the hops landing in the next frames
lanes.vf            ... the free masks of the next frames
