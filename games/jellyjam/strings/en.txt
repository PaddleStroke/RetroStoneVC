# Jelly Jam: the game's own strings (docs/i18n.md). The kit's words (PRESS A TO %s, GAME OVER, PAUSED, GO!, P%d WINS!...)
# are in games/common/strings. The game's name and the chefs' names (PIP, DOT, HOPSY, MOCHI...) are never translated.

#. The title prompt's verb: PRESS A TO <VERB> (the kit's PRESS_TO). Cooking: the jellies are made in a kitchen. max 12
VERB = COOK
#. The first duel's banner shows the controls in two lines under it. { } and ~ are drawn as arrows (left, right, down).
#. max 36
HINT_MOVE = { }: MOVE - ~: DROP
#. A and B are the pad's buttons (keep the letters): they turn the falling pair of jellies. max 36
HINT_TURN = A B: TURN THE PAIR
#. A duel's level (1 player: each computer chef is a level): the banner before it, and on the board between the fields.
#. max 10
LEVEL_N = LEVEL %d
#. Under the level's banner: the computer chef you play against; %s is its name (PIP, BRUNO...). max 18
VS_NAME = VS %s
#. 2-4 players: the banner before each round (best of 3). max 16
ROUND_N = ROUND %d
#. Over the jellies melting in a chain (each melt makes the ones above fall and melt again): "3 CHAIN!". In a field 9
#. columns wide: max 9
CHAIN_N = %d CHAIN!
#. The banner when you beat a computer chef (then the next one comes). max 16
YOU_WIN = YOU WIN!
