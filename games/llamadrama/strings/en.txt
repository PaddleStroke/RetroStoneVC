# Llama Drama (games/llamadrama): its strings, the English source (docs/i18n.md).
# Everything else comes from the house kit (games/common/strings/en.txt). The game's name is never translated.

#. The title's prompt verb: PRESS A TO <VERB> (the kit's PRESS_TO). The llama spits at the hornets.
#. max 14
VERB = SPIT

#. In play, for the first 5 s of a run: small letters centred on row 27 (at the bottom). ^ and ~ are drawn as the
#. up and down arrows (the d-pad): they turn the llama's head; A is the pad's A button.
#. max 34
HINT_PAD = ^~ AIM   A: SPIT

#. The same hint on a touch screen: a finger on the sky turns the llama's head there and it spits there.
#. max 34
HINT_TOUCH = TAP THE SKY TO SPIT

#. In play, 2x letters (2 columns each) centred on row 5 for 2.5 s, the first time each new kind of hornet comes:
#. a fast hornet that stops, buzzes, then dashes at a llama ...
#. max 19
NEW_DART = FAST DART!

#. ... a big hornet that takes three hits ...
#. max 19
NEW_BIG = BIG HORNET!

#. ... a little swarm of hornets flying together.
#. max 19
NEW_SWARM = SWARM!

#. On the game-over panel, under the medal: how many hornets the llama popped (bonked down with stars, nothing hurt).
#. max 18
POPPED_N[one] = %d HORNET POPPED
POPPED_N[other] = %d HORNETS POPPED
