# Gecko Golf: the game's own strings (docs/i18n.md). The kit's words (PRESS A TO %s, GAME OVER, PAUSED...) are in
# games/common/strings. The game's name is never translated.

#. The title prompt's verb: PRESS A TO <VERB> (the kit's PRESS_TO): a golf swing. max 12
VERB = SWING
#. A hint at the bottom of the screen on the first hole (1 player), until the first shot: the d-pad's up and down
#. arrows (^ ~ are drawn as arrows) aim, A held charges the power. max 38
HINT = ^~: AIM - HOLD A, LET GO: SHOOT
#. The same hint on a touch screen: pull back from the ball like a slingshot, let go to shoot. max 38
HINT_TOUCH = PULL BACK, LET GO TO SHOOT
#. Big letters (2x: max 19 characters, else small) when the ball drops in the cup on the first stroke.
HOLE_IN_ONE = HOLE IN ONE!
#. Big letters (max 19) when the ball drops in on the second stroke (it gives a stroke back).
IN_TWO = IN TWO!
#. Big letters (max 19) when the ball drops in after three strokes or more.
IN_THE_HOLE = IN THE HOLE!
#. Big letters (max 19) when the ball falls in the water (a penalty stroke, the ball comes back).
SPLASH = SPLASH!
#. Big letters (max 19) when the ball leaves the course's sides (a penalty stroke, the ball comes back).
OUT_OF_BOUNDS = OUT OF BOUNDS!
#. Big letters (max 19) when a player has no strokes left: that player's game is over.
NO_STROKES = NO STROKES LEFT
#. Under a hole in one or a hole in two: the strokes given back to the player's budget. max 30
STROKES_BACK[one] = +%d STROKE
STROKES_BACK[other] = +%d STROKES
#. Under SPLASH! and OUT OF BOUNDS!: one more stroke is taken from the budget. max 30
PENALTY = PENALTY STROKE
#. At the top of the screen with 2-4 players: the hole being played. max 12
HOLE_N = HOLE %d
#. A line at the foot of the game-over panel: how many holes in one the run had. max 18
ACES_N = HOLES IN ONE: %d
#. The courses' names, in big letters when the course changes: a green meadow, ... max 19
MEADOW = MEADOW
#. ... sand dunes (a desert), ... (big letters, max 19)
DUNES = DUNES
#. ... snowy hills, ... (max 19)
SNOW = SNOWY HILLS
#. ... a golf course at night, ... (max 19)
NIGHT = NIGHT COURSE
#. ... a land of sweets. (max 19)
CANDY = CANDY LAND
