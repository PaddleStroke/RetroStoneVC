# Prickle Patch (games/pricklepatch): its strings, the English source (docs/i18n.md).
# A little gardener (a hedgehog, a tortoise or a hen) walks around a vegetable patch while its garden tools work by
# themselves against endless garden pests (slugs, beetles, ants, wasps, crows...). Pests drop gems; each level offers
# three new tools or upgrades. Survive 15 minutes and beat the Crow King to save the garden. Coins buy small permanent
# boosts in the garden shed. Everything else comes from the house kit (games/common/strings/en.txt). The game's name is
# never translated. Keep it gentle and family-friendly: the pests are "chased off" or "popped", never killed.
