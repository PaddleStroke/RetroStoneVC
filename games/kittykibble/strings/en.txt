# Kitty Kibble (games/kittykibble): its strings, the English source (docs/i18n.md).
# Everything else comes from the house kit (games/common/strings/en.txt; the title says PRESS A TO PLAY). The game's
# name is never translated.

#. In play, as each room begins (2 s): small letters on a panel over the maze (row 12), above the room's name;
#. %d is the room's number (1, 2, 3...). Also the last line of the game-over panel (the room reached).
#. max 30
ROOM_N = ROOM %d

#. In play, as each room begins: the room's name in 2x gold letters (2 columns each) on the panel (row 14; small
#. letters if it is longer than 17). The kitten eats the kibble of five rooms of a house, in turn ...
#. max 17
ROOM_KITCHEN = KITCHEN

#. ... the living room ...
#. max 17
ROOM_LIVING = LIVING ROOM

#. ... the bedroom ...
#. max 17
ROOM_BEDROOM = BEDROOM

#. ... the garden ...
#. max 17
ROOM_GARDEN = GARDEN

#. ... and the attic.
#. max 17
ROOM_ATTIC = ATTIC

#. In play, when the last kibble of a room is eaten (2 s, before the next room): 2x gold letters on a panel (row 14).
#. max 17
CLEAR = ROOM CLEAR!

#. In play with 2-4 players, small letters centred on row 3 (row 1 with 3-4 players) for 2 s, when a robot vacuum
#. catches a kitten (that player is out); %s is the player's tag (P2).
#. max 30
CAUGHT = %s CAUGHT!
