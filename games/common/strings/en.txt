# The 8BCraft house UI kit's strings (games/common): the English source. docs/i18n.md explains the format;
# tools/i18n_build.py compiles them, with each game's own strings, into the game.
#
#   KEY = TEXT              the text runs to the end of the line; "..." keeps spaces at its ends
#   KEY[one] = TEXT         a plural: the forms one, few, many, other (the language's rule picks one)
#   #. a note               for translators, about the next key: where it shows; "max N" = its room in columns
#                           (8-px columns; a Japanese, Chinese or Korean character takes 2; default: the English)
# Keep %s and %d (and their order, or number them: %2$s %1$d). A, B, START and SELECT are the pad's buttons:
# keep their letters. Capitals only matter in English: the font has no lowercase (the tool writes capitals).

#. The title prompt; %s is the game's verb (PLAY, SWIM, HOP...). The A button is drawn left of the line.
#. max 34
PRESS_TO = PRESS A TO %s
#. The default verb of the title prompt.
#. max 10
PLAY = PLAY
#. The title's join line; %s lists the free player tags ("P2 / P3 / P4").
#. max 38
JOIN = %s: PRESS A TO JOIN
#. The same with players already joined: B makes a player leave.
#. max 38
JOIN_LEAVE = %s: PRESS A TO JOIN - B: LEAVE
#. The join line when every slot is taken (2 to 4 players).
#. max 38
FULL[one] = %d PLAYER - B: LEAVE
FULL[other] = %d PLAYERS - B: LEAVE
#. A player's tag (the title's slots, the score corners, the ranking): P1..P4. Two columns.
#. max 2
PLAYER_TAG = P%d

#. max 20
